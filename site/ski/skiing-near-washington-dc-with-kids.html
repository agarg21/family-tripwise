Which one fits your kids

  • Kids 5 and under: Bryce Resort or Wisp Resort. Free with a ticketed adult at both (Wisp: one child per adult, plus a $5 card).
  • First lessons, ages 4½ to 7: Bryce Resort. Kinder private lessons include a 90-minute lesson, rentals and a full-day ticket.
  • Closest to DC: Whitetail Resort. About 76 miles in a straight line; ski school groups kids under 7 by ability.
  • Cheapest early-season day: Bryce Resort. Weekdays through Dec 10, if it opens before Dec 11: $42 adults, $38 juniors online.
  • Night skiing on a school night: Wisp Resort. Wednesday nights from $19 adults and $14 kids, online.

Hill by hill

Whitetail Resort

Mercersburg, PA · about 76 miles from the White House in a straight line

Day price not readfor 2026–27, as of our read

  • Kids' group lessons for ages 7–12; children under 7 go in smaller groups by ability
  • All children in lessons must be potty trained
  • Lessons don't include lift tickets or rentals; group lessons can add them at a discount
  • Pass holders save 20% on group lessons through Epic Mountain Rewards

Prices: We didn't read a 2026–27 day ticket price; its lessons page points pass holders to Epic Mountain Rewards.

Getting there and sources

Getting there: Drive; it's the closest of the four.

Bryce Resort

Basye, VA · about 93 miles from the White House in a straight line

$291Saturday lift tickets for 4 online, estimate; rentals extra, read Nov 24

  • Kids 5 and under ski free with a ticketed adult (rentals extra)
  • Kinder private lessons: skiers 4½–7, snowboarders 6–7, with rentals and a full-day ticket
  • First Time Package for beginners 8 and up; private lessons from age 3
  • Book kinder and first-time lessons online at least 24 hours ahead

Prices on its page, read Nov 24:

  • Weekend or holiday day, junior 12 and under: $87 / $102
  • Weekday day, adult / junior (online): $60 / $55
  • Night from 4:30 p.m., adult / junior (online): $40 / $30
  • Early season weekday, through Dec 10, adult / junior (online): $42 / $38
  • Full-day rental with helmet (online): $70

Prices: 2026–27 rates; regular season from Dec 11. Online rates are $15 under the window. Holidays: Dec 21–Jan 3, Jan 16–18, Feb 13–15. Opening day not set yet.
