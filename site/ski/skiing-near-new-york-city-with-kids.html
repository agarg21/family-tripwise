Which one fits your kids

  • Under 6, first time on snow: Big SNOW American Dream. Little SNOW Day: 2 hours with rentals for $24.99 on non-holiday weekdays.
  • Under 6, outdoors: Mountain Creek. 6 and under ski free with an adult Access Ticket.
  • No car: Big SNOW American Dream. The only hill here with a direct bus from Manhattan.
  • Cheapest Saturday we could price: Mount Peter. $348 in lift tickets for the four of you, the only 2026–27 weekend prices posted so far.
  • 3rd, 4th or 5th grader: Belleayre. Takes the SKI NY Kids Passport any day, holidays included.

Hill by hill

Big SNOW American Dream

East Rutherford, NJ · indoor

From $24.99+ tax per person, 2 hours with rentals, non-holiday weekdays; weekend prices differ, read Nov 10

  • 6 and under: Little SNOW Day, 2 hours with rentals
  • Ages 5–12: Camp BIG, a 3-hour beginner program with rentals, helmet and jacket and pants
  • Snow play and tubing from age 3; 36 inches with an adult, 42 inches alone

Prices on its page, read Nov 10:

  • SNOW Day, ages 7+: 2 hours, rentals, jacket and pants, helmet: $99.99 + tax
  • Little SNOW Day, 6 and under: $24.99 + tax
  • Camp BIG, ages 5–12: $149.99 + tax
  • Snow play and tubing, 1.5 hours: $29.99 + tax

Prices: The page names no season, and says Saturday, Sunday and holiday prices differ from these but doesn't show them. Gloves are $12 extra.

Getting there and sources

Getting there: NJ Transit bus 355 from Port Authority Gate 305, under 30 minutes in normal traffic. Weekends and holidays hourly 8 a.m.–9 p.m.; weekdays only 10 a.m. and 12:30 p.m.

Mount Peter

Warwick, NY · about an hour from NYC, the hill says

$348Saturday lift tickets, family of 4, estimate; rentals extra, read Nov 10

  • Free beginner ski and snowboard lessons every day, the hill says (no details on what they include)
  • 4 and under (Tiny Tots, one per adult): free midweek with an adult ticket; weekends priced below
  • Snow tubing; sessions on its tubing calendar

Prices on its page, read Nov 10:

  • Weekend all day, adult: $89
  • Weekend all day, junior 5–11: $85
  • Weekend Tiny Tot, 4 and under: $45
  • Midweek all day, adult or junior: $75
  • Saturday twilight (3 p.m. to close): $65

Prices: 2026–27 rates. Rentals and helmets aren't included; Tiny Tot tickets are sold in person only.

Getting there and sources

Getting there: Drive; no public transit option on its page.

Mountain Creek

Vernon, NJ

$139.99+ tax, Go Package per person, ages 7+: ticket and rental; no season named, read Nov 10

  • 6 and under ski free with an adult Access Ticket (tickets are limited)
  • Kids Camp, ages 4–12: 2-hour lesson, rental and a full-day ticket (price not on its page)
  • Vern's Outdoor Park, 6 and under, 30-minute sessions on weekends (needs a day ticket)

Prices on its page, read Nov 10:

  • Go Package, ages 7+: full-day ticket, rental, self-guided learning area: $139.99 + tax
  • Go Package after 3 p.m.: $99.99

Prices: Lift tickets are sold online only and weren't showing prices on Nov 10. Closer parking lots are $20 and $50. The Go Package page names no season.

Getting there and sources

Getting there: Drive; the general lot is free, closer lots cost extra (online only).

Free or cheap passes for 3rd to 5th graders

SKI NY Kids Passport

3rd, 4th and 5th graders from any state

  • Free lift tickets with a paying adult, up to 2 uses at each participating hill, Dec 1, 2026 to May 1, 2027. No rentals or lessons.
  • Cost: $45 processing fee (its page says per application; its FAQ says per child), no refunds.
  • How: Apply at app.junipass.com with a photo and proof of grade; the passport is emailed by Thanksgiving week.
  • Where: Near NYC: Belleayre any day (the parent's ticket bought on its website, or a season pass); Thunder Ridge and Plattekill not on holidays; Windham, Big SNOW and Mountain Creek non-holiday weekdays only, the last two arranged ahead. Hunter doesn't take it.

SKI NY Kids Passport (read Nov 10)

PA Outdoor Adventure Pass

4th and 5th graders from any state

  • A lift ticket or lesson deal at each participating Pennsylvania hill, set by the hill (not free tickets), plus outdoor deals all year, valid through August 2027.
  • Cost: $69 per child.
  • How: Buy online with photos of your child and you and proof of grade; grade checks take 1–3 business days.
  • Where: Near NYC: Camelback, Blue Mountain and Shawnee Mountain; each sets its own offer and blackout dates.

PA Outdoor Adventure Pass (read Nov 10)
