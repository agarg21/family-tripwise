Which one fits your kids

  • Shortest drive: Lee Canyon. About 31 miles from the Strip in a straight line; under an hour, it says.
  • First lessons, ages 3 to 6: Brian Head Resort. Private lessons made for 3- to 6-year-olds; groups start at 5.
  • Kids 7 to 12, multi-week: Lee Canyon. Multi-week Freeriders and Mountaineers programs.
  • Weekend away: Brian Head Resort. A Utah resort about 169 miles away in a straight line; plan a weekend.
  • Cheapest day: Lee Canyon or Brian Head Resort. Kids free at both; adult tickets start at $9 on many days, so compare dates.

Hill by hill

Lee Canyon

Mount Charleston, NV · about 31 miles in a straight line

$0kids 12 and under with the free Power Kids pass, read Nov 25

  • Kids 12 and under ride free with a free Power Kids pass
  • Kids and youth programs for ages 4–17
  • Freeriders and Mountaineers multi-week programs for ages 7–12 (cost not posted yet)
  • Snow Kids day program (details on its page)

Prices on its page, read Nov 25:

  • Adult day ticket, online, starting at: $9
  • Kids 12 and under (Power Kids pass): $0

Prices: 2026/27 lift tickets are on sale online only. Its page says "Starting as low as $9 + a $10 Lee Cash" and that prices are variable and go up with demand.

Getting there and sources

Getting there: Drive; it says it's less than an hour from the Strip. Pick up cards at its pick-up boxes.

Brian Head Resort

Brian Head, UT · about 169 miles in a straight line

$0kids 12 and under with the free Power Kids pass, read Nov 25

  • Kids 12 and under always ski free with a free Power Kids pass, or a free kids' day pass from the window
  • Private lessons for ages 3–6; Kids Camp group lessons for ages 5–12
  • Lessons need a lift ticket; rentals aren't included
  • Kids Camp costs 5% more booked under 5 days ahead, 15% more under 24 hours

Prices on its page, read Nov 25:

  • Adult day ticket, online, starting at (many days): $9
  • Kids 12 and under (Power Kids pass): $0

Prices: Its 26-27 lift tickets are on sale, with many days starting at $9, it says. It also says it's open for the season.

Getting there and sources

Getting there: Drive; it's in southern Utah, so plan a weekend. Lifts run 9:30 a.m. to 4 p.m.
