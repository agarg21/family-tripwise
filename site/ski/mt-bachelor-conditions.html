Plan the day with kids

  • Season and hours: Opening day is scheduled for Fri, Dec 4, 2026, conditions permitting. Winter lift hours are "coming soon"; a full-day ticket is listed as 9am to 4pm. No closing date posted.
  • Where to base: Sunrise has the beginner and family zones; Mt Bachelor's first-timer guide says to park there for group lessons. But Mountain Rippers (ages 4-6), the Childcare Center and morning private lessons are at West Village Lodge.
  • Childcare, 6 weeks to 10 years: State-licensed Childcare Center, lower level of West Village Lodge, 8am to 5pm (half days 8am-12pm or 1-5pm), daily from Dec 4. Book 48-72 hours ahead, 3-4 weeks for holidays. No session rates posted.
  • Snow Explorer: Childcare kids can go out to an On-Snow Fun Zone. Bring their snow gear and goggles; Mt Bachelor doesn't rent it.
  • Lessons, ages 4 to 6: Mountain Rippers, up to 3 kids per instructor, daily Dec 19 to Apr 4. Full day 9:30am-3:30pm with rentals, helmet, lift ticket and supervised lunch. Must be potty trained.
  • Where kids meet: Mountain Rippers Room, lower level of West Village Lodge. Check-in 8:30-9:15am on weekdays, 9am on weekends and holidays; late arrivals may be turned away.
  • Kids' tickets: Ages 5 and under ski free but need a child ticket from a ticket window. Youth is 6-12, teen 13-18. Kids Ski Free (6-12) skis free for the same days as a parent's adult (19-64) 3-of-5, 4-of-6 or 5-of-8 day ticket bought online in advance.
  • Parking: General parking in the West Village, Sunrise and Skyliner lots is free, first come, first served. Lots can fill by mid-morning on busy days; then use another base and the Interlodge Shuttle. All cars out by 5pm.

Getting there from Bend

  • Car: Mt Bachelor says Bend and Sunriver are both about 30 minutes away; leave early, as road conditions can vary. Its pages we read give no chain or traction rule.
  • Bus: the Cascade East Transit (CET) shuttle runs from Bend daily through closing weekend (fares and stops on CET's schedule, not on Mt Bachelor's pages).
  • Childcare drop-off: follow Century Drive to its end, about half a mile past the Sunrise exit, into the West Village lot.
  • Tickets: pick-up boxes for online tickets in Bend and outside each base lodge.

If the snow is thin

  • Lessons and childcare: no cash refunds. Cancel by 5pm PT the day before a lesson (before the start date for childcare) for a credit worth the full cost; no-shows lose it.
  • Lift tickets: the ticket pages we read post no weather or refund policy; the hours page says opening day is conditions permitting.
  • Off the slopes: a 2-hour snow tubing session on a 375-foot hill, sled dog rides and free Forest Service snowshoe tours. No hours, ages or prices posted except the free tour.
