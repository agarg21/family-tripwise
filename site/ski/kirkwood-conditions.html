Kirkwood, south of Lake Tahoe, California · Ski conditions

Plan the day with kids

  • Season and hours: Scheduled to open Fri, Dec 4, 2026. Ticket office hours are "coming soon" and no closing date is posted.
  • Lift hours: Kirkwood's planning page says lifts close at 4pm daily; it gives no opening time (that page still names a 2023 opening date, so confirm).
  • Childcare: None. Kirkwood's planning page says childcare is not offered (the same page still names a 2023 opening date, so confirm before you go).
  • Lessons: From age 3 (Kirkwood's planning page). Kids' groups are listed for ages 3-4, 5-6 and 7-14; under 7 go in smaller groups by ability. Potty trained; helmet required. Lift tickets and rentals not included.
  • Lesson morning: Kids' groups meet at the Pre-Registration Tent, top of the Timber Creek stairs on the left. Arrive 60 minutes early if renting, 30 if not. Ages 5-14 full day 10:30am to 3:45pm; lunch included if offered.
  • Beginner area: Timber Creek is the beginner hub, with a learning area by Timber Creek Lodge (rentals, food, ski school). Kirkwood says restroom access there is limited by construction.
  • Lift tickets: Kirkwood's home page says lift tickets will be limited and to buy ahead. We found no ticket page we could read, so we show no ticket prices.
  • Tubing and more: Tubing, snowshoeing, cross-country skiing and fat biking. Kids over 5 can tube on their own; younger kids ride with an adult. No hours or prices on the guide (posted Feb 2024).
  • Parking: The parking page says parking is free outside the winter season and there's no overnight parking; it posts no winter prices or rules. Check before you drive.

Lift tickets and lessons: Kirkwood's pages we could read post no lift ticket, lesson or parking prices, so we can't sum a family-of-4 day; check live. See Kirkwood home page.

Getting there from Reno and the Bay Area

  • Car: Kirkwood says Reno-Tahoe International Airport is 79.9 miles away. The resort sits between two mountain passes; check Caltrans for road conditions before you go.
  • Kirkwood warns that Mormon Emigrant Trail is closed in winter, though GPS may still suggest it.
  • Buses: Bay Area Ski Bus and NAC Ski run from the Bay Area and Sacramento; Discover Lake Tahoe offers private shuttles from Tahoe. No fares on Kirkwood's page.

If the snow is thin

  • Lessons aren't canceled for weather while the resort runs, and there's no refund for weather, wind or snow levels. Cancel more than 48 hours ahead for a full refund; inside 48 hours a one-day lesson costs a 50% fee.
  • Off the slopes: tubing, snowshoeing and cross-country skiing (from a Feb 2024 Kirkwood guide; no hours or prices posted).
  • We didn't find a weather refund policy for lift tickets on the pages we read; check before you buy.
