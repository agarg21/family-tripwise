Plan the day with kids

  • Season and hours: Heavenly plans to open for skiing Fri, Nov 20, 2026, on the California side, lifts 9am to 4pm. No closing date posted. Until then the gondola runs 9am to 4pm for sightseeing only.
  • Childcare: At California Main Lodge, ages 6 weeks to 6 years, 8:30am to 4pm. Heavenly says it books up fast: reserve online, or call 530-542-6912 if a day isn't bookable online.
  • Ski & Play, ages 3 to 4: A full day of childcare with a ski lesson, at the same center. Your child must be fully potty trained for the lesson.
  • Lessons: Kids' groups are listed for ages 3-4, 5-6 and 7-14; under 7 go in smaller groups by ability. Potty trained; helmet required. Lift tickets and rentals not included.
  • Lesson morning: Check in at the Boulder ski school building, California Lodge or the gondola kids' tent (your email says which). Arrive 30 minutes early (15 for ages 3-4), 60 if renting. Full day 10am to 3:45pm with lunch.
  • Lift tickets: Heavenly's home page says lift tickets will be limited and suggests buying ahead. Its ticket page blocks our reader, so we show no ticket prices.
  • Tubing and more: A tubing hill and a mountain coaster near the top of the gondola, and an ice rink in Heavenly Village. Heavenly's family guide (posted Dec 5, 2025) gives no ages, hours or prices.
  • Parking: Free every day at Boulder and Stagecoach lodges (Stagecoach is small: come early). California Lodge charges cars with 3 or fewer people before 11:30am on weekends, holidays and peak days.

Childcare and parking prices on Heavenly's pages, read Nov 17:

  • Childcare, infant or toddler (6 weeks to 30 months), full day: $260
  • Childcare, preschool (2.5 to 6 years), full day: $235
  • Ski & Play, ages 3 to 4, full day: $300, or $353 with rentals
  • California Lodge parking, weekends, holidays and peak days before 11:30am (3 or fewer people): $20

Getting there from Reno

  • Car: Heavenly says the drive from Reno's airport takes roughly 1 hour 5 minutes, depending on traffic and weather. It suggests 4-wheel drive and snow tires (or chains), and points to the California and Nevada transportation departments for road reports.
  • Free shuttle: California Lodge to Heavenly Village, where the gondola starts, and back.
  • Kingsbury shuttle: Stateline Transit Center to Boulder and Stagecoach lodges; the timetable runs from 6:40am, with the last return from Boulder at 5:35pm. No fare listed.
  • Heavenly Village garage belongs to the city of South Lake Tahoe (rates on the city's site); nearby casinos sell daily parking (no price listed).

If the snow is thin

  • Lessons aren't canceled for weather while the resort runs, and there's no refund for weather, wind or snow levels. Cancel more than 48 hours ahead for a full refund; inside 48 hours a one-day lesson costs a 50% fee.
  • Off the slopes: the gondola ride, ice skating in Heavenly Village, and the mountain coaster near the top of the gondola.
  • We didn't find a weather refund policy for lift tickets on the pages we read; check before you buy.
