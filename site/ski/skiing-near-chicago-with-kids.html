Which one fits your kids

  • Kids 6 and under: Chestnut Mountain Resort. Their lift ticket is free; the 7-and-up weekend rate is $87.
  • First lessons, ages 3 to 6: The Mountain Top at Grand Geneva. Snow Cubs on Saturday and Sunday mornings (and holiday weekdays), or a private lesson any day.
  • After school: The Mountain Top at Grand Geneva. Noon to 8 p.m., Monday to Thursday outside holiday dates: $70 adults, $45 kids.
  • Epic Pass families: Wilmot Mountain. Pass holders save 20% on group lessons through Epic Mountain Rewards.
  • Bigger hill, weekend away: Chestnut Mountain Resort. A 475-foot drop and 19 trails near historic Galena; the farthest drive of the three.

Hill by hill

The Mountain Top at Grand Geneva

Lake Geneva, WI · about 65 miles from the Loop in a straight line

$306Saturday lift tickets for 4 with tax, estimate; rentals extra, read Nov 24

  • Snow Cubs lessons for ages 3–6 on Saturday and Sunday mornings all season, and weekdays during holidays
  • Private lessons for ages 3–6 any day; Mountain Mites lessons for ages 7–14
  • Children six and under: a cheaper lift ticket, or a ticket and rentals together
  • Opening day is planned for Saturday, Dec 12, weather permitting

Prices on its page, read Nov 24:

  • Fri, Sat, Sun or holiday, all day, adult: $100
  • Fri, Sat, Sun or holiday, all day, child: $75
  • Fri, Sat or holiday, 3–9 p.m., adult / child: $75 / $55
  • Mon–Thu, noon–8 p.m. (not holiday dates), adult / child: $70 / $45
  • Six and under, lift ticket: $15
  • Six and under, lift ticket and rentals: $60

Prices: 2026–27 rates, plus 5.5% sales tax. No child age range is given, so we priced the 8-year-old as a child. Holidays: Dec 18–Jan 3, Jan 15–18, Feb 12–15.

Getting there and sources

Getting there: Drive; it's part of the Grand Geneva resort, so you can stay on site.

Chestnut Mountain Resort

Galena, IL · about 145 miles from the Loop in a straight line

$261Saturday window tickets for 4 (the 5-year-old free), estimate; tax not stated; rentals extra, read Nov 24

  • Kids 6 and under get a free lift ticket
  • Powder Pups lessons for ages 4–10; private lessons from age 4
  • Burton Learn to Ride snowboarding from age 7

Prices on its page, read Nov 24:

  • Fri, Sat, Sun or holiday, lift only, ages 7+: $87
  • Fri, Sat, Sun or holiday, lift and rental: $142
  • Mon–Thu (not holidays), lift only: $55
  • Mon–Thu, lift, rental and group lesson: $155
  • Sundays from 1 p.m. (Jan 3–Feb 21, some dates excluded), lift only: $53

Prices: 2026–27 window prices from its rates page, which doesn't say whether tax is included. Holidays: Dec 25, 2026–Jan 3, 2027, Jan 18 and Feb 15, 2027.

Getting there and sources

Getting there: Drive; it's the farthest of the three, near historic Galena.

Chestnut Mountain rates · Chestnut Mountain Powder Pups · Chestnut Mountain lessons · Chestnut Mountain winter activities (read Nov 24)

Wilmot Mountain

Wilmot, WI · about 52 miles from the Loop in a straight line

Epic Pass hill2026–27 day prices not read

  • Kids in lessons must be potty trained; groups are set by age and ability
  • Lessons don't include lift tickets or rentals; group lessons can add them at a discount
  • Pass holders save 20% on group lessons through Epic Mountain Rewards (sign in before checkout)

Prices: Its site says lift tickets are always cheaper bought online in advance; we didn't read a 2026–27 day price this time.

Getting there and sources

Getting there: Drive; it sits just over the Illinois–Wisconsin line.
