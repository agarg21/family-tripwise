Which one fits your kids

  • Toddler under 36 inches: Mount Peter. Its Little Tikes hill is for kids under 36 inches, reached by a carpet lift.
  • Preschooler riding with you: Camelback Resort or Mountain Creek or Mount Peter. Double tubes from 33 inches at Camelback and 36 inches at Mountain Creek and Mount Peter.
  • Kids 48 inches and up, short drive: Campgaw Mountain. About 24 miles from Midtown in a straight line; $39 a weekend session.
  • Biggest park: Blue Mountain Resort. It calls itself the USA's largest snow tubing park, with more than 55 lanes.
  • Cheapest weekend for kids 5 and 8: Mount Peter or Campgaw Mountain. Mount Peter, $196 for 4. Campgaw is $156 only if both kids are 48 inches or taller; measure first.

Our picks from the parks' published rules, not from a visit. Opening days depend on snow; check each park's page before you go. Skiing instead: skiing near New York City with kids.

Park by park

Mount Peter

Warwick, NY · about an hour's drive, the park says

$196weekend session for 4, estimate, read Nov 24

  • 42 inches to ride alone; 36 to 41 inches in a double tube with a parent or guardian
  • Under 36 inches: the Little Tikes hill, with an attendant at the top; kids under 8 need an adult with them there
  • Every tuber needs a ticket, and so does any adult who goes into the Little Tikes area
  • 90-minute sessions; book online, sessions can sell out

Prices on its page, read Nov 24:

  • Weekday session, per person: $40
  • Weekend or holiday session, per person: $49
  • Little Tikes, weekday: $40
  • Little Tikes, weekend or holiday: $49

Prices: 2026–27 rates on its tubing page; all tickets include the snow play area.

Getting there and sources

Getting there: Drive; the park says it is about an hour from New York City. Arrive 30 minutes before your session to park, buy tickets and sign waivers.

Mount Peter tubing (read Nov 24)

Campgaw Mountain

Mahwah, NJ · about 24 miles from Midtown in a straight line

$156weekend session for 4 if everyone is 48 inches, estimate, read Nov 24

  • 48 inches or taller (hat off) to ride, no exceptions; no double tubes
  • Each rider carries their own tube; a parent can't haul a child's tube too
  • 2-hour sessions; tickets go on sale 14 days ahead; a signed waiver is needed for every rider

Prices on its page, read Nov 24:

  • Monday to Thursday, 2 hours: $33
  • Friday, 2 hours: $35
  • Weekend or holiday, 2 hours: $39

Prices: Rates on its tubing page, which names no season. Changes less than 48 hours ahead may cost $10 a ticket, and a session can be rescheduled only once; no-shows pay in full.

Getting there and sources

Getting there: Drive; its tubing page gives no transit option.

Campgaw tubing (read Nov 24)

Mountain Creek

Vernon, NJ · about 40 miles from Midtown in a straight line

Prices not postedfor 2026–27, as of our read

  • 42 inches to ride alone; 36 to 42 inches in a double tube or linked to a responsible adult
  • 2-hour sessions starting when your code is scanned
  • Tickets online only, in advance; no sales at the hill

Prices: 2026–27 prices aren't posted yet; its ticket table still shows last season as closed when we read it, and an FAQ price names no season, so we don't use it. Its rules also say one guest per tube, which conflicts with its double-tube rule; ask when you book. Tickets can't be refunded.

Getting there and sources

Getting there: Drive; park in North General Parking or the Sandhill Lot; the tubing entrance is in the General Parking Lot near Cobblestone Village.

Mountain Creek snowtubing (read Nov 24)

Camelback Resort

Tannersville, PA · about 75 miles from Midtown in a straight line

Priced by datesee the booking page

  • 44 inches to ride alone; 33 to 43 inches in a double tube or chain with an adult 18 or older; under 33 inches can't ride
  • Riders under 14 need a participating adult with them
  • 2-hour sessions starting on the hour; each rider needs their own ticket on their own RFID card

Prices: No prices on its page; they show only when you pick a date and time, and it says prices can change with demand.

Getting there and sources

Getting there: Drive to the Poconos; arrive at least 30 minutes before your session.

Camelback snow tubing tickets (read Nov 24)

Blue Mountain Resort

Palmerton, PA · about 80 miles from Midtown in a straight line

Priced by date2026–27 tickets on sale

  • 36 inches or taller to ride; no age or weight limits; single or double tubes
  • Everyone, kids included, needs their own named ticket; a parent signs the waiver for under-18s
  • 2-hour sessions; after 5 p.m. the park adds night lights and music

Prices: 2026–27 tickets are on sale, priced by date and time on its booking page; the page lists no dollar amounts.

Getting there and sources

Getting there: Drive; tubing is based at the Adventure Center in the resort's Valley area.
