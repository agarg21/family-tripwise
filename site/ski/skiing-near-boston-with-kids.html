Blue Hills, Nashoba Valley, Wachusett and Pats Peak in southern New Hampshire: kids' lesson ages, free-kid rules and grade-school passes, from each hill's own page.

Which one fits your kids

  • 5 and under, on a weekday: Pats Peak. 5 and under ski free Monday to Thursday outside holidays.
  • First time, ages 2 to 4: Blue Hills Ski Area. Tiny Tracks: a 30-minute private lesson with lift pass and ski rental included (price not shown).
  • Cheapest Saturday we could price: Blue Hills Ski Area. $216 in lift tickets for the four of you, morning session.
  • 4th or 5th grader: Pats Peak. Takes the Ski NH 4th & 5th Grade Passport; book ahead on weekends.
  • Night skiing: Wachusett Mountain or Blue Hills Ski Area. Wachusett until 9:30 p.m. from Dec 25; Blue Hills until 9 p.m. on weeknights.

Our picks from the hills' published details, not from a visit. We don't report snow conditions here; check each hill's snow report before you go. Bigger mountains a longer drive away, with conditions and webcams, in Vermont: Mount Snow, Stratton, Okemo, Killington.

Hill by hill

Blue Hills Ski Area

Canton, MA · about 10 miles in a straight line from Boston

$216Saturday morning lift tickets, family of 4, estimate; rentals extra, read Nov 24

  • Tiny Tracks, ages 2–4: a 30-minute private lesson with lift pass and ski rental included
  • Group lessons for ages 4–6 run on surface lifts only; ages 7 and up have their own groups
  • Every lesson also needs an RFID pass bought separately, the lessons page says (Tiny Tracks includes its lift pass)
  • Tubing returns for 2026–27, 90-minute sessions (prices coming soon)

Prices on its page, read Nov 24:

  • Weekend or holiday all day, all-mountain: $80
  • Weekend or holiday half day (8:30–12:30 or 12:30–4:30), all-mountain: $62
  • Weekend or holiday half day, 6 and under: $30
  • Weekend or holiday, beginner area: $45
  • Midweek all day, all-mountain: $52
  • Midweek all day, 6 and under: $30
  • Mon–Thu night, 6–9 p.m.: $33

Prices: The page names no season but lists 2026–27 holiday periods (Dec 26–Jan 1, Jan 16–18, Feb 13–19). Lesson prices aren't shown on its lessons page.

Free tickets for 4th and 5th graders

Ski NH 4th & 5th Grade Passport

4th and 5th graders from any state

  • One free single-day lift ticket or trail pass at each of 30+ New Hampshire member areas, valid any day except blackout dates; the rules don't say whether a paying adult is needed.
  • Cost: $59 per child; each sibling needs their own.
  • How: Apply in the WinterKids app with proof of grade (report card, school letter or portal screenshot); approval takes 2–3 business days.
  • Where: Near Boston: Pats Peak (reserve ahead on weekends). Blackouts: Dec 27, 2026–Jan 3, 2027, Jan 16–18, Feb 13–15 and Feb 20, 2027. One offer per area per child; can't be combined with other discounts.

Ski NH Passport rules (read Nov 24)
