Common questions

Chicago family hotel FAQ

What is the best family hotel in Chicago?

There is no single best hotel for every family. Embassy Suites and Homewood solve different suite needs, Sable puts Navy Pier at the door, Swissotel has a purpose-built Kids Suite, InterContinental and Hilton Chicago answer different pool and location jobs, Hotel Zachary fits a Wrigley trip, and Four Seasons or Langham serve different luxury priorities.

Which Chicago family hotels have an indoor pool?

Embassy Suites Magnificent Mile, Homewood Suites Chicago-Downtown, InterContinental Magnificent Mile, Hilton Chicago, Four Seasons, and The Langham list indoor pools. Pool hours, closures, depth, crowding, and child rules can change. InterContinental's notice, rechecked November 5, 2026, still shows an elapsed August 10-September 16 project schedule without a year. Its completion, changing facilities, current hours and pass availability remain unconfirmed. Check directly before relying on it as a pool day.

Do these Chicago hotel prices include taxes and fees?

Not necessarily. For a family of 4 (2 adults + kids 5 and 8), a normal weekend, Fri–Sun, Jan 22–24, 2027, showed about $215 a night (Homewood) to $520 a night (Swissotel) on Google Hotels for hotels priced both nights, seen November 5, 2026; Four Seasons showed $1,248 for Friday only and Langham no price. Google does not show the room type or which taxes and fees are included, so treat them as estimates, not prices for a family suite or Kids Suite. Parking, destination charges, food and a second room may be extra. Price your exact dates, ages, room and full stay total before deciding.
