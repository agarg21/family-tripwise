Short answer

Choose the hotel that removes the biggest family friction

Mandalay Bay and Four Seasons share the strongest water-resort setup at different price and atmosphere levels. Vdara and Marriott's Grand Chateau solve two different central-suite needs. Tahiti Village and Cancun Resort trade centrality for kitchens and water time. Use the other four when themed value, older-kid energy, a modern North Strip resort, or a Summerlin base matters more.

Common questions

Las Vegas family hotel FAQ

What is the best family hotel in Las Vegas?

There is no single best hotel for every family. Mandalay Bay fits a water-resort trip, Vdara and Marriott's Grand Chateau fit different suite needs, Tahiti Village and Cancun Resort fit off-Strip water time, and the other options answer distinct budget, older-kid, luxury, or nature-base needs.

Should a family stay on or off the Las Vegas Strip?

Stay on the Strip when the chosen hotel and nearby activities reduce transfers enough to outweigh casino, crowd, and walking friction. Stay off the Strip when a kitchen, parking, waterslides, a quieter reset pattern, or a Red Rock day matters more than immediate resort access.

Do these nightly prices include resort fees and taxes?

Not necessarily. The figures are Google Hotels listing prices per night for 2 adults and kids 5 and 8 on Tuesday January 19, February 2 and February 23, 2027, seen October 31, 2026. Google does not show the room type or which taxes and resort fees are included, so treat them as estimates. Parking and optional purchases can be extra. Compare the exact room, party, dates, fees and full stay total before booking.
