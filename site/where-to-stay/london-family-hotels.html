Before you book any of the three

  • Book the exact 6-person version (with the sofa bed) and enter every child's age. Confirm who sleeps where and any sofa charge.
  • Check the entrance, lift and inside stairs. We have not verified stroller, step-free or quiet access.
  • Get the full price with all charges. A refundable card hold or prepaid deposit is not an extra stay fee.

Not a family of 6?

  • These prices are for 6 people. For 4 or 5, search the same apartments with your own party and every child's age.
  • A child counted as an adult, a different sofa version or other dates can change the price.
  • Don't book for fewer guests than will stay, or compare against a two-adult rate.
