Coming in December? Vessel holiday lighting and Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade are over by then.

Which one fits your kids

  • Kids under 6: NYBG Holiday Train Show. Model trains in a conservatory, with more outdoors, and you can go by day.
  • Ages 4 and 8 together: Bronx Zoo Holiday Lights. With the 2-in-1 ticket, daytime animals from 1 p.m., then the lights, so each age gets something.
  • Keeping tickets at zero: Rockefeller Center tree, Bryant Park ice rink. No ticket is listed for the tree. The rink is free only with your own skates.
  • Cold or wet day: NYBG Holiday Train Show, Christmas Spectacular. The conservatory trains are indoors, with more outdoors. The show is indoors.
  • Free for under-3s: Lightscape, Bronx Zoo Holiday Lights. Lightscape lists ages 0 to 2 free; the zoo, 2 and under.
  • Sensory needs: NYBG Holiday Train Show, Lightscape, Bronx Zoo Holiday Lights. The dates are on the card.

Our picks from the published details, not from a visit. We have no crowd data of our own, so we don't name quiet nights.

Bryant Park ice rink

Winter Village, Midtown Manhattan

Free with your own skates4 rentals ≈ $75 to $258, estimate, price checked Nov 3

When: Now to Feb 28, 2027, weather permitting; 8 a.m. to midnight Nov 19 to Jan 3

  • Skate aids for small kids, $26, free before 10:30 a.m. if available
  • Free helmets; kids' skates from size 6T
  • Stroller parking from mid-November, first come
  • Own-skates tickets must be reserved, and are released a week ahead

Costs extra: Skate aids $26, socks or a lock $6.24 each, bag check $6.24 an item.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Rentals run $18.72 to $64.48 each, fee included, cheaper off-peak. Free with your own skates and a reserved ticket.

Good to know: Under-14s need an adult, and you can't carry a child while skating. There's no re-entry. A non-skating adult gets in free per two kids under 14; extras cost $11.29.

Lightscape

Brooklyn Botanic Garden

≈ $88 to $144tickets for 4, estimate from published prices, price checked Nov 3

When: Nov 20, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027

  • A trail of light displays, with wheelchair-accessible paths
  • No strobes, but some flashing lights
  • Sensory-friendly session from 4 p.m., Dec 3 and Dec 18
  • Skip Dec 10 and Dec 17: both are 21+ nights

Costs extra: Travel and food.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Fees included. Adult $29 Value nights, $39 off-peak, $44 peak; child 3-11 $15, $22, $28; age 0-2 free. Prices vary by night; the ticket calendar shows each night's tier.

Good to know: Earplugs are available at the box office. Trail length and stroller rules aren't on the page.

Brooklyn Botanic Garden Lightscape page (read Nov 3 unless noted)

Bronx Zoo Holiday Lights

Bronx Zoo

≈ $90 to $122tickets for 4, estimate from published prices, price checked Nov 3

When: Select nights, Nov 21, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027

  • Arrive from 1 p.m. with the 2-in-1 ticket to see animals by day; check your ticket type includes it
  • New this year: the Children's Zoo Farmyard opens for the lights
  • Sensory Inclusive Night on Dec 3, reserve ahead

Costs extra: Holiday Train $4 a ride; car parking $22.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Dated tickets: adult $29.95 or $37.95, child 3-12 $14.95 or $22.95, age 2 and under free. An any-night ticket is ≈ $142 for 4. We did not see whether tax or fees are added, or whether these prices cover the 2-in-1 ticket.

Good to know: It runs rain or shine. The calendar on the ticket page shows which nights are open. Stroller rules aren't on the pages we read.

NYBG Holiday Train Show

New York Botanical Garden, Bronx

≈ $100 to $112tickets for 4, estimate; All-Garden Pass, no year stated; the show price may differ, price checked Nov 3

When: Nov 14, 2026 to Jan 10, 2027; open to 8 p.m. on select evenings

  • Model trains inside a glass conservatory, more outdoors
  • Sensory-friendly mornings: Nov 21 and Jan 9, 9 to 11 a.m., with music off
  • Allow about 3 hours, per NYBG's guide from last season

Costs extra: Parking was $22 last season, first come.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Adult $35 weekday, $39 weekend; child $15, $17; under 2 free. The admission page states no year and no child age. Last season's show tickets charged kids $25-$27, so check your date. NYBG's show page says the All-Garden Pass includes the show. We did not see whether tax or fees are added.

Good to know: Last season NYBG called weekdays the quietest and Dec 20 to Jan 3 the busiest, with timed entry by day. Check whether that holds this year.

Christmas Spectacular

Radio City Music Hall, Midtown Manhattan

Check livewe couldn't read a price on Nov 3

When: Nov 4, 2026 to Jan 4, 2027, 230 shows

  • 90 minutes with no intermission
  • Under 2 sits on a lap with no ticket
  • Free stroller check; free sensory bags with headphones, ID needed

Costs extra: Ticket prices weren't on the pages we read; check fees at checkout.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: The official pages we read show no prices; they send you to the ticket calendar.

Good to know: It has sudden loud noises, flashing lights, smoke, confetti and pyrotechnics, so think about that for little ones. No minimum age is listed.
