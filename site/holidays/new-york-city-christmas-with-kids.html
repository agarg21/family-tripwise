Christmas in New York City With Kids: 8 Outings, Free to $144

Lights, trains, skating and a parade for 2 adults and 2 kids. Paid tickets run about $88 to $144 for 4.

Researched with AI (Miles Rowan) · sources linked and dated · How we check

  • Free: Vessel holiday lighting, Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, Bryant Park ice rink
  • Paid, tickets for 4: about $88 to $144, estimate
  • Price on its ticket calendar: Christmas Spectacular
  • Next up: Christmas Spectacular, Nov 4

New this season

  • The Vessel at Hudson Yards holds its second holiday lighting on Nov 16, free, with Anthony Ramos headlining.
  • Macy's parade turns 100 on Nov 26, with a record 100 inflatables, per NYC Tourism.

Which one fits your kids

  • Spending nothing on tickets: Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, Vessel holiday lighting, Bryant Park ice rink. Bryant Park skating is free only with your own skates.
  • Under 3: Lightscape, Bronx Zoo Holiday Lights. Both list babies free: 0 to 2 at Lightscape, 2 and under at the zoo.
  • Cold or wet day: NYBG Holiday Train Show, Christmas Spectacular. The trains run inside a warm conservatory; the show is indoors.
  • Early bedtime: Bryant Park ice rink, Bronx Zoo Holiday Lights, NYBG Holiday Train Show. Skate before 10:30 a.m., reach the zoo at 1 p.m., or see the trains by day.
  • Sensory needs: NYBG Holiday Train Show, Lightscape, Bronx Zoo Holiday Lights. Each lists special sessions; the dates are on its card.

We have no crowd data of our own, so we don't name quiet nights.

Bryant Park ice rink

Winter Village, Midtown Manhattan

Free≈ $75 to $258 for 4 with rentals, estimate, read Nov 3

When: Now to Feb 28, 2027, weather permitting; 8 a.m. to midnight Nov 19 to Jan 3

  • Skate aids for small kids, $26, free before 10:30 a.m. if available
  • Free helmets; kids' skates from size 6T
  • Stroller parking from mid-November, first come

Costs extra: Skate aids $26, socks or a lock $6.24 each, bag check $6.24 an item.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free with your own skates and a reserved ticket. Rentals run $18.72 to $64.48 each, fee included, cheaper off-peak. Four rentals: ≈ $75 to $258.

Good to know: Under-14s need an adult, and you can't carry a child while skating. A non-skating adult gets in free per two kids under 14; extras cost $11.29. No re-entry. Own-skates tickets are released a week ahead.

Bryant Park rink page (read Nov 3 unless noted)

Lightscape

Brooklyn Botanic Garden

≈ $88 to $144tickets for 4, estimate from published prices, read Nov 3

When: Nov 20, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027

  • An outdoor walking trail of light displays
  • Wheelchair-accessible paths; no strobes, but some flashing lights
  • Sensory-friendly early entry at 4 p.m., Dec 3 and Dec 18

Costs extra: Travel and food.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Fees included. Adult $29 Value nights, $39 off-peak, $44 peak; child 3-11 $15, $22, $28; age 0-2 free. The page doesn't say which nights are which: the ticket calendar will.

Good to know: Dec 10 and Dec 17 are 21+ nights, so skip those with kids. Earplugs are free at the box office. Trail length and stroller rules aren't on the page.

Brooklyn Botanic Garden Lightscape page (read Nov 3 unless noted)

Bronx Zoo Holiday Lights

Bronx Zoo

≈ $90 to $122tickets for 4, estimate from published prices, read Nov 3

When: Select nights, Nov 21, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027

  • Arrive from 1 p.m. to see animals by day before the lights
  • New this year: the Children's Zoo Farmyard opens for the lights
  • Sensory Inclusive Night on Dec 3, reserve ahead

Costs extra: Holiday Train $4 a ride; car parking $22 (zoo's rates page, read Nov 2).

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Dated tickets: adult $29.95 or $37.95, child 3-12 $14.95 or $22.95, age 2 and under free. An any-night ticket is ≈ $142 for 4.

Good to know: It runs rain or shine. The calendar on the ticket page shows which nights are open. Stroller rules aren't on the pages we read.

Bronx Zoo Holiday Lights tickets page (read Nov 3 unless noted)

NYBG Holiday Train Show

New York Botanical Garden, Bronx

≈ $100 to $112tickets for 4, estimate from published prices, read Nov 3

When: Nov 14, 2026 to Jan 10, 2027; open to 8 p.m. on select evenings

  • Model trains inside a warm glass conservatory
  • Sensory-friendly mornings: Nov 21 and Jan 9, 9 to 11 a.m., with music off
  • Allow about 3 hours, per NYBG's guide from last season

Costs extra: Parking was $22 last season, first come.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: All-Garden Pass, which NYBG says includes the show: adult $35 weekday, $39 weekend; child $15, $17; under 2 free. Last season's show tickets charged kids more ($25-$27), so check your date.

Good to know: Last season NYBG called weekdays the quietest and Dec 20 to Jan 3 the busiest, with timed entry by day. Check whether that holds this year.

NYBG admission page · NYBG Train Show page (read Oct 29) · NYBG last-season ticket page (read Nov 3 unless noted)

Christmas Spectacular

Radio City Music Hall, Midtown Manhattan

Check liveno price on the pages we read, Nov 3

When: Nov 4, 2026 to Jan 4, 2027, 230 shows

  • 90 minutes with no intermission
  • Under 2 sits on a lap with no ticket
  • Free stroller check; free sensory bags with headphones, ID needed

Costs extra: Prices vary by date and seat; check fees at checkout.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: The official pages we read show no prices; they send you to the ticket calendar.

Good to know: It has sudden loud noises, flashing lights, smoke, confetti and pyrotechnics, so think about that for little ones. No minimum age is listed.
