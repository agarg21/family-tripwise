How we check: each night is a separate 1-night Google Hotels search for 2 adults and kids 5 and 8, collected through DataForSEO on Nov 14, 2026, in US dollars. We compare a hotel only when both nights of both weekends had a price; "no price" never means sold out. We have not stayed at these hotels. Each weekend leaves this page 7 days before it starts.
