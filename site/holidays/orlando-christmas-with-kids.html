Which one fits your kids

  • Before Thanksgiving, Nov 14 to 22: Festival of Trees. Indoors, daytime hours, Santa on weekends.
  • Thanksgiving weekend, Fri Nov 27 to Sun Nov 29: Frontyard Holiday Festival, Now Snowing, Christmas Bricktacular, Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party. Frontyard (from Nov 27) and Now Snowing (from Nov 28) are free to enter; Bricktacular runs all three days and Mickey's party Nov 27 and 29, both ticketed.
  • Kids under 6: Lake Eola holiday nights. Free train, snow every half hour and Santa at Lake Eola, starting at 5:30 p.m.
  • Ages 4 and 8 together: Colorful Christmas. Indoor crafts and a scavenger hunt, no late night, and handy if it rains.
  • Keeping tickets at zero: Lake Eola holiday nights, Now Snowing, Frontyard Holiday Festival, Disney Springs Christmas Tree Stroll. Free to enter; parking or rides may cost.
  • Sensory-friendly: Asian Lantern Festival: Into the Wild, A Charlie Brown Christmas: Live on Stage. Set quieter sessions on the dates on each card.
  • Disney's big night: Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party. The costliest outing we could price, with a midnight finish, so weigh it against your kids' bedtime.

Our picks from the published details, not from a visit. We have no crowd data of our own, so we don't name quiet nights.

Outing by outing

Santa's Wonderland at Bass Pro Shops

Bass Pro Shops Orlando, 5156 International Drive

Freechecked Nov 9

When: Nov 8 to Dec 24, 2026, in store

  • A free 4x6 photo with Santa and a video; book a free slot up to 7 days ahead at basspro.com/santa, or walk in (first come, first served)
  • Crafts, and a mailbox for letters to Santa; add an email address for a reply
  • Christmas Tales story time with Santa and Mrs. Claus, Tuesdays at 5 p.m. to Dec 22 (book it separately)
  • Merry Morning Breakfast, Sat Dec 5 at 9 a.m., up to 4 per family; booking opens Nov 28

Costs extra: Photo packages, if you want more than the free print. We saw no parking notes.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: a free 4x6 photo with Santa and a free video; extra photo packages cost more (no price posted).

Good to know: The chain lists no daily Santa hours, age rules or sensory-friendly times; check the booking page for times. The breakfast price isn't stated.

Bass Pro Shops 2026 announcement · Orlando store page (read Nov 9 unless noted)

Disney Springs Christmas Tree Stroll

Disney Springs, Walt Disney World

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Disney's page gives no dates; Santa through Dec 24, per Kissimmee's tourism board

  • Themed Christmas trees to walk past
  • Meet Santa, per Disney's holiday page

Costs extra: Food and shopping. Orlando's tourism board lists free parking and admission.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: Disney says its Disney Springs holiday events are free.

Good to know: Disney's page lists no dates or hours for the stroll. Check before you go, and check for a line to see Santa.

Walt Disney World · Visit Orlando · Experience Kissimmee (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Frontyard Holiday Festival

Dr. Phillips Center, downtown Orlando

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Nov 27 to Dec 31, 2026: Wed to Fri 5 to 10 p.m., Sat and Sun 2 to 10 p.m.; from Dec 16 also Mon and Tue; closed Dec 14, 15, 24, 25

  • Weekend photos with Santa, 3 to 5 p.m., and a daily story corner
  • Crafts and letters to Santa at the Little Elves' Workshop
  • Free family movies Sundays at 6:30 and 7:45 p.m.

Costs extra: Food and drinks. We didn't see a parking price.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free to enter. Food, drinks, bocce, curling and private fire pits are separate, with no prices shown.

Good to know: The center says dates, times and programs can change. Snow times are on its daily calendar, not this page. We found no stroller rules here.

Dr. Phillips Center (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Now Snowing

Celebration Town Center, near Kissimmee

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Nightly Nov 28 to Dec 31, 2026, from 5:30 p.m.

  • Snow at 6, 7, 8 and 9 p.m., per Kissimmee's tourism board
  • Ice rink, holiday train and wagon rides in the town center

Costs extra: Skating $15 for 30 minutes; train $10 and wagon $7 a person; private carriage $85 for up to 4.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: admission and public parking are both free. Rides and skating cost extra.

Good to know: The town's page doesn't list snow times; those are from the tourism board. One line on its page runs the event to Jan 1, 2027; we use Dec 31. Santa photos run through Dec 24 at a package price. We found no stroller rules.

Celebration Town Center · Experience Kissimmee (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Lake Eola holiday nights

Lake Eola Park, downtown Orlando

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Fri to Sun, Dec 4 to 20, 2026; train 5:30 to 9 p.m.; snow from Dec 5, 6 to 9 p.m.; Santa also Dec 17, 21 and 22

  • 15 minutes of snow every half hour from 6 p.m., on Washington Street
  • Free Holiday Train on the Northeast Lawn
  • Tree lighting Fri Dec 4, 5:30 to 9:30 p.m., with snow and photos with Santa

Getting there: Parking in downtown lots and garages; we saw no price. The city suggests SunRail, rideshare or the free LYMMO.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: the city lists the train, the snowfall and Santa as free.

Good to know: Santa is first come: you scan a QR code on site to join the line, and it may close before 9 p.m. The city's page shows both 9 and 9:30 p.m. as the end. Free movies run on the East Lawn Dec 5, 12 and 19 at 7 p.m. We found no stroller rules.

Festival of Trees

Orlando Museum of Art, Loch Haven Park

≈ $76tickets for 4, estimate; $23 adults, $15 ages 3 to 12, price checked Nov 12

When: Daily Nov 14 to 22, 2026, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. (Tue Nov 17 closes at 3 p.m.)

  • Santa on Santa Claus Lane Sat-Sun Nov 14-15 and 21-22, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.
  • Breakfast with Santa Nov 14, 15 and 21, 8:30 to 10 a.m. (separate ticket)
  • Reindeer Romp kids' party Fri Nov 20, 6:30 to 9 p.m. (separate ticket)
  • Decorated trees and gingerbread on display; strollers welcome

Costs extra: $8 a day to park in the lot next to the museum.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Tickets sell on Eventbrite. The festival pages don't say what kids under 3 pay, or list prices for the breakfast or the party.

Good to know: Nine days only, the 40th year. We saw no timed entry. Festival Café open 11 a.m. to 3 p.m.

Dazzling Nights

Harry P. Leu Gardens, Orlando

From ≈ $136tickets for 4, estimate; from $35 adults, $33 ages 4 to 12, fees included; 3 and under free, price checked Nov 4

When: Select nights, Nov 20, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027; entry from 5:30 p.m. every 15 minutes

  • Walk-through light trail, about 3/4 mile
  • Age 3 and under free

Costs extra: Parking isn't included; valet must be prepaid, or a free shuttle runs from an off-site garage.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Adults from $35, children 4 to 12 from $33; packages with food and photos from $266 for a family of 4. Prices vary by night and entry time. Entry closes 5 minutes after your time slot.

Good to know: No closing time or stroller rule on the page.

Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party

Magic Kingdom, Walt Disney World

≈ $736 to $896tickets for 4, estimate; $189 to $229 each by date, $10 less for ages 3 to 9; plus tax, price checked Nov 4

When: Nov 8, 9, 12, 13, 15, 17, 19, 20, 24, 25, 27, 29; Dec 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13, 15, 17, 18, 20, 22, 2026; 7 p.m. to midnight

  • Parade, fireworks, stage show and characters
  • Cookies and drinks included at set spots
  • Runs to midnight; you can enter from 4 p.m.

Costs extra: Tax; dessert parties, dining and PhotoPass.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: A separate party ticket; no regular park ticket needed. Disney's page doesn't list a price for under 3.

Good to know: The priciest of the outings we could price. Hotels, parking and park days are not counted.
