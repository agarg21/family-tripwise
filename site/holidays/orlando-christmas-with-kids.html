Which one fits your kids

  • Thanksgiving Day, Thu Nov 26: Disney Springs Christmas Tree Stroll. Free, though no stroll dates are posted; check the app for Santa that day. Or Gatorland park, open all holidays 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., about $120 for 4 plus tax (estimate). Closed: Crayola.
  • Thanksgiving weekend, Fri Nov 27 to Sun Nov 29: Frontyard Holiday Festival, Now Snowing, Christmas Bricktacular, Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party. Frontyard (from Nov 27) and Now Snowing (from Nov 28) are free to enter; Bricktacular runs all three days and Mickey's party Nov 27 and 29, both ticketed.
  • Kids under 6: Lake Eola holiday nights. Free train, snow every half hour and Santa at Lake Eola, starting at 5:30 p.m.
  • Ages 4 and 8 together: Colorful Christmas. Indoor crafts and a scavenger hunt, no late night, and handy if it rains.
  • Keeping tickets at zero: Lake Eola holiday nights, Now Snowing, Frontyard Holiday Festival, Disney Springs Christmas Tree Stroll. Free to enter; parking or rides may cost.
  • Sensory-friendly: Asian Lantern Festival: Into the Wild, A Charlie Brown Christmas: Live on Stage. Set quieter sessions on the dates on each card.
  • Disney's big night: Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party. The costliest outing we could price, with a midnight finish, so weigh it against your kids' bedtime.

Our picks from the published details, not from a visit. We have no crowd data of our own, so we don't name quiet nights.

Outing by outing

Santa's Wonderland at Bass Pro Shops

Bass Pro Shops Orlando, 5156 International Drive

Freechecked Nov 9

When: Nov 8 to Dec 24, 2026, in store

  • A free 4x6 photo with Santa and a video; book a free slot up to 7 days ahead at basspro.com/santa, or walk in (first come, first served)
  • Crafts, and a mailbox for letters to Santa; add an email address for a reply
  • Christmas Tales story time with Santa and Mrs. Claus, Tuesdays at 5 p.m. to Dec 22 (book it separately)
  • Merry Morning Breakfast, Sat Dec 5 at 9 a.m., up to 4 per family; booking opens Nov 28

Costs extra: Photo packages, if you want more than the free print. We saw no parking notes.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: a free 4x6 photo with Santa and a free video; extra photo packages cost more (no price posted).

Good to know: The chain lists no daily Santa hours, age rules or sensory-friendly times; check the booking page for times. The breakfast price isn't stated.

Bass Pro Shops 2026 announcement · Orlando store page (read Nov 9 unless noted)

Disney Springs Christmas Tree Stroll

Disney Springs, Walt Disney World

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Santa Nov 11 to Dec 24, 2026, by virtual queue only (Disney, read Nov 15); no stroll dates posted

  • Themed Christmas trees to walk past
  • Meet Santa near Disney's Pin Traders: no walk-up line; join the free virtual queue in the My Disney Experience app once you are at Disney Springs

Costs extra: Food and shopping. Orlando's tourism board lists free parking and admission.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: Disney says its Disney Springs holiday events are free.

Good to know: Queue requests open at 9 and 11 a.m., 1, 3 and 5 p.m.; Disney says times can change. Each guest may request once, in a party of up to 10. No dates or hours are posted for the tree stroll.

Walt Disney World · Disney virtual queues (read Nov 15) · Visit Orlando (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Frontyard Holiday Festival

Dr. Phillips Center, downtown Orlando

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Nov 27 to Dec 31, 2026: Wed to Fri 5 to 10 p.m., Sat and Sun 2 to 10 p.m.; from Dec 16 also Mon and Tue; closed Dec 14, 15, 24, 25

  • Weekend photos with Santa, 3 to 5 p.m., and a daily story corner
  • Crafts and letters to Santa at the Little Elves' Workshop
  • Free family movies Sundays at 6:30 and 7:45 p.m.

Costs extra: Food and drinks. We didn't see a parking price.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free to enter. Food, drinks, bocce, curling and private fire pits are separate, with no prices shown.

Good to know: The center says dates, times and programs can change. Snow times are on its daily calendar, not this page. We found no stroller rules here.

Dr. Phillips Center (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Now Snowing

Celebration Town Center, near Kissimmee

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Nightly Nov 28 to Dec 31, 2026, from 5:30 p.m.

  • Snow at 6, 7, 8 and 9 p.m., per Kissimmee's tourism board
  • Ice rink, holiday train and wagon rides in the town center

Costs extra: Skating $15 for 30 minutes; train $10 and wagon $7 a person; private carriage $85 for up to 4.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: admission and public parking are both free. Rides and skating cost extra.

Good to know: The town's page doesn't list snow times; those are from the tourism board. One line on its page runs the event to Jan 1, 2027; we use Dec 31. Santa photos run through Dec 24 at a package price. We found no stroller rules.

Celebration Town Center · Experience Kissimmee (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Lake Eola holiday nights

Lake Eola Park, downtown Orlando

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Fri to Sun, Dec 4 to 20, 2026; train 5:30 to 9 p.m.; snow from Dec 5, 6 to 9 p.m.; Santa also Dec 17, 21 and 22

  • 15 minutes of snow every half hour from 6 p.m., on Washington Street
  • Free Holiday Train on the Northeast Lawn
  • Tree lighting Fri Dec 4, 5:30 to 9:30 p.m., with snow and photos with Santa

Getting there: Parking in downtown lots and garages; we saw no price. The city suggests SunRail, rideshare or the free LYMMO.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: the city lists the train, the snowfall and Santa as free.

Good to know: Santa is first come: you scan a QR code on site to join the line, and it may close before 9 p.m. The city's page shows both 9 and 9:30 p.m. as the end. Free movies run on the East Lawn Dec 5, 12 and 19 at 7 p.m. We found no stroller rules.

Holidays at Universal Orlando

Universal Studios Florida, Islands of Adventure and Epic Universe

Park ticket, check pricechecked Nov 14

When: Nov 14, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027

  • Included with a Universal Studios Florida or Islands of Adventure ticket; not stated for Epic Universe
  • New this year: holiday decor at Epic Universe (Super Nintendo World, Isle of Berk, Celestial Park)
  • Holiday Parade featuring Macy's at Universal Studios Florida; no reservation, come 25-30 min early
  • Grinchmas show in Seuss Landing; Hogwarts Castle light show from dusk to close

Costs extra: Grinch & Friends Character Breakfast (reserve; select days) and the Holiday Tour with Santa (about 2.5 hours), both with no price posted.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Included with park tickets at Universal Studios Florida and Islands of Adventure, Universal's FAQ says; it doesn't say so for Epic Universe. We could not read a day-ticket price.

Good to know: Parade start times are in the Universal Orlando app. Universal's guide gives the dates without a year; we read them as this season. It says nothing about ages or strollers.

Universal: guide to the holidays (posted Nov 4) (read Nov 14 unless noted)

Holiday Ho, Ho, Ho-Down

Gatorland, south Orlando

≈ $120tickets for 4, estimate; $34.99 adults, $24.99 ages 3 to 12, before $3 off each online; 2 and under free, price checked Nov 20

When: Sat and Sun Dec 5, 6, 12, 13, 19 and 20, 2026

  • Holiday characters, music and treats
  • Gator park open 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., so it's a daytime outing
  • The gator park (not the Ho-Down) is open Thanksgiving Day, Nov 26: Gatorland says it opens every day, all holidays included

Costs extra: Tax (amount not listed). Stroller rental $11 single, $16 double. Parking is free, Gatorland says (read Nov 15).

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Included with regular admission, per Kissimmee's tourism board.

Good to know: Dates come from Kissimmee's tourism board and Visit Orlando; Gatorland's events page still showed Halloween when we looked in early November. Mostly outdoors.

Colorful Christmas

Crayola Experience, Florida Mall

≈ $124tickets for 4, estimate; $30.99 each plus tax; 2 and under free, price checked Nov 20

When: Nov 21, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027

  • Holiday crafts, a scavenger hunt and a crayon character in holiday dress
  • Mail a letter to Santa; a singing Christmas tree and a 10-minute singing light show
  • Closed Thanksgiving Day, Nov 26; Black Friday 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.
  • Christmas Eve 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.; closed Christmas Day
  • Open to 8 p.m. Dec 21 to 23 and Dec 26 to 30

Costs extra: Tax, plus Wax Hands (add-on; event page, Nov 4).

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Orlando's tourism board says it's included with admission; Crayola's page doesn't say so outright. Children under 15 need an adult with them (FAQ, Oct 27).

Good to know: Indoors. Crayola's page doesn't mention Santa himself.

Dazzling Nights

Harry P. Leu Gardens, Orlando

From ≈ $136tickets for 4, estimate; from $35 adults, $33 ages 4 to 12, fees included; 3 and under free, price checked Nov 4

When: Select nights, Nov 20, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027; entry from 5:30 p.m. every 15 minutes

  • Walk-through light trail, about 3/4 mile
  • Age 3 and under free

The Magic Show: Holiday Edition

JW Marriott, Grande Lakes Orlando (the show page also lists the Ritz-Carlton there)

≈ $200 to $360tickets for 4, estimate; $49.99 to $89.99 a seat on the Jan 2 show page; no child price posted; fees and tax not shown, price checked Nov 22

When: Fri–Sat to Dec 12, plus Sun Nov 22, Mon Nov 23, Sun Dec 6 and Sat Jan 2, 2027; none listed Dec 13 to Jan 1; 5 or 7 p.m.

  • Magician Drew Thomas, an America's Got Talent finalist; the show page calls it great for all ages
  • Small audience: designed for fewer than 100 guests; the Jan 2 show is listed as one hour
  • No minimum age on the show pages

Before you buy: All sales are final: no refunds or exchanges. Parking isn't included; eating at a partner restaurant gets it validated, per the show page.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Only the Jan 2 page we read lists prices; other dates may differ. The show pages list no child price or free age.

Good to know: Some nights have a second show (time not listed). The show calendar lists no dates Dec 13 to Jan 1, but its Jan 2 page says the show plays every Friday and Saturday, so check live before planning around those weeks. The resort's own event page still showed May dates on Nov 15, so book through the show's site.

≈ $736 to $896tickets for 4, estimate; $189 to $229 each by date, $10 less for ages 3 to 9; plus tax, price checked Nov 4

When: Nov 8, 9, 12, 13, 15, 17, 19, 20, 24, 25, 27, 29; Dec 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13, 15, 17, 18, 20, 22, 2026; 7 p.m. to midnight

A Christmas Carol at Orlando Shakes

Margeson Theater, Orlando Shakes

Check livewe couldn't read a price on Nov 15

When: Nov 25 to Dec 24, 2026, 20 dates, mostly Wed and Fri–Sun; 2 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. shows, times vary by date

  • Ages 5 and up only: under-5s aren't admitted, per the show page, so a 4-year-old can't go
  • Rated PG for spooky content and ghosts, with heavy fog and smoke effects
  • 1 hour 50 minutes, with one 15-minute intermission

Before you buy: ASL-interpreted and audio-described show Dec 17 at 7:30 p.m.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: The show page lists no regular adult or child prices.

Good to know: The ticket site lists 20 dates, ending Christmas Eve; it doesn't say which dates have the 2 p.m. or the 7:30 p.m. show.

Orlando Ballet’s The Nutcracker

Steinmetz Hall, Dr. Phillips Center, 445 S Magnolia Ave

Check livewe couldn't read a price on Nov 18

When: Fri Dec 4 to Sun Dec 27, 2026; 1 p.m. show on Christmas Eve

  • Family-friendly shows (2 p.m. matinees from Dec 12, and Christmas Eve at 1 p.m.) add pre-show activities, and you can step out and back in
  • 1 hour 57 minutes plus a 20‑minute intermission

Before you buy: Kids must be 2 or older (ballet’s page). Dec 22–23 matinees: the theater’s showtime list has only 7 p.m., so confirm the time. Parking is sold per show; we saw no rate.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Neither the ballet’s nor the theater’s page lists single-ticket prices; check live on the ticket page.

Good to know: For Dec 22 and 23, the theater’s showtime list has only the 7 p.m. shows, though both pages list 2 p.m. family shows those days. Confirm the time before you buy.
