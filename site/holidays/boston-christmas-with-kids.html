Coming in December? Boston's holiday tree arrives and Copley Square tree lighting are over by then.

Which one fits your kids

  • Kids under 6: Boston's holiday tree arrives. A free daytime hour; by our reading of the 11 a.m. to noon listing, it's over before an afternoon nap.
  • Ages 4 and 8 together: Copley Square tree lighting. Crafts, Santa and a children's chorus in one free hour; then cookies and photos with Santa at the Fairmont Copley Plaza, 6 p.m.
  • Keeping tickets at zero: Boston's holiday tree arrives, Copley Square tree lighting, Boston Common Tree Lighting, First Night Boston. All free with no tickets; getting there and food may cost.
  • A cold or wet day: Santa in the City at the Prudential Center. Indoors at the Prudential Center on Saturdays through Dec 19.
  • Sensory-friendly: Boston Ballet's The Nutcracker. Dec 4 at 1:30 p.m., designed for neurodiverse guests and guests with sensory sensitivities.

Our picks from the published details, not from a visit. We have no crowd data of our own, so we don't name quiet nights.

Boston's holiday tree arrives

Boston Common, 139 Tremont Street

Freechecked Oct 28

When: Tuesday, Nov 24, 2026, 11 a.m. to noon

  • The tree from Nova Scotia arrives by police escort at about 11 a.m.
  • A short daytime event, good for kids too young for the evening lighting

Costs extra: Getting there and food.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free; the city's listing names no tickets or registration.

ZooLights at Stone Zoo

Stone Zoo, Stoneham, MA

Check livechecked Nov 10

When: Nov 20 to Jan 3, 4 to 9:30 p.m. (last entry 8:30 p.m.); closed Thanksgiving and Christmas Day. The zoo's page gives no year; check before you book.

  • Children under 2 free
  • Timed tickets; buy online at least a day ahead, as evenings can sell out
  • Stroller rental at the zoo

Costs extra: Stroller rental: $5 single, $7 double, with a photo ID held at admissions.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Plan-ahead pricing: the online price shows when you pick a date, and gate prices are higher. Under 2 free.

Good to know: All sales are final; the zoo says it refunds automatically if it cancels for weather.

Zoo New England ZooLights page (read Nov 10 unless noted)
