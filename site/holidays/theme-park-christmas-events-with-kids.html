Why Disney, Universal and Knott's aren't compared

Not listed yet: Disney and Knott's Berry Farm, because we could not confirm this season's dates and prices on their official pages.

Universal Orlando: Nov 14 to Jan 3 at all three parks, included with a park ticket. No year printed; we read it as this season. We could not read a day-ticket price. Universal Orlando events guide (read Nov 3).

Which one fits your kids

  • All about Santa: Hersheypark or SeaWorld San Diego. Both pages list Santa with reindeer.
  • Kids who love LEGO: LEGOLAND Florida or LEGOLAND California. Both list reindeer feeding and a giant LEGO tree or light trail.
  • A parade and a train: Silver Dollar City. Its page lists a lights parade and a sing-along steam train.
  • Rain in the forecast: SeaWorld Orlando. Its page says many shows are indoors, rain or shine.
  • Lowest ticket: Busch Gardens Williamsburg. It's a separate event ticket, so add parking and tax.

If bedtime matters: go by day for Santa and shows, and skip fireworks nights. Check the park's showtimes first. We have no crowd data, so we don't name quiet nights.
