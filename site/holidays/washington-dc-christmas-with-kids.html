Which one fits your kids

  • Kids under 6: Season's Greenings. Daytime trains with no tickets, so you can leave when naps call.
  • Ages 4 and 8 together: ZooLights. Animal lanterns from 5 p.m., before bedtime, with a maze and mini-train for the older one.
  • Keeping tickets at zero: Season's Greenings, DowntownDC Holiday Market, Holiday Boat Parade & Party. Free to enter; food, parking and shopping may cost.
  • Sensory-friendly: Season's Greenings. Dec 5 in the morning; sign up first. Jan 2 in the evening; sign up first.
  • Staying in the car: Bull Run Festival of Lights. A light drive that still works if a child falls asleep.
  • One big night: Christmas Illuminations. Two nights only, with a lit maze, a camel and fireworks.

Our picks from the published details, not from a visit. We have no crowd data of our own, so we don't name quiet nights.

DowntownDC Holiday Market

F Street NW, 7th to 9th Streets

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Nov 13 to Dec 23, 2026, closed Thanksgiving; Mon to Thu noon to 8 p.m., Fri and Sat 11 a.m. to 9 p.m., Sun 11 a.m. to 8 p.m.

  • A zone just for kids, says the tourism board
  • Lights, and live concerts Thursday to Sunday

Costs extra: Shopping and food; prices vary by vendor.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free to walk in, says the tourism board.

Good to know: We saw no stroller notes for this street market. Weekend evenings: check before you bring a stroller. Metro: Gallery Place-Chinatown.

DowntownDC Holiday Market · Tourism board guide (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Season's Greenings

U.S. Botanic Garden, on the National Mall

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Nov 26, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027; 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., closed Dec 25; to 8 p.m. on Dec 8, 10, 15, 17, 22 and 29

  • Model trains running through national parks and landmarks made from plant parts
  • Strollers welcome
  • Sensory-friendly openings Dec 5 (morning) and Jan 2 (evening); sign up first

Costs extra: Getting there and food on evening music nights; we saw no prices.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: the garden says no admission fee and no tickets for any area.

Good to know: The trains may stop in bad weather, and the train display is outdoors, so check the sky first.

U.S. Botanic Garden holiday page (read Nov 4 unless noted)

National Christmas Tree lighting

The Ellipse, President's Park

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Thu Dec 3, 2026, by free ticket only; lottery entries end Nov 4 at 3 p.m. ET, winners told Nov 12

  • The park service hasn't described this year's display yet
  • In 2025: 59 smaller trees with ornaments designed by students

Costs extra: Getting there; ticket holders get arrival and security details before the night.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: no charge for the lottery or tickets, up to 5 per winner, and no fee to visit the tree site.

Good to know: Entering doesn't guarantee tickets. The park service says visiting details are still to come, so we can't give viewing dates or hours yet, or stroller notes.

National Park Service tree page · Lottery notice (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Holiday Boat Parade & Party

The Wharf, District Pier

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Sat Dec 5, 2026: activities from 5 p.m., boats 7 p.m., fireworks about 8 p.m.

  • Photos with Santa in The Grove
  • Ornament decorating and games at M Street Landing
  • Lit boats, then fireworks over the channel

Costs extra: Parking in The Wharf's garages, which fill fast on event days; we saw no price.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free for spectators, says The Wharf.

Good to know: Boats don't arrive until 7 p.m., so small kids can do Santa and crafts at 5 and leave early. Fireworks are loud.

The Wharf boat parade (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Folger Frost Fair

Folger Shakespeare Library, Capitol Hill

Free entrychecked Nov 4

When: Nov 27, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027; hours not on the festival page

  • Printing press demonstrations, with a printed souvenir to take home
  • Free family acting workshop, Dec 5 at 11:30 a.m. and 1 p.m.
  • Free family storytimes, Dec 22 to 24 at 10:30 a.m.

Costs extra: Other programs may charge; check each one.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: The tourism board says free admission with pay-what-you-will timed passes; the Folger's page gives no price for the whole fair.

Good to know: No age range, hours or stroller notes on the festival page. Check your pass and time first.

Folger Frost Fair · Tourism board December guide (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Bull Run Festival of Lights

Bull Run, Centreville, VA

≈ $30 to $45tickets for 4, estimate; $30 to $45 a vehicle online, by date, price checked Nov 4

When: Daily, Nov 14, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027, weather permitting; 5:30 to 9:30 p.m. Mon to Thu, to 10 p.m. Fri to Sun and holidays

  • A light drive you do from your own car
  • Carnival at the end with a petting zoo, Holiday Village and Stable, included
  • Carnival rides and camel rides cost extra

Costs extra: Tax, transaction fees, and rides, which aren't priced on the page.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Price is per vehicle, not per person; buying early saves the most.

Good to know: The carnival is closed on Thanksgiving; the light drive runs. You'll need a car, and this one is outside DC.

NOVA Parks: Bull Run (read Nov 4 unless noted)

ZooLights

Smithsonian's National Zoo

≈ $40tickets for 4, estimate; $10 each, ages 3 and up; 2 and under free, price checked Nov 4

When: Nov 20, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027; 5 to 9 p.m., Sundays to 8 p.m.; last entry 30 minutes before close; Dec 16 is 21+ only, Dec 8 and 9 are Member Nights

  • Animal lanterns across ocean, desert, grassland, rainforest and polar areas
  • Winter maze, mini-train and carousel at extra cost
  • Saturday carolers on Dec 5, 12, 19 and 26

Costs extra: Parking $30 ahead or $40 that day; maze, mini-train and carousel $6 each; light-up glasses $3.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Buy ahead: the zoo says capacity is limited. No refunds; exchanges up to 48 hours before.

Good to know: Most animals are resting and indoor houses closed on regular nights, so go for the lights. The page has no stroller notes.

National Zoo: ZooLights (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Winter Walk of Lights

Meadowlark Botanical Gardens, Vienna, VA

≈ $76 to $92tickets for 4, estimate; $19 each online to Dec 3 and Jan 1 to 3, $23 Dec 4 to 31; no child price shown, price checked Nov 4

When: Nov 11, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027; from 5:30 p.m. to Dec 3, from 5 p.m. after; last entry 9:30 p.m.

  • Half-mile paved trail, stroller and wheelchair accessible
  • Hot drinks, pretzels and make-your-own s'mores for sale

Costs extra: Tax and transaction fees; door price $24 when sold at the door.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Dec 4 to 31 is online only, no door tickets.

Good to know: The page doesn't say if babies or toddlers need a ticket. You'll need a car, and this one is outside DC. Tickets can't be refunded.

NOVA Parks: ticket information (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Christmas Illuminations

George Washington's Mount Vernon, VA

≈ $152tickets for 4, estimate; $58 ages 12+, $36 ages 6 to 11, 5 and under free; your 4-year-old is free, price checked Nov 4

When: Sat Dec 19 and Sun Dec 20, 2026, 5:30 to 8:30 p.m.; fireworks about 8 p.m.

  • Straw-bale kids' maze filled with Christmas lights
  • Aladdin the Camel and carols in the field
  • Fireworks over the Potomac

Costs extra: Parking is free, first come. Mansion tour add-on $5; s'mores kit $8 for 2.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Separate from daytime estate tickets: the estate closes between the two.

Good to know: Large strollers and stroller wagons aren't allowed on the east lawn; the page gives no other stroller rule. Fireworks are loud and late for small kids.

Mount Vernon event page (read Nov 4 unless noted)
