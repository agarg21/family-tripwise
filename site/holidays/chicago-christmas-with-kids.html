Coming in December? Magnificent Mile Lights Festival, Chicago Thanksgiving Parade and Chicago Christmas Tree lighting are over by then.

Which one fits your kids

  • Kids under 6: ZooLights. Santa through Dec 23. Free on reserved Mondays through Dec 14. Strollers and wagons allowed.
  • Ages 4 and 8 together: Wintrust Winter WonderFest. Skating is in the ticket, and there's more at Navy Pier.
  • Keeping tickets at zero: ZooLights, Magnificent Mile Lights Festival. ZooLights on a reserved Monday, or the free parade day.
  • Cold or wet day: Wintrust Winter WonderFest, Christmas Around the World. Indoor options, from the organizers' pages.
  • Sensory needs: Illumination at The Morton Arboretum, ZooLights, Holiday Magic. Each has one sensory night, dated on its card.

Our picks from the published details, not from a visit. We have no crowd data of our own, so we don't name quiet nights.

Magnificent Mile Lights Festival

Michigan Avenue, Oak Street to Wacker Drive

Freechecked Nov 3

When: Saturday, Nov 21, 2026: daytime fest 11 a.m. to 4 p.m., parade 5:30 p.m., fireworks about 7:15 to 7:30 p.m. (sources differ)

  • Free photos with Santa at the daytime fest, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.
  • Parade at 5:30 p.m., so little ones can do the daytime part only

Costs extra: Travel and food.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free, per the city's tourism board.

Good to know: Michigan Avenue closes 3 to 7:30 p.m. and some streets from 8 a.m. The organizer says the north end's east side is less crowded. We found no stroller rules.

Christmas Around the World

Griffin Museum of Science and Industry, Hyde Park

Museum admission, check pricechecked Nov 4

When: Opens Nov 7, 2026; the page lists no closing date

  • Indoors: a four-story Grand Tree and light displays
  • You can bring a stroller, except in posted exhibits
  • All-ages evening event Dec 19 (the Dec 10 one is 21+)

Costs extra: Food; check parking on the museum's page.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Included in General Admission. The museum's price table didn't read clearly, so check the price on its pricing page. Kids are 3 to 11. No Illinois free days remain in 2026.

Good to know: Naughty or Nice: All Ages Edition is an evening event on Dec 19. The Dec 10 one is 21+. Check hours on the museum's page.

ZooLights

Lincoln Park Zoo, Lincoln Park

≈ $28 to $48tickets for 4, estimate; $7 to $12 each, bought ahead, everyone needs a ticket, price checked Nov 4

When: Nov 20, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027, 4:30 to 9 p.m. Closed Nov 26, Dec 8, 15, 24, 25

  • Free Mondays with a reservation: Nov 23, 30, Dec 7, 14. Tickets release a week ahead, 11 a.m. CT
  • Santa nightly through Dec 23; photos cost extra
  • Last entry 8 p.m. Strollers and wagons allowed; crowded nights can be hard
  • Sensory-friendly night: Tue Nov 24, lights static or slowed, music softer

Costs extra: Parking, limited and paid, price not listed.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: $7 to $12 bought ahead, by date; $20 on the day or for a flexible ticket. Every person needs a ticket, including babies. The page doesn't say which dates cost what.

Good to know: Check Dec 1 before you plan it: the zoo's page lists it as closed in one place but not another. Dec 21 and 28 are not free Mondays. Every person needs a ticket.

Lincoln Park Zoo event page (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Illumination at The Morton Arboretum

Lisle, in the western suburbs

≈ $88tickets for 4, estimate; 4 at the $22 adult starting price; no child price is listed, and prices vary by date and time, price from Oct 1 release, read Nov 4

When: Nov 14, 2026 to Jan 2, 2027, from 4:30 p.m.

  • A one-mile paved trail the arboretum says strollers can roll on
  • Kids from age 4 count as a ticket on the AnyDay ticket
  • Sensory night: Tue Nov 17, static lights, low music

Costs extra: Pine Whistle Railroad $9, age 3 and under free; premium parking $18 a car.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Adults start at $22. Cheaper tickets on select nights for EBT, LINK and WIC cardholders: $10 adult, $5 child. Prices vary by day and time, and buying ahead costs less.

Good to know: You can't reach the trail until your ticket's entry time.

Illumination · Oct 1 release (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Wintrust Winter WonderFest

Festival Hall, Navy Pier

≈ $108 to $128tickets for 4, estimate; $27 online a day ahead, $32 at the door, age 3 and up, price checked Nov 4

When: Nov 27, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027, from 10 a.m.; closing time varies. Closed Dec 25; Nov 30 to Dec 2 not listed

  • In Festival Hall; its photos show an indoor rink. Skating is in every ticket
  • Age 2 and under free

Costs extra: Centennial Wheel $10 each, same day.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: $27 online at least 24 hours ahead; $32 later or at the door. Children 2 and under are free; everyone 3 and up needs a ticket.

Good to know: Some dates close at 5 p.m., as do Dec 24 and 31. The page doesn't mention strollers or Santa.

Navy Pier (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Holiday Magic

Brookfield Zoo Chicago, Brookfield

≈ $120tickets for 4, estimate; 4 at the $29.95 starting price; the page shows no child price, price checked Nov 4

When: Select dates Nov 21, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027, 3 to 9 p.m. Not listed: Nov 26, Nov 30 to Dec 2, Dec 7 to 9, Dec 14 to 16, Dec 24, 25
