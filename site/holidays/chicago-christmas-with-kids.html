Coming in December? Magnificent Mile Lights Festival and Chicago Thanksgiving Parade are over by then.

Which one fits your kids

  • Thanksgiving Day, Thu Nov 26: Chicago Thanksgiving Parade. Free parade from 7:45 a.m. (the organizer's site says 8 to 11). Also free: Morton Arboretum grounds in Lisle (a drive west; last entry 3 p.m.) and Lincoln Park Zoo (no Thanksgiving hours posted). Closed: ZooLights, Holiday Magic, Lightscape.
  • Kids under 6: ZooLights. Santa through Dec 23. Free on reserved Mondays through Dec 14. Strollers and wagons allowed.
  • Ages 4 and 8 together: Wintrust Winter WonderFest. Skating is in the ticket, and there's more at Navy Pier.
  • Keeping tickets at zero: ZooLights, Magnificent Mile Lights Festival, Santa's Wonderland at Bass Pro Shops. ZooLights on a reserved Monday, the Mag Mile Lights parade on Nov 21, or a free Santa photo in Bolingbrook.
  • Cold or wet day: Wintrust Winter WonderFest, Christmas Around the World, Winter Flower Show at Garfield Park Conservatory. Indoor options, from the organizers' pages.
  • Sensory needs: ZooLights, Holiday Magic, Lightscape at the Chicago Botanic Garden. Each has one sensory night, dated on its card.

Our picks from the published details, not from a visit. We have no crowd data of our own, so we don't name quiet nights.

Outing by outing

Santa's Wonderland at Bass Pro Shops

Bass Pro Shops Bolingbrook, 709 Janes Ave, in the southwest suburbs (a drive from the Loop)

Freechecked Nov 10

When: Nov 8 to Dec 24, 2026, in store

  • A free 4x6 photo with Santa and a video; book a free slot up to 7 days ahead at basspro.com/santa, or walk in (first come, first served)
  • Crafts, and a mailbox for letters to Santa; add an email address for a reply
  • Christmas Tales story time with Santa and Mrs. Claus, Tuesdays at 5 p.m. to Dec 22 (book it separately)
  • Merry Morning Breakfast, Sat Dec 5 at 9 a.m., up to 4 per family; booking opens Nov 28 (price not posted)

Costs extra: Photo packages, if you want more than the free print. We saw no parking or transit notes; it is a suburban store, so check your route.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: a free 4x6 photo with Santa and a free video; extra photo packages cost more (no price posted).

Good to know: A suburban store in Bolingbrook, not in the city. The dates are chain-wide; the chain lists no daily Santa hours, age rules or sensory-friendly times, so check this store's booking page for Santa slots, story time and the breakfast. The breakfast price isn't stated.

Vintage Holidays in Long Grove

Historic Downtown Long Grove, in the northwest suburbs (Lake County)

Freechecked Nov 9

When: Nov 20 to Dec 24, 2026. Kickoff Fri Nov 20, 5 to 8 p.m.; bridge and tree lighting 7:30 p.m.

  • Free photos with Santa Sat Nov 28, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., by the covered bridge; first come, no booking
  • Santa in the village Nov 29, Dec 5, 6 and 20, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. (subject to change)

Costs extra: Horse-drawn carriage rides, weekends Nov 21 to Dec 20: $15 a seat, 5 and under free on a lap. About $45 for 2 adults and kids 4 and 8 (estimate).

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free admission, per Visit Lake County, the county tourism board.

Good to know: A suburban outing: the organizer lists no transit, parking or stroller notes, so plan on driving. The kickoff is the same night as the Millennium Park tree lighting. Carriages need a waiver, no refunds; sign-up said coming soon when we read it.

Freechecked Nov 19

Chicago Thanksgiving Parade

State Street, the Loop

Freechecked Nov 13

When: Thursday, Nov 26, 2026: starts 7:45 a.m., per the tourism board and the Chicago Sun-Times (citing an organizers' spokesperson); the organizer's site lists 8 to 11 a.m. (sources differ)

  • Plan on 7:45 a.m.: the earlier start lets TV coverage begin at 8, per the Chicago Sun-Times (read Nov 18)
  • Smaller crowds at the south end, near Ida B. Wells Drive, per the organizer
  • Balloons dip under the L tracks by Harold Washington Library, per the organizer
  • Free later that day: Morton Arboretum grounds in Lisle, western suburbs (last entry 3 p.m.; buildings and Children's Garden closed), read Nov 15
  • Free later that day: Lincoln Park Zoo by day, opens 10 a.m. (no Thanksgiving hours posted; ZooLights closed that night), read Nov 15

Costs extra: Travel and food.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free to watch from the street, per the city's tourism board. Paid VIP seats are listed; we saw no price.

Good to know: The route runs north on State Street, Ida B. Wells Drive to Randolph Street. Expect closures and bus detours; L stops nearby include Harold Washington Library and Jackson. Live on CBS Chicago, 8 to 10 a.m. We found no stroller rules.

CTA Holiday Train and Holiday Bus

A different 'L' line and bus route each day, citywide

Just the CTA farechecked Nov 18

When: Bus: Nov 24 to Dec 21, late morning to about 8 p.m. Train: Nov 27 to Dec 22, afternoon and early evening.

  • Check CTA's schedule for today's line, then find the train in CTA Train Tracker (a candy cane) or the bus in CTA's holiday bus tracker (live only while it runs)
  • Santa waves from a sleigh on the train and from the bus roof hatch, per CTA
  • Photos with Santa on the train Sat Nov 28, Dec 5, 12 and 19, on your own device; first come, one set-up per family

Fare: About $6.25 for 4 on one 'L' ride, estimate: $2.50 per adult, $1.25 at age 8, under 7 free, per CTA. A ride back within 2 hours is free.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: 'L' $2.50, bus $2.25 an adult; $1.25/$1.10 reduced for ages 7 to 11; under 7 free with a paying adult. Pay by Ventra card, app or contactless tap; up to 2 more rides within 2 hours are free. It runs as regular service, so normal CTA fares apply and there is no separate ticket, per CTA.

Good to know: It's regular service, so boarding isn't guaranteed, and Santa photo stops have a firm cut-off. CTA encourages small folding strollers; fold when it's crowded, and no strollers on escalators. Bus times are approximate.

Winter Flower Show at Garfield Park Conservatory

Garfield Park, by the CTA Green Line Conservatory stop

From ≈ $25tickets for 4, estimate; from outside Chicago: 2 × $10 + $5 (age 8); no price listed for age 4, price checked Nov 18

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Outside Chicago: adults $10, kids 7 to 17 $5. Chicago residents free; donations welcome. The flower show is part of general admission. Book a ticket ahead; walk-ins get in only if there's room. No price is listed for kids under 7.

Good to know: Show details and holiday hours aren't posted yet, and hours may change on holidays; check before Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's. One FAQ answer says last entry is 4:30 Thursday to Sunday. The Children's Garden needs its own 30-minute reservation.

ZooLights

Lincoln Park Zoo, Lincoln Park

≈ $28 to $48tickets for 4, estimate; $7 to $12 each, bought ahead, everyone needs a ticket, price checked Nov 20

When: Nov 20, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027, 4:30 to 9 p.m. Closed Nov 26, Dec 8, 15, 24, 25

  • Free Mondays with a reservation: Nov 23, 30, Dec 7, 14. Tickets release a week ahead, 11 a.m. CT
  • Santa nightly through Dec 23; photos cost extra
  • Last entry 8 p.m. Strollers and wagons allowed; crowded nights can be hard
  • Sensory-friendly night: Tue Nov 24, lights static or slowed, music softer

Costs extra: Parking: limited paid lot on Cannon Drive, price not listed, not included in tickets.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: $7 to $12 bought ahead, by date; $20 on the day or for a flexible ticket. Any gate tickets left are cash only. Every person needs a ticket, including babies. The page doesn't say which dates cost what.

Good to know: Check Dec 1 before you plan it: the zoo's page lists it as closed in one place but not another. Dec 21 and 28 are not free Mondays. Security screening at entry: walk through scanners at the East and West Gates; bags and strollers may be inspected. Leave outside food and drinks at home.

Metra Holiday Train

Six Metra lines; Santa at Millennium Station on Metra Electric

≈ $40tickets for 4, estimate; $10 a ticket; Metra lists no child price or age rule, so we count all 4, price checked Nov 14

When: Dec 5: BNSF, Milwaukee North, Rock Island. Dec 12: Metra Electric, UP Northwest. Dec 13: Metra Electric. Dec 19: Metra Electric, UP North.

  • Tickets on sale from Monday, Nov 16 at noon at shop.metra.com; Metra expects them to sell out fast
  • Decorated trains with holiday characters make round trips, per Metra
  • Metra Electric rides (Dec 12, 13 and 19) go to a winter wonderland at Millennium Station with games, treats and Santa

Other costs: None listed on Metra's page.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: $10 a ticket, up to five per order, at shop.metra.com. Tickets are required and must be bought in advance, per Metra. All sales are final: no refunds and no swaps to another Holiday Train. Pay by card through PayPal; no PayPal account needed.

Good to know: Departure times are in each line's holiday schedule, linked from Metra's page. Metra's page says nothing about ages, strollers, bags or food, or whether Santa rides on the other lines. The decorated cars also run in regular service during December, per Metra.

Illumination at The Morton Arboretum

Lisle, in the western suburbs

≈ $88tickets for 4, estimate; 4 at the $22 adult starting price; no child price is listed, and prices vary by date and time, price from Oct 1 release, read Nov 4

When: Nov 14, 2026 to Jan 2, 2027, from 4:30 p.m.

  • A one-mile paved trail the arboretum says strollers can roll on
  • Kids from age 4 count as a ticket on the AnyDay ticket

Costs extra: Pine Whistle Railroad $9, age 3 and under free; premium parking $18 a car.

Holiday Magic

Brookfield Zoo Chicago, Brookfield

≈ $120tickets for 4, estimate; 4 at the $29.95 starting price; the page shows no child price, price checked Nov 15

When: Select dates Nov 21, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027, 3 to 9 p.m. Not listed: Nov 26, Nov 30 to Dec 2, Dec 7 to 9, Dec 14 to 16, Dec 24, 25

  • Santa, free with admission: Nov 21, 22, 27 to 29; Dec 4 to 6, 11 to 13, 18 to 23

Lightscape at the Chicago Botanic Garden

Glencoe, in the north suburbs

Check livewe couldn't read a price on Nov 18

When: Nov 13, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027; most nights 4:30 to 10:30 p.m., last entry 8:45; closed Thanksgiving and Christmas Day

  • 1.3-mile trail, paved and crushed gravel; strollers welcome except on 21+ nights
  • Kids under 3 free; most visitors spend 45 to 75 minutes
  • Family Nights with Santa: Wed Dec 9 and 16
  • Sensory night Wed Dec 2: smaller crowds, lower lights and music

Costs: Tickets vary by date and time, plus a fee for non-members. Parking for non-members: $18 a car booked ahead, $25 on arrival.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Prices change by date, time and demand, and non-members pay a service fee on each paid ticket. We couldn't open the ticket page to read a price.

Good to know: Timed entry every 15 minutes; don't come more than 15 minutes early. Rain or shine. Two nights are 21+ only, with no strollers; check the date. Some nights close at 9:30.
