Which one fits your kids

  • Kids under 6: River Walk Holiday Lights. Free and no ticket, so an early look costs nothing if bedtime wins. Avoid parade night, Nov 27: no strollers then.
  • Ages 4 and 8 together: Illuminate. A light trail where strollers are allowed, with timed entry from 5:30 p.m. Hands-on stations on Family Night, Dec 11.
  • Keeping tickets at zero: River Walk Holiday Lights, Free Saturdays at the Alamo, Lights of Esperanza, Holidays at The Rock, Santa's Wonderland at Bass Pro Shops. Free to enter; parking, skating or food may cost.
  • Sensory-friendly: Illuminate. Dec 1, 5:30 to 7 p.m.: walk the trail with the sound off, or bring headphones.
  • Big day out: Holiday in the Park, Christmas Celebration. Whole-day parks where the holiday shows and lights come with admission on select dates.

Our picks from the published details, not from a visit. We have no crowd data of our own, so we don't name quiet nights.

Outing by outing

Santa's Wonderland at Bass Pro Shops

Bass Pro Shops San Antonio, 17907 IH-10 West

Freechecked Nov 9

When: Nov 8 to Dec 24, 2026, in store

  • A free 4x6 photo with Santa and a video; book a free slot up to 7 days ahead at basspro.com/santa, or walk in (first come, first served)
  • Crafts, and a mailbox for letters to Santa; add an email address for a reply
  • Christmas Tales story time with Santa and Mrs. Claus, Tuesdays at 5 p.m. to Dec 22 (book it separately)
  • Merry Morning Breakfast, Sat Dec 5 at 9 a.m., up to 4 per family; booking opens Nov 28

Costs extra: Photo packages, if you want more than the free print. We saw no parking notes.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: a free 4x6 photo with Santa and a free video; extra photo packages cost more (no price posted).

Good to know: The chain lists no daily Santa hours, age rules or sensory-friendly times; check the booking page for times. The breakfast price isn't stated.

Bass Pro Shops 2026 announcement · San Antonio store page (read Nov 9 unless noted)

River Walk Holiday Lights

Downtown River Walk

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Nightly, Nov 27, 2026 to Jan 11, 2027 (tourism board date; the River Walk says early January)

  • About 200,000 lights in the cypress trees over the river
  • Boat caroling on select December evenings; we saw no schedule

Costs extra: Parking, food and any river boat ride; we saw no prices.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: the River Walk says viewing the lights takes no ticket.

Good to know: The River Walk is a public park open all day, but no lighting hours are posted. We found no stroller or step-free route notes for ordinary nights, so check your route.

River Walk lights page · Visit San Antonio lights guide (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Ford Holiday River Parade

River Walk, from the Tobin Center

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Fri Nov 27, 2026, 6 p.m.; live broadcast from 7:05 p.m.

  • Lit floats on the river, with Santa and Mrs. Claus
  • The season's lighting happens the same night
  • No strollers, ice chests or carriers on the River Walk during the event

Paid seats: Optional: about $16 to $35 a seat with fees in most areas, or up to $117 in Area L. Ages 2 and up need a seat ticket. No refunds.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free in the viewing area on the east side of the River Walk, Pecan Street to Richmond Avenue; first come, bring chairs.

Good to know: Paid-seat arrival windows run from 6 p.m. to about 7:50 p.m. by area; the page doesn't say when floats reach each spot, so small kids may wait.

River Walk parade page · Seat tickets (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Free Saturdays at the Alamo

Alamo Plaza and Plaza de Valero

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Nov 28, 6 to 8 p.m. tree lighting; Dec 5, 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. living history; Dec 12, 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. Cowboy Santa; Dec 19, 6 to 8 p.m. holiday movie

  • Cowboy Santa and Mrs. Claus, with free photos, Dec 12
  • Frontier life demonstrations and a band, Dec 5
  • Holiday movie on the pavilion screen, Dec 19; bring a chair or blanket

Costs extra: Parking; we saw no price.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: the Alamo lists all four as free.

Good to know: Dec 5 includes a musket firing, which is loud. The church and grounds are closed during the Nov 28 lighting. The film isn't named.

Texas A&M University-San Antonio

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Tue Dec 1, 2026; no start time posted yet

  • Ice slide, mechanical reindeer and letters to Santa
  • Ornament decorating and build-a-snowman

Costs extra: Nothing listed; parking is first come in Lots 2 and 3.

Holidays at The Rock

The Rock at La Cantera, Frost Plaza

Free entry; skating extrachecked Nov 4

When: Nov 21 to Jan 3; tree lighting Sat Nov 21, 7 to 10 p.m.; free movies Dec 18 to Jan 2

  • Outdoor ice rink, with skate rental on site
  • Free movies include Elf Dec 20, The Grinch Dec 21, Rudolph Dec 23 and Frozen Dec 26

Costs extra: Skating; book online, walk-ups are rare and tickets are non-refundable.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Tree lighting and movies are free, with free parking at the lighting. Skating needs a ticket; no price is listed.

Good to know: Rink days vary: Thu to Sun 4 to 10 p.m. for Dec 1 to 18, then daily 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. to Jan 3. Closed Thanksgiving and New Year's Eve; closes 7 p.m. Christmas Eve. Some movies are not for little kids, so check the list.

The Rock at La Cantera (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Illuminate

San Antonio Botanical Garden

≈ $126tickets for 4, estimate; $32.99 adult, $29.99 child, with the $3 fee each, price checked Nov 4

When: Nov 20, 2026 to Jan 10, 2027; timed entry 5:30 to 9 p.m.; sensory night Dec 1; Family Night Dec 11, 6 to 9 p.m.

  • Glowing light trail with handcrafted lanterns
  • Strollers allowed; under 3 need no ticket
  • Family Night stations: cider sachets and nature ornaments

Costs extra: Parking: $20 pass bought ahead, or free on Old Austin Rd. until full.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Adult $29.99, child 3 to 12 $26.99, plus $3 fee each. Timed tickets; Family Night and the sensory night come with a regular ticket.

Good to know: Pick an early slot for small kids. On Dec 1, choose entry between 5:30 and 7 p.m.; the trail can be walked without sound.

Garden: Illuminate · Family Night · Sensory night (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Zoo Lights

San Antonio Zoo, Brackenridge Park

Check livewe couldn't read a price on Nov 4

When: Nov 14, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027, select dates

  • Light zones through the zoo after dark
  • Holiday Express Train, says the tourism board

Costs extra: Possibly the train; we saw no price. A $99 pack covers 4 tickets, hot dogs, chips and drinks, with no end date stated.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Regular zoo admission was listed from $8 each on Nov 1, ages 3 and up; 2 and under free. The tourism board says it comes with regular zoo admission; the zoo's own page doesn't say or show a 2026 price.

Good to know: The zoo posts no 2026 Zoo Lights hours we could find. Its garage is free during zoo hours; check that covers your evening.

ice! featuring Rudolph

JW Marriott San Antonio Hill Country Resort

Check livewe couldn't read a price on Nov 9

When: Nov 23, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027

  • Rudolph ice scenes and two-story ice slides, with smaller slides for little kids
  • Kept at 9°F, with free parkas from infant sizes up; kids 3 and under free
  • Small strollers only: no double strollers or wagons
  • The resort says most visitors spend 20 to 40 minutes inside

Costs: Tickets: check your date on the ticket calendar. Parking: the resort lists $29 a day self-park, $45 valet; if you are not staying there, parking is charged each time you enter.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Buy ahead for a date and entry time; no box office on site. Bundles add tubing and other activities; no bundle prices were shown on Nov 9.

Good to know: Dress kids warm under the parkas. Day tickets can’t be refunded, and there’s no re-entry. Resort address: 23808 Resort Pkwy.

Resort Christmas page · ice! page · ice! FAQ · Visitor FAQ (parking) · Tickets (read Nov 9 unless noted)

Christmas at the Caverns

Natural Bridge Caverns, outside the city

Check livewe couldn't read a price on Nov 4

When: Dec 5 to 23, 2026 (tourism board date; the caverns say "opens December 2026")

  • A 1/3-mile Trail of Lights and a reindeer maze
  • Santa in Discovery Village and Mrs. Claus stories
  • Synthetic skating free for ages 3 to 11 with any Christmas ticket

Costs extra: Caroling tour and Twisted Trails are add-ons. Parking was $10 on our last record.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: The caverns name three ticket types but show no prices yet.

Good to know: Caroling tours sell out, the caverns say. Check the drive from your hotel; it's outside the city.
