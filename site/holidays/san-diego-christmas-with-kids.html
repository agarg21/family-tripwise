Which one fits your kids

  • Kids under 6: Holiday Wonderland. Carousel, train, real-snow sledding and Santa all in one ticket, in one square.
  • Ages 4 and 8 together: Christmas Bricktacular. LEGO reindeer, the giant tree and characters, if you can spend the day.
  • Keeping tickets at zero: December Nights, Little Italy Tree Lighting & Christmas Village, San Diego Bay Parade of Lights. Free to attend.
  • One special ride: North Pole Limited train. Santa comes to your seat. Check the drive to Campo first.

Our picks from the published details, not from a visit. We have no crowd data of our own, so we don't name quiet nights.

Outing by outing

December Nights

Balboa Park

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Fri Dec 4, 2026, 4 to 10 p.m.; Sat Dec 5, noon to 10 p.m.

  • Saturday starts at noon, so little ones can go in daylight
  • SeaWorld family activities and photo spot at the Palm Stage

Costs extra: Food. We didn't see a parking price.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: the city calls it its largest free holiday festival.

Good to know: Last year's city page listed free shuttles from City College, downtown and Little Italy; the 2026 page wasn't updated when we read it, so check before relying on them. We found no stroller rules.

City of San Diego · Shuttles (2025 page) (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Little Italy Tree Lighting & Christmas Village

Piazza della Famiglia and India Street (W. Cedar to W. Grape), Little Italy

Freechecked Nov 9

When: Sat Dec 5, 2026, 4 to 8 p.m.; tree lighting 6:30 p.m.

  • The tree is lit at 6:30 p.m., so you can leave by 7
  • Santa is there, per the organizer; it lists no photo price
  • 10-foot nutcrackers, live music and holiday treats

Costs extra: Food from vendors. We didn't see a parking price.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free, per Visit San Diego; the organizer lists no ticket.

Good to know: Same Saturday as December Nights in Balboa Park, so pick one. The organizer lists no transit, parking or stroller notes; check its site before you go.

Little Italy Association · Visit San Diego listing (read Nov 9 unless noted)

San Diego Bay Parade of Lights

Embarcadero and Coronado waterfront

Freechecked Nov 4

When: Sundays Dec 13 and 20, 2026; starts 5:30 p.m. at Shelter Island

  • About an hour to pass any one spot, so it's a short outing
  • Less crowded: the north and south ends of the Embarcadero

Getting there: Parking is limited; the organizer suggests the trolley.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: "There is no cost to view the parade."

Good to know: The boats start at Shelter Island at 5:30 p.m., so they reach downtown and Coronado later; the page gives no clock time per spot. Arrive early. It lists free parking on Shelter Island and Harbor Island, limited.

Parade organizer · Schedule and route (read Nov 4 unless noted)

North Pole Limited train

Campo Depot, east San Diego County

From ≈ $90tickets for 4, estimate; Classic Coach from $30 adults, $15 ages 1 to 17; prices rise near Christmas, price checked Nov 4

When: Fri and Sat Nov 27 to Dec 19, 2026, 5 and 7 p.m.; Sun Nov 29 to Dec 20, 3 and 5 p.m.

  • 90-minute ride; Santa and Mrs. Claus visit every passenger
  • Under 1 on a lap rides free

Costs extra: Business and First Class cost more. We didn't see a parking price.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Classic Coach from $30 adults, $15 ages 1 to 17; Business from $50 / $35; First from $65 / $50. Fares are starting prices and rise closer to Christmas. The best seats sell out quickly.

Good to know: Online sales close 30 minutes before departure. The page doesn't mention strollers.

Pacific Southwest Railway Museum (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Lightscape

San Diego Botanic Garden, Encinitas

From ≈ $92tickets for 4, estimate; from $28 adults, $18 kids, by date; 2 and under free, price checked Nov 4

When: Nov 20, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027

  • Over 1 million lights with color and sound, per the tourism board
  • Age 2 and under free

Costs extra: Parking $15 ahead, $20 on site if space.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Adults from $28, children from $18. Prices vary by visit date. Children 2 and under are free. The page doesn't say what age counts as a child.

Good to know: The garden's page doesn't mention strollers or closing time. KPBS lists a 5 p.m. daily start.

San Diego Botanic Garden · KPBS listing · San Diego Tourism (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Holiday Wonderland

Gallagher Square, Petco Park, downtown

From ≈ $100tickets for 4, estimate; from about $25 each, by date and time; same price for kids, price checked Nov 4

When: Select dates Dec 4 to 23, 2026

  • Sledding on real snow, carousel, Christmas train, carnival games
  • Meeting Santa is in the ticket

Costs extra: Food. We didn't see a parking price.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Starts around $25 a person. Children and adults pay the same; the price varies by date and time. All attractions are included. The Padres' page gives no year; San Diego's tourism board lists 2026.

Good to know: Check sledding age or height limits and hours: the page gives none, and no stroller rules or free age. Nightly snowfall is listed.

Padres event page · San Diego Tourism (read Nov 4 unless noted)

Skating by the Sea

Hotel del Coronado, Coronado

≈ $140 to $180tickets for 4, estimate; $35 to $45 each by date, skates included, price checked Nov 4

When: Nov 20, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027. Dec 1 to 17 opens later on weekdays

  • Beachfront rink; double-runner skates in toddler sizes
  • Under 12 needs a skating adult; no strollers on the ice

Costs extra: Parking; helmets, bring your own.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: $40 to $45 through Dec 17, $45 from Dec 18; $35 before noon on select dates and all day Dec 1 and 8. No separate child price. No refund if a small child decides not to skate.

Good to know: No skate aids are offered. Weekday openings Dec 1 to 17 are 4 p.m. (Fri 2 p.m.). Closed 4 to 6 p.m. Nov 30.

Hotel del Coronado (read Nov 4 unless noted)

SeaWorld Christmas Celebration

SeaWorld San Diego, Mission Bay

From ≈ $208tickets for 4, estimate; from $51.99 each, lowest date we saw; tax and fees extra, price checked Nov 1

When: Daily Nov 13, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027

  • Meet Santa and two reindeer; snowfall every night
  • Baby Shark's Christmas Adventure sing-along

Costs extra: Parking; scavenger-hunt map.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Included with park admission. Your date can cost more.

Good to know: Fireworks on Dec 12 and Dec 31 only. SeaWorld's page prints the end year as 2026; we read it as Jan 3, 2027.

SeaWorld event page (read Nov 3) · Tickets (read Nov 1 unless noted)

Wild Holidays

San Diego Zoo Safari Park, Escondido

≈ $272 to $292tickets for 4, estimate; $78 ages 12+, $68 ages 3 to 11; online Value Days $73 / $63 where offered, price checked Nov 1

When: Nov 28 to 29, Dec 18 to 23, Dec 26, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027, to 8 p.m.

  • Story time from 4 p.m., musical tunnel of light 5 to 8 p.m.
  • Photo with Santa at Safari Base Camp on listed dates

Costs extra: Optional safaris. We didn't see a parking price.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Activities are included with admission or membership.

Good to know: Many habitats and the Africa Tram close before dark; the park suggests arriving early. Santa's hours differ by date; he takes breaks.

Jungle Bells

San Diego Zoo, Balboa Park

≈ $272 to $292tickets for 4, estimate; $78 ages 12+, $68 ages 3 to 11; online Value Days $73 / $63 where offered, price checked Nov 1

When: Dec 19, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027, to 8 p.m., not Dec 24

  • Light shows from 5 p.m., kids' stage shows and roaming performers
  • Santa's Factory photo spot at Treetops Plaza

Costs extra: The page doesn't say if Santa photos cost extra. We didn't see a parking price.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Activities are included with every zoo ticket, except on Dec 24.

Good to know: Santa is also there on Nov 27 to 29 and some December weekends, noon to 4 or 6 p.m. We didn't confirm which Value Days fall in these dates.

When: Nov 21 to 29, Dec 5 to 6, 12 to 13, Dec 19 to Jan 3

  • Feed LEGO reindeer, dance around the giant LEGO tree

  • Holiday lights across the aircraft carrier
  • Age 3 and under free; youth tickets are ages 4 to 12

Golden State Ballet's The Nutcracker

San Diego Civic Theatre, 1100 Third Avenue, downtown

Check livewe couldn't read a price on Nov 9

When: Thu Dec 17 to Wed Dec 23, 2026 (no show Dec 21)

  • Three family- and sensory-friendly matinees: Sat Dec 19 at 2 p.m., Tue Dec 22 and Wed Dec 23 at 1 p.m., with recorded music at lower volume and a flexible audience setting
  • About 2 hours 10 minutes with one intermission; the ballet recommends ages 3 and up

Before you buy: Kids under 3 aren't admitted, even on a lap, and everyone needs a ticket. All sales are final. Bags up to 12 x 6 x 12 inches; no coat check. Arrive about an hour early.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: No ticket prices are posted on the ballet's or the theatre's pages; tickets sell through the ballet's box office or Ticketmaster, the ballet's box office adds no processing fee. The three family matinees are sold at a reduced rate (amount not posted).

Good to know: Other shows have the San Diego Symphony playing live. Parking is at the Evan V. Jones Parkade; we saw no rate.
