Which one fits your kids

  • Kids under 6: Ethel M Holiday Cactus Garden. Pay at the gate, no reservations, and kids under 2 free. Santa is there nightly from 5 p.m. through Dec 23.
  • Ages 4 and 8 together: Opportunity Village Magical Forest. Rides, a 50-foot slide and mini golf. Rides need riders 42 inches tall unless a grown-up rides along.
  • Keeping tickets at zero: Downtown Summerlin Holiday Parade, Santa's Wonderland at Bass Pro Shops. No ticket listed for either; parking, food or photo packages may cost.
  • Skating: Oasis Ice Rink at Fontainebleau. Ages 3 and up, with skates included in the fee. Under-18s need an adult 21 or older.
  • Sensory-friendly: Santa Chalet, Rock Rink and holiday train at Downtown Summerlin. Sensory Santa Photos, Sat Dec 5, 10 a.m. to noon.

Our picks from the published details, not from a visit. We have no crowd data of our own, so we don't name quiet nights.

Outing by outing

Santa's Wonderland at Bass Pro Shops

Bass Pro Shops Las Vegas, 8200 Dean Martin Dr (south of the Strip)

Freechecked Nov 10

When: Nov 8 to Dec 24, 2026, in store

  • A free 4x6 photo with Santa and a video; book a free slot up to 7 days ahead at basspro.com/santa, or walk in (first come, first served)
  • Crafts, and a mailbox for letters to Santa; add an email address for a reply
  • Chain-wide: Christmas Tales story time with Santa and Mrs. Claus, Tuesdays at 5 p.m. to Dec 22 (book it separately)
  • Merry Morning Breakfast, Sat Dec 5 at 9 a.m., up to 4 per family; booking opens Nov 28

Costs extra: Photo packages, if you want more than the free print. We saw no parking notes.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Free: a free 4x6 photo with Santa and a free video; extra photo packages cost more (no price posted).

Good to know: The chain lists no daily Santa hours, age rules or sensory-friendly times; check the booking page for times. The store page says only that Santa's Wonderland runs "during most of November and December".

Downtown Summerlin Holiday Parade

Park Centre Drive, Downtown Summerlin (west Las Vegas)

No ticket listedchecked Nov 10

When: Fridays and Saturdays, Nov 20 to Dec 19, 2026, at 6 p.m.; no parade Fri Nov 27

  • Starts at 6 p.m., an early-evening show
  • Nine Friday and Saturday nights (none on Fri Nov 27), so you can pick a night that fits

Parking: The parade page has no parking notes.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: The organizer's parade page lists no ticket or price.

Good to know: Santa, an ice rink and a holiday train are also at Downtown Summerlin from Nov 13: see the Santa Chalet, Rock Rink and holiday train card. We saw no stroller notes.

Downtown Summerlin parade page (read Nov 10 unless noted)

Ethel M Holiday Cactus Garden

Ethel M Chocolates, 2 Cactus Garden Drive, Henderson (Las Vegas area)

≈ $12tickets for 4, estimate; 4 people at $3 each (kids 2 and up pay), price checked Nov 10

When: Nov 5, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027, 5 to 10 p.m. most nights; Dec 24, Dec 31 and Jan 1 to 8 p.m.; closed Dec 25

  • Santa nightly from 5 p.m. through Dec 23; the line closes at 9 p.m.
  • A lit cactus garden: the 33rd year of the display
  • Closed Christmas Day; shorter hours Dec 24, Dec 31 and Jan 1

Costs extra: Santa photos: $35 for one print, $50 for two. A $40 Santa Express Pass (up to 6 people) books a set time.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Pay at the gate, cash or card; no reservations. Under 2 and 65 and over get in free. Proceeds go to Help of Southern Nevada and Three Square Food Bank.

Good to know: Park in an Ethel M lot or the Smith's lot across the street; the page says cars left in the nearby business park or Roseman University lots are towed. No pets. We saw no stroller notes.

Ethel M Holiday Cactus Garden (read Nov 10 unless noted)

Santa Chalet, Rock Rink and holiday train at Downtown Summerlin

Macy's Promenade and The Lawn, Downtown Summerlin (west Las Vegas)

From ≈ $28tickets for 4, estimate; 4 rides on the Jupiter Express holiday train at $7 each (adults and kids pay the same), price checked Nov 11

When: Santa Chalet daily Nov 13 to Dec 24; Rock Rink Nov 13 to Jan 18, 2027 (hours vary); train rides from Nov 13

  • Santa Chalet in Macy's Promenade, open daily to Dec 24
  • Sensory Santa Photos for families with sensory needs, Sat Dec 5, 10 a.m. to noon
  • Rock Rink: all ages welcome; book ahead, walk-ups only if there's room

Costs extra: Santa photo packages from $39.99; skate rental $22 a skater.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Train $28 for 4; Santa photo packages from $39.99; skate rental ≈ $88 for 4 skaters. Train rides are per person. Santa photos are sold as packages. Rink entry price is not posted, only skate rental; some mid-week dates have special pricing (no amounts posted). Check the rink booking page.

Good to know: Early-booking offers for Santa visits Nov 13 to 25. Las Vegas Ice Theater skating show at the rink Sun Dec 6, 10:45 a.m. Hanukkah celebration with a menorah lighting and kids' activities, Sun Dec 6, 4 to 6 p.m. Another Ice Theater show Sun Dec 20, 10:45 a.m. No parking or stroller notes on these pages. Rink hours vary: check times on the rink booking page.

Downtown Summerlin holidays page (Nov 3, 2026) · Rock Rink page · Rock Rink booking (linked from the rink page; not read) (read Nov 11 unless noted)

Enchant Christmas

Las Vegas Ballpark

From ≈ $108tickets for 4, estimate; from $27 a ticket; price varies by date, time and demand, price checked Nov 10

When: Nov 20 to Dec 27, 2026

  • A light maze, plus Santa, live entertainment and caroling
  • Storybook Night, Fri Dec 4: guest characters read stories; you can bring a book for a book drive
  • Under-5s skate with a parent; snow slides are one rider at a time
  • Kids need their own add-on tickets for activities

Costs extra: Parking and skating are add-ons; we saw no prices.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: A family offer for 2 adults and 2 juniors is listed; we saw no price for it, or the junior and infant ages.

Good to know: No refunds; if weather cancels a night, Enchant offers a new date the same season. Infant tickets can't add activities. We saw no hours, stroller rules or snow-slide height and age limits.

Enchant Christmas Las Vegas · Enchant Storybook Night, Las Vegas (read Nov 10 unless noted)

Oasis Ice Rink at Fontainebleau

Fontainebleau Las Vegas, north end of the Strip

From ≈ $120tickets for 4, estimate; 4 skaters at the $30 starting fee, skates included; plus taxes and fees, price checked Nov 10

When: Nov 18, 2026 to Jan 4, 2027, nightly; hours vary (from 2 p.m. or 5 p.m.; check the night)

  • Skaters must be 3 or older
  • Under-18s skate with an adult 21 or older; every skater signs a waiver
  • Tree lighting on opening night, Wed Nov 18, 5 to 7 p.m.

Costs extra: Taxes and fees on top of the $30 fee; fire-pit rentals from $200.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Skate fees are per person; the page lists no separate child price. Bringing your own skates doesn't lower the fee.

Good to know: Late sessions run to 2 a.m. on some dates, so check the night's hours. Nevada residents with ID get a cheaper late-night rate on select dates, paid at the door. Skating ends at 11:30 p.m. on New Year's Eve.

Fontainebleau Las Vegas (read Nov 10 unless noted)

Opportunity Village Magical Forest

Opportunity Village, 6300 W. Oakey Blvd., Las Vegas

Check livewe couldn't read a price on Nov 10

When: Nov 27 to 29, Dec 3 to 24 and Dec 26, 2026 to Jan 1, 2027; 5:30 to 9 p.m. Sun to Thu, to 9:30 p.m. Fri and Sat

  • Forest Express train, a 50-foot Avalanche Slide, a carousel and 8-hole mini golf
  • Rides need riders 42 inches tall unless a grown-up rides along
  • Santa visits; kids 3 and under free

Costs extra: Midway games, $5 each.

Ticket details, tips and sources

Ticket: Tickets are for one date, can't be refunded and are best booked online; kids 3 and under are free. We couldn't read a single-ticket price.

Good to know: Free parking in the College of Southern Nevada lot. Diaper bags are allowed; backpacks aren't. No outside food except water. Nov 30 to Dec 2 and Dec 25 aren't on the date list. We saw no stroller notes. Groups of 10 or more pay $19 each ($21 on peak nights).
