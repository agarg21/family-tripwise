Event

ZooLights at Lincoln Park Zoo: four free Monday nights with a reservation

When
Friday, November 20, 2026 to Sunday, January 3, 2027, 4:30 to 9 p.m.; last entry 8 p.m. No ZooLights on Thanksgiving (November 26), Tuesdays December 1, 8 and 15, or December 24 and 25.

Event

Smithsonian ZooLights: USD 10 tickets, free for kids 2 and under

When
Select evenings from November 20, 2026 to January 3, 2027: Nov 20-22, Nov 27-29, Dec 4-6, Dec 11-13, Dec 18-23, Dec 26-30 and Jan 2-3 (December 16 is adults-only). Monday to Saturday 5-9 p.m. (last entry 8:30 p.m.), Sunday 5-8 p.m. (last entry 7:30 p.m.), rain or shine.
Cost
USD 10 per person; everyone age 3 and older needs a ticket and children 2 and younger are free. Event parking is USD 30 per vehicle in advance or USD 40 on the day, subject to availability.
Booking
Tickets can be bought online in advance, and the zoo encourages buying early because capacity is limited. December 8 and 9 are Member Nights, with free entry tickets reserved in advance by zoo members.
Family note
Indoor animal exhibits are closed on ZooLights nights, and the zoo closes to everyone at 4 p.m. and reopens at 5 p.m. for ticket holders. Woodley Park-Zoo and Cleveland Park Metro stations are within walking distance.

Official page, checked October 28, 2026: Smithsonian National Zoo ZooLights news release (October 8, 2026). Listed here until January 3, 2027.
