Chicago

Event

ZooLights at Lincoln Park Zoo: four free Monday nights with a reservation

When
Friday, November 20, 2026 to Sunday, January 3, 2027, 4:30 to 9 p.m.; last entry 8 p.m. No ZooLights on Thanksgiving (November 26), Tuesdays December 1, 8 and 15, or December 24 and 25.
Cost
Fixed-date tickets USD 7 to USD 12 per person bought in advance, by date; USD 20 bought on the day or for a flexible ticket. Free on Mondays November 23 and 30 and December 7 and 14 with a reservation.
Booking
Everyone needs a ticket, including children and infants. Free Monday tickets are released one week ahead at 11 a.m. CT.
Family note
Because infants need a ticket too, count every child when booking a free Monday.

Orlando

Event

Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party: 25 nights at Magic Kingdom

When
Select nights November 8 to December 22, 2026: November 8, 9, 12, 13, 15, 17, 19, 20, 24, 25, 27 and 29; December 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13, 15, 17, 18, 20 and 22. The party runs 7 p.m. to midnight; ticket holders may enter Magic Kingdom from 4 p.m.
Cost
USD 189 to USD 229 per ticket plus tax, by date. Children ages 3 to 9 pay USD 10 less per ticket.
Booking
Separate event ticket. Disney says tickets are limited and may not be available on the day of the event or earlier.
Family note
A late night for young children: the party ends at midnight. Check remaining nights on Disney's ticket page.

Washington, DC

Event

National Christmas Tree Lighting: free ticket lottery closes November 4

When
Lottery open from October 28 at 10 a.m. ET until Wednesday, November 4, 2026 at 3 p.m. ET. Winners are notified Thursday, November 12, 2026. The ceremony is December 3, 2026 on the Ellipse.
Cost
Free to enter and free tickets.
Booking
Enter online on the Recreation.gov ticket page for the ceremony. Each winner may receive up to five tickets; entering does not guarantee tickets.
Family note
Up to five tickets per winner covers many families. The release gives no start time and no age rules for children.

London

Event

Hyde Park Winter Wonderland: every visitor needs a booked entry slot

When
Every day from Thursday, November 19, 2026 to Sunday, January 3, 2027, except Christmas Day. Most days 10 a.m. to 10 p.m., some days open at 11 a.m., noon or 2 p.m.; last entry 9:30 p.m.
Cost
Off-Peak entry slots are free but must still be booked. Standard and Peak slots are paid; check the current price on the official ticket site.
Booking
Pre-book a date and an hourly arrival slot for everyone, including children ("whether you're 2 or 92"). Advance prices apply until 23:59 the day before.
Family note
The final day, January 3, 2027, is billed as a Family Fun Day; details were not yet published.
