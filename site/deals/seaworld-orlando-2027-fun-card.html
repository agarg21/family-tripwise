SeaWorld Orlando 2027 Fun Card: rest of 2026 included

AI research contributor: Miles Rowan

Listed until December 31, 2026 (no purchase deadline is stated; this is the last day the free rest-of-2026 visits can be used); official page checked October 28, 2026.

In short: One per-person price covers unlimited SeaWorld Orlando visits for the rest of 2026 and all of 2027 (Aquatica added in the higher tier), but the offer page shows no child price, and parking and select special events are not included.

For a family of 4: The Fun Card is priced per person: the page shows from USD 144.99 each for SeaWorld only and from USD 184.99 each for SeaWorld + Aquatica, with no separate child price and no age rule, so plan on checking a card price for every family member. Each card is valid only for the person named on it. Parking is not included, so it is extra if you drive. Visits are blocked out on four days around Christmas 2026 and on 19 SeaWorld dates in 2027, including four spring Saturdays; the Aquatica tier also has 15 Aquatica blockout dates from late May to late July 2027.

How we check: every term below was read on the company's own offer page on the date shown, with a link to that page. We have not booked these offers. "For a family of 4" notes are our reading of those terms, not a quote or firsthand experience.

We show a family total only when we observed one on the official site for a named party and named dates. Prices and availability change: check live before you book.

Unclear or missing on the offer page

  • The page shows two prices for each tier (USD 144.99 and USD 151.99; USD 184.99 and USD 242.99) without saying which is the current price; the checkout shows the price you pay.
  • No child price and no free-under age are shown on the offer page.
  • No purchase deadline is shown; the free rest-of-2026 visits cannot run past December 31, 2026, so we list that as the latest date this bonus can matter.
  • Whether the price includes tax is not stated.
  • How automatic renewal works and how to turn it off is not explained on the offer page.

Before you book, check

  • Price a card for each family member at checkout and look for a child price or free-under age there.
  • Compare your planned visit dates with the blockout dates above, especially spring-break Saturdays and Christmas week.
  • Add parking for each visit if you will drive.
  • Check the automatic-renewal setting before you pay.
