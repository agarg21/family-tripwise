What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 6 and 9 (children under 3 do not need a ticket; everyone older needs a pass).

  • Unlimited SeaWorld San Antonio visits through January 2, 2028, and Aquatica San Antonio through October 11, 2027, for each person with a pass.
  • Four passes at $99.99 each, one per person aged 3 or older.
  • Discounts on merchandise, photo prints and Quick Queue; separately ticketed events are extra.
  • Each pass needs photo ID and cannot be refunded or passed on.

Is it a good deal?

  • Buy it if you will go at least three times in 2027; for two back-to-back days, the Two Day Flex Ticket ($68 a person) costs less.
  • Kids 5 and under: SeaWorld's home page offers a free Preschool Fun Card for visits in 2026; it does not say the same for 2027.
  • SeaWorld San Antonio's tickets page (read November 1, 2026) lists a Single-Day Ticket at $53.99 for a chosen date and the Any Day Ticket at $79.99.

The catch

  • Each family member aged 3 or older needs a pass.
  • Parking is not included, and tax and fees are extra.
  • Blockout dates may apply but are not listed, and the page does not say whether 2026 visits are included.

How to get it

  1. Buy on SeaWorld San Antonio's tickets page; no purchase deadline is stated, so the price could change any time.
  2. Price a pass for each family member aged 3 or older at checkout.
  3. Ask about blockout dates and 2026 visits, and add parking if you will drive.

Go to the SeaWorld San Antonio tickets page (terms checked November 1, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • Rescue Jr., a play area for younger kids.
  • Shows with beluga whales and Pacific white-sided dolphins, and orca encounters.
  • Penguin meet-and-greets, in-water dolphin interactions and backstage beluga tours (these may cost extra).
  • Aquatica San Antonio, the water park, is included with this pass through October 11, 2027.

From SeaWorld San Antonio home page, checked November 1, 2026. This is what SeaWorld San Antonio says; we have not visited.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One per-person price covers unlimited visits until January 2, 2028.
  • Aquatica water park visits through October 11, 2027 are included, and children under 3 are free.
  • Less than two single-day tickets ($53.99 each) for unlimited visits.

Cons

  • A family of 4 with kids aged 3 or older buys four passes.
  • Parking, tax and fees are extra, and the pass is non-refundable.
  • Blockout dates may apply, and none are listed.
