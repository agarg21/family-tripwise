Looking for a SeaWorld pass now? SeaWorld passes for a family of 4, park by park.

What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 6 and 9 (children under 3 do not need a ticket; everyone older needs a pass).

  • Unlimited SeaWorld San Antonio visits now through January 2, 2028, and Aquatica San Antonio through October 11, 2027, for each person with a pass.
  • Four passes at $91.99 each, one per person aged 3 or older, with free general parking.
  • 5% off in-park merchandise and photo prints, 10% off Quick Queue, and pass-holder events.
  • Passes cannot be refunded or passed on (pass rules read November 1, 2026).

Is it a good deal?

  • Buy it if you will go at least three times; for two back-to-back days, the Two Day Flex Ticket ($68 a person) costs less.
  • A SeaWorld-only 2027 Season Pass (no Aquatica) is $81.99 a person.
  • SeaWorld San Antonio's tickets page (read November 8, 2026) lists a Single-Day Ticket at $45.99 for a chosen date and the Any Day Ticket at $93.99.

The catch

  • The $91.99 price may end soon: no date is in the page text, but the Black Friday banner file name says Nov 8.
  • Each family member aged 3 or older needs a pass; tax and fees are extra.
  • The card says no blockout dates, but its fine print says they may apply: check before you visit.

What kids can do there

  • Rescue Jr., a play area for younger kids.
  • Shows with beluga whales and Pacific white-sided dolphins, and orca encounters.
  • Penguin meet-and-greets, in-water dolphin interactions and backstage beluga tours (these may cost extra).
  • Aquatica San Antonio, the water park, is included with this pass through October 11, 2027.

From SeaWorld San Antonio home page, checked November 1, 2026. This is what SeaWorld San Antonio says; we have not visited.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One per-person price covers unlimited visits until January 2, 2028, with free general parking.
  • Aquatica water park visits through October 11, 2027 are included, and children under 3 are free.
  • Less than two single-day tickets ($45.99 each) on the cheapest dates for unlimited visits.

Cons

  • A family of 4 with kids aged 3 or older buys four passes.
  • Tax and fees are extra, and the pass is non-refundable.
  • The Presale price may end Nov 8, and blockout wording conflicts.
