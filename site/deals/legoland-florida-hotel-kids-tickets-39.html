What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 5 and 8 (LEGOLAND prices the package for 2 adults and 2 kids ages 2 to 12).

  • One booking covers the hotel night, a hot breakfast each day and multi-park tickets (the package page lists LEGOLAND, the LEGOLAND Water Park, PEPPA PIG Theme Park and SEA LIFE Florida; the ticket type depends on your dates).
  • LEGOLAND's advertised starting price is $162 per person per night for this exact party (1 night, before tax); your dates will price differently.
  • The park tickets are part of the $162 starting price; $39 a day is LEGOLAND's kids' ticket price inside the package (our reading: part of that price, not an extra charge; LEGOLAND does not state its basis). Children under 2 get into LEGOLAND Florida free.
  • If your tickets cover 2 days, the second day must be used within 3 days of the first visit.

Is it a good deal?

  • A small saving at best: about 11% less for one night and two park days, but only if your night comes with a 2-day ticket and LEGOLAND's starting price; with a 1-day ticket the package costs more.
  • LEGOLAND's help page lists 2-day tickets (LEGOLAND and SEA LIFE) from $114 per person online and $174 at the gate (no date shown). The package ticket can also cover the water park and Peppa Pig park, depending on your dates.
  • Our estimate uses a Friday night (December 4). LEGOLAND says you can save up to 20% on Sunday to Thursday stays, but not compared with what, so price a weeknight too.

The catch

  • Non-refundable: you pay in full when you book.
  • Tax, a nightly resort fee and parking are extra, and the offer pages do not say how much the fee and parking are.
  • The two LEGOLAND offer pages disagree on partner hotels, so book a LEGOLAND hotel directly to be sure of the package.

How to get it

  1. Book a LEGOLAND Hotel, Pirate Island Hotel or LEGOLAND Beach Retreat stay on legoland.com by November 17, 2026, for a stay through January 9, 2027.
  2. Enter your real party and kids' ages, and check the kids' ticket price per day and which tickets come with your dates at checkout.
  3. Add parking to the booking if you will drive (otherwise it is collected at check-in), and check park hours for your dates.

Go to the LEGOLAND Florida Hotel + Ticket Package page (terms checked October 30, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • Over 50 rides, shows and attractions at LEGOLAND Theme Park, including The Dragon, Coastersaurus, LEGO NINJAGO The Ride, Lost Kingdom Adventure and MINILAND USA.
  • LEGOLAND Water Park with the Build-A-Raft Lazy River, LEGO Wave Pool and Joker Soaker.
  • PEPPA PIG Theme Park, a separate park steps away with gentle rides and play areas for preschoolers.
  • SEA LIFE Florida, an indoor aquarium with ocean tunnels, and the Cypress Gardens botanical garden.
