LEGOLAND Florida Resort: LEGO and Peppa Pig theme parks for kids 2 to 12 in Winter Haven, about an hour from Orlando

LEGOLAND Florida hotel stay: kids' park tickets from $39 a day (book by November 17)

  • Only for firm dates
  • Book by November 17, 2026
  • $162 a person a night, tickets incl.
  • Non-refundable

AI research contributor: Miles Rowan

$39a day per kid's park ticket in a LEGOLAND Florida hotel package with breakfast (starting price)

Our verdict: Good for families with firm dates who want to sleep at the park with breakfast and multi-park tickets included; skip it if you might cancel (it is non-refundable and paid in full) or want a cheaper hotel off site. Tax, a resort fee and parking are extra.

Best for: Families with kids 2 to 12 and firm dates for a LEGOLAND Florida trip before January 9, 2027 who want to stay at the resort.

Not for: Anyone who may need to cancel (the booking is non-refundable and paid in full up front), or who would rather stay at a cheaper hotel off site or in Orlando.

Saves: kids' park tickets from $39 a day in the package vs $129 per person at the front gate (not the same ticket, so a rough guide, not a fixed saving).

Trip cost: we have no complete trip price for a family of 4. LEGOLAND's starting price is per person, so 2 adults and 2 kids pay four times it for 1 night with park tickets and breakfast, before tax, the resort fee and parking; it is not a quote, so price your dates on legoland.com and add flights from your airport (for example New York City to Orlando, which we have not priced).

What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 5 and 8 (LEGOLAND prices the package for 2 adults and 2 kids ages 2 to 12).

  • One booking covers the hotel night, a hot breakfast each day and multi-park tickets (the package page lists LEGOLAND, the LEGOLAND Water Park, PEPPA PIG Theme Park and SEA LIFE Florida; the ticket type depends on your dates).
  • LEGOLAND's advertised starting price is $162 per person per night for this exact party (1 night, before tax); your dates will price differently.
  • The park tickets are part of the $162 starting price; $39 a day is LEGOLAND's kids' ticket price inside the package (our reading: part of that price, not an extra charge; LEGOLAND does not state its basis). Children under 2 get into LEGOLAND Florida free.
  • The second ticket day must be used within 3 days of the first visit.

Is it a good deal?

  • Worth it if you would stay at the resort anyway and your dates are fixed; otherwise price the same dates with the package against separate tickets plus the hotel you would otherwise use.
  • LEGOLAND Florida's own tickets page says tickets start at $129 at the front gate (no date shown). The package ticket can cover more parks and its $39 basis is not stated, so the two are not like for like.
  • LEGOLAND says you can save up to 20% on Sunday to Thursday stays, but not compared with what, so check a weekday and a weekend night yourself.

The catch

  • Non-refundable: you pay in full when you book.
  • Tax, a nightly resort fee and parking are extra, and the offer pages do not say how much the fee and parking are.
  • The two LEGOLAND offer pages disagree on partner hotels, so book a LEGOLAND hotel directly to be sure of the package.

How to get it

  1. Book a LEGOLAND Hotel, Pirate Island Hotel or LEGOLAND Beach Retreat stay on legoland.com by November 17, 2026, for a stay through January 9, 2027.
  2. Enter your real party and kids' ages, and check the kids' ticket price per day and which tickets come with your dates at checkout.
  3. Add parking to the booking if you will drive (otherwise it is collected at check-in), and check park hours for your dates.

Go to the LEGOLAND Florida Hotel + Ticket Package page (terms checked October 30, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • Over 50 rides, shows and attractions at LEGOLAND Theme Park, including The Dragon, Coastersaurus, LEGO NINJAGO The Ride, Lost Kingdom Adventure and MINILAND USA.
  • LEGOLAND Water Park with the Build-A-Raft Lazy River, LEGO Wave Pool and Joker Soaker.
  • PEPPA PIG Theme Park, a separate park steps away with gentle rides and play areas for preschoolers.
  • SEA LIFE Florida, an indoor aquarium with ocean tunnels, and the Cypress Gardens botanical garden.

From LEGOLAND Florida help page "What attractions are available at LEGOLAND Florida Resort?" (no date shown), checked October 30, 2026. This is what LEGOLAND Florida Resort says; we have not visited.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Hotel, breakfast and multi-park tickets come in one booking.
  • LEGOLAND prices the package for a family of 4, so its starting price is easy to compare.
  • Children under 2 get into LEGOLAND Florida free.

Cons

  • Non-refundable and paid in full at booking.
  • Tax, a resort fee and parking are extra, and the starting price is not a quote for your dates.
  • Book by November 17, 2026; stays end January 9, 2027.
