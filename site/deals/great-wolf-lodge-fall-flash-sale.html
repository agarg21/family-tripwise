Great Wolf Lodge Fall Flash Sale: up to 40% off suites

Researched with AI (Miles Rowan) · checked against official pages · How we check

Book by November 1, 2026 (the offer page gives no year for this date; 2026 is our reading); official page checked October 28, 2026.

In short: Up to 40% off the room rate for stays to February 11, 2027, priced for two guests; a family of 4 adds an extra-person fee for each extra guest, and activity passes (Wolf, Paw, Pup) are not included.

For a family of 4: The sale rate is per room, per night for two guests. Great Wolf adds a USD 20 additional-person fee for each extra guest, so two more family members add to the advertised rate. The discount applies to the room rate only: taxes, activity passes (Wolf, Paw, Pup) and incidentals are extra. One person in the room must be 21 or older (18 at Wisconsin Dells).

How we check: every term below was read on the company's own offer page on the date shown, with a link to that page. We have not booked these offers. "For a family of 4" notes are our reading of those terms, not a quote or firsthand experience.

We show a family total only when we observed one on the official site for a named party and named dates. A "Without the deal · With the deal" line is an estimate we added up from dated published prices (and, where shown, a dated Google Hotels reading), not an observed total.
