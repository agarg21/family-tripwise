Before you buy a pass

  • Count the visits you will really make before the pass ends: one visit is cheaper on a day ticket.
  • Price one pass for each family member who needs a ticket: San Diego and San Antonio let under-3s in free; the Orlando offer page states no age rule.
  • Check parking (extra with the Orlando and San Diego Fun Cards in our checks; included with the San Antonio pass), the blockout dates and any automatic renewal on the official page.
