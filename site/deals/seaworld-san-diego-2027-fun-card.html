SeaWorld San Diego, California: a theme park and marine zoo with dolphins, sharks, penguins, shows and kid rides

SeaWorld San Diego: unlimited visits through 2027 for $77.99 a person

We stopped listing this check after November 8, 2026 (no end date in the page text; the Black Friday banner image file name says Nov 8). The details below are what the official page said when we checked; they are kept for reference only.

Researched with AI (Miles Rowan) · sources linked and dated · How we check

$77.99a person (it was $99.99 on Nov 1): unlimited SeaWorld San Diego visits now through 2027; kids under 3 free

While it ran: from visit 2. Two visits cost $104.99 a person with the Two-Day Ticket; the card is $77.99 and covers every visit.

What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 4 and 8. Children under 3 enter free and need no card.

  • Unlimited SeaWorld San Diego visits for each person with a card, from the day you buy through December 31, 2027.
  • Four cards at $77.99 each, one per person aged 3 or older.
  • Blocked days: May 30, July 3, 4, 10, 17, 24 and 31, and September 5 (no year shown; our reading: 2027).
  • Each card is for the named person only and cannot be refunded or passed on.

Is it a good deal?

  • Buy it if you will go at least twice before the end of 2027: the Two-Day Ticket is $104.99 a person.
  • SeaWorld San Diego's tickets page (read November 8, 2026) lists single-day tickets as low as $59.99 for a chosen date.

The catch

  • The $77.99 Presale price may end Nov 8; check the price in the cart.
  • Each family member aged 3 or older needs a card; parking, tax and fees are extra.
  • Eight dates are blocked out, and the card renews automatically: check that setting before you pay.

What kids can do there

  • Kid-friendly rides such as Tentacle Twirl and Tidepool Twist.
  • Dolphin Point, plus sea lion, penguin and flamingo encounters (some encounters may cost extra).
  • A shark area with sand tiger, blacktip reef and Australian leopard sharks.
  • Sesame Place San Diego is a separate park with its own tickets.

From SeaWorld San Diego home page, checked November 1, 2026. This is what SeaWorld San Diego says; we have not visited.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One per-person price covers unlimited visits until the end of 2027.
  • $77.99 is less than the $104.99 Two-Day Ticket, and children under 3 are free.
  • Visits for the rest of 2026 are included.

Cons

  • A family of 4 with kids aged 3 or older buys four cards.
  • Parking, tax and fees are extra, and the card is non-refundable and renews automatically.
  • Eight blockout dates, mostly summer weekends.
