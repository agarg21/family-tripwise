What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 4 and 8 (children under 3 enter free; everyone older needs a card).

  • Unlimited SeaWorld San Diego visits for each person with a card, from the day you buy through December 31, 2027.
  • Four cards at $99.99 each, one per person aged 3 or older.
  • Blocked days: July 3, 4, 10, 17, 24 and 31, and September 5 (no year shown; our reading: 2027).
  • Each card is for the named person only and cannot be refunded or passed on.

Is it a good deal?

  • Buy it if you will go at least three times before the end of 2027; for two visits, the Two-Day Ticket ($101.99 a person) costs about the same.
  • SeaWorld San Diego's tickets page (read November 1, 2026) lists single-day tickets from $51.99 online and the Any Day Ticket at $117.99.

The catch

  • Each family member aged 3 or older needs a card.
  • Parking is not included, and tax and fees are added at checkout.
  • Seven summer weekend and Labor Day weekend dates are blocked, and the card renews automatically: check that setting before you pay.

How to get it

  1. Buy on SeaWorld San Diego's Fun Card page; no purchase deadline is stated, but the free rest-of-2026 visits end December 31, 2026.
  2. Price a card for each family member aged 3 or older at checkout.
  3. Check your visit dates against the blockout dates, add parking if you will drive, and check the automatic-renewal setting.

Go to the SeaWorld San Diego Fun Card page (terms checked November 1, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • Kid-friendly rides such as Tentacle Twirl and Tidepool Twist.
  • Dolphin Point, plus sea lion, penguin and flamingo encounters (some encounters may cost extra).
  • A shark area with sand tiger, blacktip reef and Australian leopard sharks.
  • Sesame Place San Diego is a separate park with its own tickets.

From SeaWorld San Diego home page, checked November 1, 2026. This is what SeaWorld San Diego says; we have not visited.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One per-person price covers unlimited visits until the end of 2027.
  • The rest of 2026 is included, and children under 3 are free.
  • Less than one Two-Day Ticket ($101.99) for unlimited visits.

Cons

  • A family of 4 with kids aged 3 or older buys four cards.
  • Parking, tax and fees are extra, and the card is non-refundable and renews automatically.
  • Summer weekend and Labor Day weekend blockout dates.
