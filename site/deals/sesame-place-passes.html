Before you buy a pass

  • Count the visits you will really make by January 2, 2028: in our checks, day tickets cost less until the 3rd visit in San Diego (prices seen November 8) and the 7th visit in Philadelphia.
  • Price a pass for each family member who needs one: Philadelphia's passes are for ages 2 and up; San Diego's tickets page lets under-3s in free, but its FAQ says under 2, so check.
  • Check parking (Philadelphia's Bronze pass includes none; Silver lists free general parking at both parks, in Philadelphia based on availability), the blockout dates and, in San Diego, the automatic renewal of full-pay passes.
