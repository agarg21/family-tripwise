Sesame Place San Diego: a 2027 Silver Pass for $121.50 a person, rest of 2026 included

We stopped listing this check after November 8, 2026 (no end date in the page text; the Black Friday banner image file name says Nov 8). The details below are what the official page said when we checked; they are kept for reference only.

Researched with AI (Miles Rowan) · sources linked and dated · How we check

$121.50a person aged 3+ (it was $148.50 on Nov 1): unlimited visits for the rest of 2026 and all of 2027, with free general parking

While it ran: from visit 3. Without the pass, 3 visits cost three Single-Day Tickets from $49.99 a person; with it, $121.50 covers every visit and parking.

What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 3 and 5 (everyone aged 3 and up needs a pass).

  • Unlimited visits to Sesame Place San Diego for the rest of 2026 and all of 2027 (through January 2, 2028), with no blockout dates.
  • Free general parking on every visit, and 2 free Friend Tickets.
  • Pay $121.50 a person in full, or 9 payments of $13.50 at 0% APR.

Is it a good deal?

  • Buy it if you will go three or more times before 2028; for one or two visits, Single-Day Tickets (from $49.99, date-specific) cost less.
  • Only this year: the Seasonal Any-Day Ticket is $49.99 for one visit by December 31, 2026.
  • Gold ($148.50) adds 4 guest tickets instead of 2; Platinum ($231.75) adds preferred parking.

The catch

  • The $121.50 price may end soon: no date is in the page text, but the Black Friday banner file name says Nov 8.
  • Every family member aged 3 and up needs a pass; taxes and fees are extra, and passes are non-refundable.
  • Full-pay passes renew automatically after a year unless you cancel.

What kids can do there

  • Ride Abby's Fairy Flight and watch the Sesame Street Party Parade.
  • Meet Big Bird, Cookie Monster and Elmo, or book Dine with Elmo & Friends.
  • In the water park: slides, a lazy river and a family wave pool.
  • A Very Furry Christmas runs November 7 to January 3.

From Sesame Place San Diego home page, checked November 1, 2026. This is what Sesame Place San Diego says; we have not visited.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Visits for the rest of 2026 come with a 2027 pass.
  • Free general parking and no blockout dates.
  • From the 3rd visit, it costs less than Single-Day Tickets on the cheapest dates.

Cons

  • A family of 4 with kids aged 3 or older buys four passes.
  • Taxes and fees are extra, and passes are non-refundable.
  • The Presale price may end Nov 8, and full-pay passes renew automatically.
