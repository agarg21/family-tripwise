What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 3 and 5 (everyone aged 3 and up needs a pass).

  • Unlimited visits to Sesame Place San Diego for the rest of 2026 and all of 2027 (through January 2, 2028), with no blockout dates.
  • Free general parking on every visit, and 2 free guest tickets per season.
  • Pay $148.50 a person in full, or 9 monthly payments of $16.50 at 0% APR.

Is it a good deal?

  • Buy it if you will go three or more times before 2028; for one or two visits, Single-Day Tickets (from $61.99, date-specific) cost less.
  • Only this year: the Seasonal Any-Day Ticket is $49.99 for one visit by December 31, 2026.
  • Gold ($184.50) adds 4 guest tickets instead of 2; Platinum ($283.50) adds preferred parking.

The catch

  • Every family member aged 3 and up needs a pass; one price is shown for all ages.
  • Taxes and fees are extra, and passes are non-refundable.
  • Full-pay passes renew automatically after a year unless you cancel.

How to get it

  1. Buy on Sesame Place San Diego's Season Pass page; no sale end date is stated, so the price could change.
  2. Buy one Silver Pass for each family member aged 3 and up.
  3. Turn off automatic renewal if you only want 2027.

Go to the Sesame Place San Diego Season Pass page (terms checked November 1, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • Ride Abby's Fairy Flight and watch the Sesame Street Party Parade.
  • Meet Big Bird, Cookie Monster and Elmo, or book Dine with Elmo & Friends.
  • In the water park: slides, a lazy river and a family wave pool.
  • A Very Furry Christmas runs November 7 to January 3.

From Sesame Place San Diego home page, checked November 1, 2026. This is what Sesame Place San Diego says; we have not visited.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Visits for the rest of 2026 come with a 2027 pass.

Cons

  • A family of 4 with kids aged 3 or older buys four passes.
  • Taxes and fees are extra, and passes are non-refundable.
  • No sale end date is stated, and full-pay passes renew automatically.
