Beaches Resorts: all-inclusive family resorts in Negril, Jamaica and in Turks & Caicos (Caribbean)

Beaches: $135 resort credit per child 5 and under with a new passport

  • Only for some families
  • Book by October 31, 2026
  • Travel by November 6, 2026
  • Up to $270 per booking
  • 5+ paid nights

AI research contributor: Miles Rowan

$135resort credit per child 5 or under with a new passport

Our verdict: A credit for resort extras, not a room discount, with a very short booking and travel window.

Best for: Families with a child 5 or under whose passport was issued after August 1, 2025, who can travel by November 6, 2026.

Not for: Anyone who can't start a 5-night stay by November 6, 2026 (the terms don't say whether it must also end by then), or who wants money off the room price.

Saves: up to $270 in resort credit for two eligible kids ($135 each). It pays for extras such as spa, room upgrades or scuba; it does not lower the room price.

Trip cost: we have no checked price for a family of 4 yet, and the offer needs at least 5 paid nights. Price your dates on beaches.com and add flights from your airport (for example New York City).

What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 2 and 5, both with passports issued after August 1, 2025.

  • $270 in resort credit for the booking ($135 per child), given at check-in after the resort sees each child's original passport.
  • With only one eligible child (for example kids aged 4 and 8): $135.
  • Spend it on listed extras: spa (plus a 15% service charge), room upgrades, extra nights, internet upgrades, private dinners, wet suit rental, photos, scuba, laundry or a cabana. No cash value.

Is it a good deal?

  • The room price for your dates is the normal Beaches price; this credit only helps if you would buy the listed extras anyway.
  • A child's passport has its own State Department fees, which this credit does not cover (it is spent only on resort extras); check the current fee on travel.state.gov.

The catch

  • Very short: book by October 31 and travel by November 6, 2026.
  • Only kids 5 and under at check-in with a U.S. or Canadian passport issued after August 1, 2025; at most two per booking.
  • Credit for extras only: not cash and not off the room price. The offer page does not list which resorts take part.

How to get it

  1. Book at least 5 paid nights on beaches.com or with a travel advisor by October 31, 2026, travelling by November 6, 2026 (ask whether the whole stay must end by then).
  2. Register the booking on the Beaches First Passport Offer page. Registering does not guarantee the credit.
  3. Bring each child's original passport to check-in; the resort checks it and adds the credit.

Go to the Beaches First Passport Offer page (terms checked October 28, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • Kids Camp, included in the stay: five age levels from infants to age 12, three sessions between 8 a.m. and 9 p.m. daily.
  • Sesame Street parades, stage shows and weekly activities.
  • Pirates Island water parks: waterslides, lazy rivers, a surf simulator and areas for toddlers.
  • Kids scuba (PADI Bubblemaker and SEAL Team) for ages 8 and up, an arcade, and a Choo Choo train at Turks & Caicos for ages 3 to 10.

From Beaches kids and family vacations page, checked October 29, 2026. This is what Beaches Resorts says; we have not visited.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Real money toward extras if your child qualifies.
  • Kids Camp is included, and the resorts have Sesame Street shows and Pirates Island water parks.
  • Easy to claim: register online, show the passport.

Cons

  • Booking closes October 31 and travel ends November 6, 2026.
  • Only children 5 or under with a passport issued after August 1, 2025 count.
  • No discount on the room; the credit has no cash value.
