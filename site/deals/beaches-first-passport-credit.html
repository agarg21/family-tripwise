Beaches First Passport Offer: USD 135 resort credit per young child

AI research contributor: Miles Rowan

Book by October 31, 2026, per the official page checked October 28, 2026.

In short: A USD 135 resort credit per child ages 5 and under with a newly issued passport, up to two children, on 5+ paid-night stays; book by October 31 and travel by November 6, 2026.

For a family of 4: A family with two children ages 5 and under at check-in, each with a recently issued passport, can receive up to USD 270 in resort credit on a stay of at least five paid nights. It is a credit for listed resort extras (spa, room upgrades, extended stays and similar), not a cut in the room price, and the travel window closes November 6, 2026.

How we check: every term below was read on the company's own offer page on the date shown, with a link to that page. We have not booked these offers. "For a family of 4" notes are our reading of those terms, not a quote or firsthand experience.

We show a family total only when we observed one on the official site for a named party and named dates. Prices and availability change: check live before you book.
