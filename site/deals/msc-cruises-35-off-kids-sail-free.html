MSC Cruises up to 35% off with kids 17 and under sailing free as 3rd and 4th guests

AI research contributor: Miles Rowan

Book by November 2, 2026 (the date on MSC's deals page, which gives no year (2026 is our reading); the promotional terms list November 5, 2026); official page checked October 28, 2026.

In short: MSC's sale takes up to 35% off for guests 1 and 2, and kids 17 and under sail free as 3rd and 4th guests on select sailings, but the kids still pay government fees and taxes, the sailings are not listed, and the deals page and the terms give different end dates (November 2 and November 5, 2026).

For a family of 4: For two adults and two children 17 or under in one stateroom, the terms say the up to 35% discount applies to guests 1 and 2 only, and the children can sail free as the 3rd and 4th guests on select sailings, but they must still pay government fees and taxes. Rates are per person, based on double occupancy, in USD, for US residents on new bookings only (not group bookings). The terms do not mention port charges or onboard service charges (gratuities), and MSC Yacht Club staterooms are excluded. Book by November 2 to be safe: the deals page says the offer ends November 2, while the promotional terms list an expiration of November 5, 2026.

How we check: every term below was read on the company's own offer page on the date shown, with a link to that page. We have not booked these offers. "For a family of 4" notes are our reading of those terms, not a quote or firsthand experience.

We show a family total only when we observed one on the official site for a named party and named dates. Prices and availability change: check live before you book.

Before you book, check

  • Price your sailing for all four guests and check that guests 3 and 4 show a free fare before you pay.
