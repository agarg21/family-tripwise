What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 6 and 8, so four passes.

  • Visits through Labor Day 2027 at Great America and Hurricane Harbor Chicago.
  • Free general parking, per the pass chart.
  • Buy by Nov 30 for a free Bring-A-Friend ticket, use by May 31, 2027.

Is it a good deal?

  • Buy it if you can go twice or more by Labor Day 2027; water park days count too.
  • Ages 3 to 5: Six Flags terms dated July 2026 list a free Pre-K Pass with a paid pass, so you may need fewer passes.
  • Gold at $99 adds all Midwest Six Flags parks and all of 2026 and 2027.

The catch

  • The pass stops at Labor Day 2027, so fall visits and the rest of 2026 are not listed.
  • Silver leaves Fright Fest blank, and extra-fee events are excluded.
  • A fee of up to $9.99 and tax are extra; all sales are final, blackout dates are not stated, and free parking has a footnote we couldn't read.

How to get it

  1. Buy online on the Six Flags Great America Season Passes page by Nov 30.
  2. Pick Silver ($69), not the $169 Silver Bundle, unless you want the meal plan.
  3. For a child aged 3 to 5, check the free Pre-K Pass before paying for a fourth pass.

Go to the Six Flags Great America Season Passes page (terms checked November 6, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • The pass covers Hurricane Harbor Chicago as well as the rides; check water park opening dates.
  • Check the park's height rules and small-kid rides before you buy; we did not read them.
  • Silver lists 10% off food and merchandise, so a snack stop costs a little less.
