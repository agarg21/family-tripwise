What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 5 and 8, so four passes.

  • Visits all of 2027 at Fiesta Texas and Hurricane Harbor San Antonio.
  • Free general parking, per the pass chart.
  • Bring-A-Friend: buy by Nov 30 for a free ticket (use by Jan 4, 2027), but the chart leaves it blank for Silver, so check at checkout.

Is it a good deal?

  • Buy it if you can go three or more times in 2027; water park days count too.
  • A single Good Any Day Ticket is also $69, so at that price the pass costs no more; check blackout dates.
  • Gold at $99 adds all Six Flags parks in Texas and Oklahoma and the rest of 2026, and lists no blackout dates.

The catch

  • No child price or age rule is shown, so check at checkout whether young kids need a pass.
  • Silver lists 2027 visits only, and the chart lists holiday event admission for no pass, so don't count on it for Holiday in the Park.
  • A fee of up to $9.99 and tax are extra; blackout dates are not stated, and free parking has a footnote we couldn't read.

How to get it

  1. Buy online on the Six Flags Fiesta Texas Season Passes page by Nov 30.
  2. Pick Silver ($69), not the $159 Silver Bundle, unless you want the dining plan.
  3. Check the fee, tax and any under-age rule at checkout before you pay.

Go to the Six Flags Fiesta Texas Season Passes page (terms checked November 6, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • Check the park's height rules for small kids before you buy; we did not read them. Holiday in the Park runs on select dates, Nov 21 to Jan 4, with a park ticket: Looney Tunes Christmas show, an elf-led sing-along and Santa's Cottage.
  • S'mores and Santa photos cost extra at the holiday event.
  • The pass also covers Hurricane Harbor San Antonio in 2027; check its opening dates.

From Six Flags Fiesta Texas Holiday in the Park page, checked November 4, 2026. This is what Six Flags Fiesta Texas says; we have not visited.
