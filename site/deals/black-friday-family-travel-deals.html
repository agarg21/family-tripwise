How to check any Black Friday travel deal

  1. Price the same dates twice, with and without the offer, for your real party. "Up to 65% off" says little until you do.
  2. Check who counts. Child ages and the 3rd and 4th guest rules change the family total most.
  3. Add what the headline leaves out: taxes, resort fees, parking and required tips.
  4. Read the book-by and travel dates, including blackout dates around school holidays.
