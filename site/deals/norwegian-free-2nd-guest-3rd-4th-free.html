Norwegian Cruise Line Free 2nd Guest sale and free 3rd and 4th guests on select sailings

AI research contributor: Miles Rowan

Book by November 2, 2026 (this is the Free 2nd Guest booking deadline; the free 3rd and 4th guest offer has no stated end date); official page checked October 28, 2026.

In short: A Free 2nd Guest sale runs to November 2, 2026, and 3rd and 4th guests get a free fare on select sailings, but the terms say the free 3rd and 4th guests need guests 1 and 2 paying full fare, state no child ages and do not say whether the two offers combine.

For a family of 4: For two adults and two children in one stateroom, the Free at Sea terms say guests 3 and 4 get a free fare on select sailings when they share the stateroom with guests 1 and 2 paying full fare, with no deposit for the qualifying 3rd and 4th guests. The separate Free 2nd Guest sale (booking October 27 to November 2, 2026) covers all open sailings (except charters and inaugural sailings), but the terms do not say whether a family can get the free 2nd guest and the free 3rd and 4th guests on the same booking, and the 3rd and 4th guest offer asks for guests 1 and 2 at full fare. The Free 2nd Guest terms say taxes, fees and port expenses are included in the cruise fare for NCL's US and Canada office; the 3rd and 4th guest terms do not say how taxes, fees and port expenses apply to the free guests. Discretionary onboard service charges are extra (Free 2nd Guest terms).

How we check: every term below was read on the company's own offer page on the date shown, with a link to that page. We have not booked these offers. "For a family of 4" notes are our reading of those terms, not a quote or firsthand experience.

We show a family total only when we observed one on the official site for a named party and named dates. Prices and availability change: check live before you book.

Unclear or missing on the offer page

Before you book, check

  • Price your sailing for all four guests and check that guests 3 and 4 show a free fare before you pay.
  • Ask whether the Free 2nd Guest price and the free 3rd and 4th guests apply together on your booking.
  • Check what taxes, fees and port expenses are charged for each child.
  • Add discretionary onboard service charges for every guest before you compare this with other cruise lines.
