Royal Caribbean Kids Sail Free: kids 12 and under pay no cruise fare

AI research contributor: Miles Rowan

Book by November 2, 2026, per the official page checked October 28, 2026.

In short: Kids 12 and under pay no cruise fare on select 3+ night sailings, but every guest still pays taxes, fees and port expenses, and Thanksgiving and Christmas-to-New-Year sailings are excluded.

For a family of 4: Two full-fare guests (for example, two parents) pay full fare; two kids 12 and under in the same stateroom pay USD 0 cruise fare under Kids Sail Free. Taxes, fees and port expenses are still charged for all four, so the kids are not free. The separate Free 3rd and 4th Guests offer works the same way for any 3rd and 4th guest, with no age limit stated in our record.

How we check: every term below was read on the company's own offer page on the date shown, with a link to that page. We have not booked these offers. "For a family of 4" notes are our reading of those terms, not a quote or firsthand experience.

We show a family total only when we observed one on the official site for a named party and named dates. Prices and availability change: check live before you book.
