$0 eachlift tickets for kids 12 and under, December 5 to 11; confirm the limit of one kid per paying adult

Our verdict: Free kid lift tickets if early December suits you, but we could not see adult ticket prices, so check the whole trip before booking.

Best for: Families free to ski December 5 to 11 with kids 12 and under.

Not for: Families who need holiday-week dates, or who ski with more kids than paying adults.

Also note: Kid rentals up to 50% off

Saves: 2 kid lift tickets at $0 each and up to 50% off kid ski rentals; no dollar saving shown without a regular ticket price.

Trip cost: we have no checked price for a family of 4. Price tickets and lodging on deervalley.com; flights from New York City, lessons and rentals are extra.

What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 6 and 9, skiing one or more days from December 5 to 11.

  • A free daily lift ticket for each child 12 and under. Deer Valley's package page and a sponsored TownLift article limit it to one child per paying adult, so confirm.
  • 50% off Tot and Junior ski rentals during the week (the package page says 20%, so ask).
  • The offer covers both online and walk-up tickets, though Deer Valley limits daily sales.

Is it a good deal?

  • Worth it only if your total, with 2 adult tickets, lodging and travel, beats another resort on your dates.
  • Optional package, book by November 11: up to 30% off lodging and lift tickets December 4 to 17, a $100 resort credit, and free airport transfers on 3+ nights.

The catch

  • Likely one free child per paying adult, so a parent alone with 2 kids may still pay for one child.
  • It runs for one week only, and this offer does not discount adult tickets on the pages we read.
  • No ticket price shows on the pages we read, so you have to price it yourself.

How to get it

  1. Pick a ski day from December 5 to 11, 2026 and buy lift tickets on deervalley.com, early, because daily sales are limited.
  2. For lodging, call 800-558-3337 or email lodging@deervalley.com by November 11, 2026 and ask for the Ski, Stay & Save package.
  3. Ask how to book the kid rental discount before you arrive.

Go to the Deer Valley Kids Ski Free page (terms checked November 9, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • Ski school with private lessons, group lessons and clinics for all levels.
  • Children's Center for non-skiers aged 2 months to 12 years, with art, stories and indoor and outdoor playgrounds; $215 a day at the regular rate for ages 3 to 12, reservations required.
  • Some family lodging includes breakfast and access to heated outdoor pools.

From Deer Valley Children's Center and winter pages, checked November 2, 2026. This is what Deer Valley Resort says; we have not visited.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Both kids' lift tickets should be free with 2 paying adults.
  • Kid rentals are discounted too.
  • Works for online and walk-up tickets.

Cons

  • Only one week, December 5 to 11.
  • No ticket price shows on the pages we read.
  • Lodging, flights and lessons are not covered.
