LEGOLAND New York, in Goshen, NY, about 60 miles from New York City: a LEGO theme park and hotel for kids aged 2 to 12

LEGOLAND New York: a 2027 season pass from $99 a person, with the rest of 2026 included

  • Only for 3+ visits
  • Listed until November 6, 2026
  • Pays off from visit 3
  • 60 miles from NYC
  • Standard: 52 blockout dates
  • Preferred $129 adds parking

AI research contributor: Miles Rowan

$99a person, ages 2+ (regular $119): rest of 2026 and all of 2027 on the Standard pass

Our verdict: Two of the cheapest online day tickets ($49 each) cost about the same as one $99 Standard pass, so the pass pays off from the 3rd visit; for one or two visits, day tickets cost the same or less.

Best for: Families within a drive of Goshen, New York, with LEGO fans aged about 2 to 12 who will go three or more times. Driving most visits or going on busy days? Preferred ($129) adds free standard parking and has no blockout dates.

Not for: One or two visits: LEGOLAND New York sells one-day tickets online from $49 a person on the cheapest dates, so two visits can cost about the same as a pass.

Saves: from your 3rd visit, one $99 Standard pass costs less than three online day tickets (from $49 each on the cheapest dates); the pass is down from $119.

Trip cost: we have no checked price for a family of 4; plan on one pass for everyone aged 2 and up, plus parking on the Standard pass (price not shown) and your drive from New York City (about 60 miles).

What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 4 and 7, so four passes (everyone aged 2 and up needs one).

  • Visits at LEGOLAND New York from the day you buy through January 2, 2028, on the days the park is open; it closes for the season on January 3, 2027.
  • The rest of 2026 includes Holiday Bricktacular (November 27, 2026 to January 2, 2027), with holiday LEGO builds, a dance party and family skating on a synthetic (Glice) rink.
  • Standard ($99) has 52 blockout dates; Preferred ($129) has none listed and adds free standard parking; Premium ($219) adds 4 Bring-A-Friend tickets and PEPPA PIG Theme Parks.

Is it a good deal?

  • Buy it if you will go three or more times before 2028 (our estimate, using the lowest online day-ticket price); for one or two visits, online day tickets (from $49 on the cheapest dates) can cost the same or less.
  • The front gate price for one day is $99 a person, the same as a Standard pass.
  • If you drive, Preferred ($129) includes free standard parking, which Standard does not.

The catch

  • The Standard pass has 52 blockout dates and no free parking, and the pass page shows neither the dates nor the parking price: check both before you buy, and if you will drive most visits, compare Preferred ($129).
  • From now to December the park opens on weekends and select weekdays only, and four rides close from November 2.
  • No sale end date is stated, and passes cannot be refunded or shared.

How to get it

  1. Buy online on LEGOLAND New York's Season Passes page; no sale end date is stated.
  2. Pick Standard ($99) if your dates are not blocked, or Preferred ($129) for free standard parking and no blockout dates.
  3. Buy one pass for each family member aged 2 and up; you can add the digital passes to the LEGOLAND app wallet, and they are scanned at entry.

Go to the LEGOLAND New York Season Passes page (terms checked October 30, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • Holiday Bricktacular runs November 27, 2026 to January 2, 2027, with holiday LEGO builds, a holiday dance party and family skating on a synthetic (Glice) rink.
  • In 2027, Red, White & BOOM! on July 4 adds fireworks seen through 3D glasses, and a new LEGO Festival is listed as coming.
  • The resort lists more than 50 rides, attractions and seasonal events, and the LEGOLAND Hotel is steps from the park entrance.

From LEGOLAND New York seasonal events page, checked October 30, 2026. This is what LEGOLAND New York Resort says; we have not visited.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • About 60 miles from New York City, so it works as a day trip.

Cons

  • Standard has 52 blockout dates and no free parking.
  • Every family member aged 2 and up needs a pass, with no child price.
  • No sale end date is stated, and passes are non-refundable.
