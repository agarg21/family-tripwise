LEGOLAND California hotel package: kids' tickets from USD 39 a day

AI research contributor: Miles Rowan

Book by November 17, 2026, per the official page checked October 29, 2026.

In short: Book a LEGOLAND Hotel or LEGOLAND Castle Hotel stay by November 17, 2026 for stays through January 9, 2027, with Park Hopper tickets, daily hot breakfast and early park access; advertised from USD 169 per person per night for a family of four before tax, but reservations are non-refundable and parking is extra.

For a family of 4: LEGOLAND's advertised pricing is based on exactly this party: 2 adults and 2 children ages 2 to 12 for a 1-night hotel stay, before tax. The deals page shows a starting price of USD 169 per person per night and kids' tickets from USD 39 per day; these are advertised starting prices, not a quote for your dates. The package includes a Park Hopper ticket (LEGOLAND Theme Park and SEA LIFE Aquarium; multi-day visits must be used within 3 days of the first visit), a daily hot buffet breakfast and early park access. Parking is charged on arrival unless pre-purchased, reservations are non-refundable and full payment is due at booking.

How we check: every term below was read on the company's own offer page on the date shown, with a link to that page. We have not booked these offers. "For a family of 4" notes are our reading of those terms, not a quote or firsthand experience.

We show a family total only when we observed one on the official site for a named party and named dates. Prices and availability change: check live before you book.

Unclear or missing on the offer page

  • How many ticket days come with each night of the stay is not stated.
  • Partner hotels conflict: the package page lists Hopper tickets, early access and breakfast at select partner hotels, while the kids' tickets offer page says the hotel offer is not valid for partner hotels.
  • No blockout dates are listed; LEGOLAND says dates are subject to availability and blockout dates may be added at any time.
  • No minimum stay is stated, and the early park access start time is not given.
  • The basis of the from-USD 39 per day kids' ticket price inside the package (ticket type, party, number of days) is not stated.
  • A separate ticket-only kids' offer (from USD 39 with an adult Hopper ticket) ends November 1, 2026; this check covers only the hotel package.

Before you book, check

  • Price your exact dates for your real party and children's ages, and check the kids' ticket price per day at checkout.
  • Add tax and parking to the quoted price.
  • Book the LEGOLAND Hotel or LEGOLAND Castle Hotel directly if you want the package benefits; partner-hotel terms conflict.
  • Check for Wednesday park closures on your dates.
  • Book only when your dates are firm: reservations are non-refundable.
