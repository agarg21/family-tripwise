$39a day per kid's park ticket when you stay at a LEGOLAND hotel (starting price)

Our verdict: Good for families with firm dates who want to sleep at the park; skip it if you might cancel (it is non-refundable and paid in full) or want a cheaper hotel off site. Tax and parking are extra.

Best for: Families with kids 2 to 12 and firm dates for a LEGOLAND California trip before January 9, 2027 who want to stay at the park.

Not for: Anyone who may need to cancel (the booking is non-refundable and paid in full up front), or who would rather stay at a cheaper hotel off site.

Saves: kids' park tickets from $39 a day in the package (regular 1-day tickets from $69 online), but the tickets differ, so treat it as a guide, not a fixed saving.

Trip cost: we have no checked price for a family of 4 on named dates. LEGOLAND's starting price above is for exactly 2 adults and 2 kids for 1 night; price your dates on legoland.com, then add tax, parking and flights from your airport (for example New York City to San Diego, which we have not priced).

What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 4 and 8 (LEGOLAND prices the package for 2 adults and 2 kids ages 2 to 12).

  • One booking covers the hotel night, Park Hopper tickets for LEGOLAND and the SEA LIFE Aquarium, a hot buffet breakfast each day and early park access.
  • LEGOLAND's advertised starting price is $169 per person per night for this exact party (1 night, before tax); your dates will price differently.
  • Kids' park tickets from $39 a day inside the package; children under 2 get into the park free.
  • Multi-day park visits must be used within 3 days of the first visit.

Is it a good deal?

  • Worth it if you would stay at the park anyway and your dates are fixed; otherwise price your dates at a LEGOLAND hotel with the package against separate tickets plus the hotel you would otherwise use.
  • LEGOLAND's own help page lists 1-day tickets from $69 per person online and $139 at the gate (no date shown). The package ticket is a Park Hopper and the $39 basis is not stated, so the two are not like for like.
  • The package page does not say how many ticket days come with each night, so price the same dates with and without the hotel before you pay.

The catch

  • Non-refundable: you pay in full when you book.
  • Tax and parking are extra, and the park is closed on Wednesdays in some seasons.
  • The two LEGOLAND offer pages disagree on partner hotels, so book the LEGOLAND Hotel or LEGOLAND Castle Hotel directly to be sure of the package.

How to get it

  1. Book a LEGOLAND Hotel or LEGOLAND Castle Hotel stay on legoland.com by November 17, 2026, for a stay through January 9, 2027.
  2. Enter your real party and kids' ages, and check the kids' ticket price per day at checkout.
  3. Add parking to the booking if you will drive (otherwise it is charged on arrival), and check for Wednesday closures on your dates.

Go to the LEGOLAND California Hotel + Ticket Package page (terms checked October 29, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • More than 60 rides, shows and attractions, designed for kids ages 2 to 12.
  • Theme park rides and areas such as The Dragon coaster, Driving School, Dino Valley, LEGO Galaxy, Miniland USA and THE LEGO MOVIE WORLD.
  • LEGOLAND Water Park with Build-A-Raft River and Pirate Reef (the package terms list only the theme park and SEA LIFE).
  • SEA LIFE Aquarium, included in the package's Park Hopper ticket.

From LEGOLAND California Things to Do page, checked October 30, 2026. This is what LEGOLAND California Resort says; we have not visited.
