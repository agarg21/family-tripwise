What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 3 and 6, so four passes (everyone aged 2 and up needs one).

  • Unlimited visits at Sesame Place Philadelphia from the day you buy through January 2, 2028, including the holiday event A Very Furry Christmas and next year's Halloween event.
  • Bronze ($99.99) has four blocked days, all October 2027 weekends; Silver ($175.50) has none listed and adds free general parking plus 2 free guest tickets.
  • You can pay in 9 monthly payments ($11.11 for Bronze) at 0% APR, with the first payment due when you buy.

Is it a good deal?

  • Buy it if you will go four or more times before 2028; for one to three visits on the cheapest dates, one-day tickets ($31.99 online, date-specific) cost less.
  • Sesame Place's gate price for one day is $110.99, so a single walk-up visit costs more than a Bronze pass.
  • If you drive, Silver ($175.50) includes free general parking, which Bronze does not.

The catch

  • The cheapest Bronze pass does not include parking, and the pass page does not say what parking costs.
  • No sale end date is stated, the passes cannot be refunded or shared, and taxes and fees are extra.
  • The park opens on select dates only, so check the calendar before planning visits.

How to get it

  1. Buy online on Sesame Place Philadelphia's Season Pass page; no sale end date is stated.
  2. Pick Bronze ($99.99) if you will not drive often, or Silver ($175.50) for free general parking and no blockout dates.
  3. Buy one pass for each family member aged 2 and up, then enroll each person's photo for entry at your first visit.

Go to the Sesame Place Philadelphia Season Pass page (terms checked October 30, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • A Very Furry Christmas runs on select dates from November 14 to January 3, with Santa, holiday shows, Hanukkah and Kwanzaa celebrations and New Year's Eve fireworks.
  • Meet Sesame Street characters such as Elmo, Abby and the Count, and watch the shows and parades.
  • In summer, Sesame Summer Splash has water play areas and a six-story family raft ride.
  • Elmo's Springtacular adds music, magic and pirate adventures on spring weekends.
