Atlantis Bahamas Fall Into Paradise: up to 35% off 4+ nights, USD 250 resort credit

AI research contributor: Miles Rowan

Book by November 22, 2026 (the offer page gives no year for this date; 2026 is our reading); official page checked October 29, 2026.

In short: Book a 4+ night Atlantis stay by November 22 for travel through January 31, 2027 and save up to 35%, with a USD 250 per-room resort credit, daily breakfast for two and kids 5 and under eating free at select venues; Thanksgiving week and mid-December to January 1 are blacked out, and a 21% charge, a daily resort fee and mandatory gratuities are added.

For a family of 4: For 2 adults and 2 children, the free daily buffet breakfast covers two people only (a folio credit valued up to USD 100 a day), so the children's breakfasts are extra unless a child is 5 or under and eats a kids' entrée free at a participating venue alongside an adult ordering a full-priced entrée (the Kids Eat Free page lists guests of The Coral, The Royal, The Reef and The Cove; Harborside is not listed). The USD 250 resort credit is per room, per stay and can pay for Atlantis Kids Adventures, Dolphin & Marine Adventures or Atlantis food and beverage outlets, but not taxes, gratuities or retail. Atlantis lists mandatory gratuities per person, per night for guests 12 and older, so the amount depends on your children's ages. "Up to 35%" is a maximum; the page does not say which rooms or dates get it.

How we check: every term below was read on the company's own offer page on the date shown, with a link to that page. We have not booked these offers. "For a family of 4" notes are our reading of those terms, not a quote or firsthand experience.

We show a family total only when we observed one on the official site for a named party and named dates. Prices and availability change: check live before you book.

Unclear or missing on the offer page

  • The booking deadline has no year; we read it as November 22, 2026, because travel runs to January 31, 2027.
  • Which room types, hotels or dates get the full 35% is not stated; "up to" is a maximum.
  • The December blackout start conflicts on the page: December 15 in one place, December 16 in another.
  • Whether the resort fee is charged per room or per person is not stated in the lines we read, and cancellation and deposit rules sit in general terms we did not read.
  • The footnote to "Kids 5 & Under Eat Free at Select Participating Venues" is not shown on the offer page; the separate Kids Eat Free page gives the adult-entrée condition.

Before you book, check

  • Price your exact dates for your real party and room on the official site, and confirm how much of the 35% your dates actually get.
  • Avoid November 21 to 27 and December 15 to January 1, or confirm the blackout dates directly.
  • Add the 21% charge, the daily resort fee and mandatory gratuities for everyone 12 and older to the quoted price.
  • Budget breakfast for the children: the free breakfast covers two people.
  • Read the cancellation and deposit terms before you pay.
