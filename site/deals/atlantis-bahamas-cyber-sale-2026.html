Researched with AI (Miles Rowan) · sources linked and dated · How we check

30%off at most on 4+ nights, plus a $250 resort credit for your room from 5 nights ($500 at 10+)

Our verdict: Worth a look only for 5+ nights at Atlantis: the credit can pay for the kids club, but no regular rate is shown and fees and 21% tax add up.

Best for: Families planning 5 or more nights at Atlantis in early December or in 2027 outside mid-February to late March, who would use the kids club or dolphin activities.

Not for: Families wanting all-inclusive, a 2 or 3 night stay, or a trip from December 28 to January 1 or February 12 to March 28, 2027.

Also note: From $77.00/day resort fee + 21% tax

Saves: up to 30% off the room on 4+ nights (a maximum), plus one room credit: $250 for 5 nights, $300 for 6, $350 for 7 to 9, $500 for 10+.

Trip cost: we have no checked price for a family of 4. Fees, tax, meals and flights (for example from New York City) are extra.

What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 4 and 8, in one room for 5 nights at The Coral.

  • Up to 30% off the room rate at The Coral, The Royal, The Reef or Harborside; the page does not say which rooms or dates get the full 30%.
  • One $250 credit for the room on a 5-night stay, usable at the kids club (Atlantis Kids Adventures), Dolphin & Marine Adventures or Atlantis restaurants; the credit has no cash value.
  • "Kids 5 & Under Eat Free" is listed for the 4-year-old, but the offer page gives no conditions.

Is it a good deal?

  • Added at The Coral: a $77.00 daily resort fee (per room or per person is not stated), tips up to $14 per person a night for guests 12+, and 21% tax on the room.
  • Atlantis's earlier Fall Into Paradise sale (ended Nov 22) listed up to 35% off and a $250 credit from 4 nights; this sale's top discount is lower.
  • No regular rate seen: price your dates with and without the sale on the Atlantis site. The credit doesn't pay fees.

The catch

  • The credit table starts at 5 nights; a 4-night stay gets the discount only.
  • Not valid December 28, 2026 to January 1, 2027 or February 12 to March 28, 2027.
  • 21% tax and levies, the daily resort fee and gratuities for everyone 12 and older are added to the room price.

How to get it

  1. Book a new reservation of 4 or more nights at The Coral, The Royal, The Reef or Harborside by December 8 (The Cove has its own up-to-20% offer).
  2. Stay December 1 to 27, 2026 or January 3 to November 15, 2027, avoiding the blackout dates; all nights must fall in one period.
  3. Before you pay, compare the price with and without the sale and read the cancellation and deposit terms.

Go to the Atlantis Exclusive Cyber Sale offer page (terms checked November 23, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • Aquaventure, the resort's water park, with the Leap of Faith slide through a shark-filled lagoon.
  • Poseidon's Playzone, a play area for kids under 54 inches tall.
  • Atlantis Kids Adventures, a kids club for ages 3 to 12 with cooking classes, crafts, treasure hunts and outdoor games.
  • Dolphin Cay and open-air marine habitats with more than 250 marine species.

From Atlantis family-friendly resort page, checked October 30, 2026. This is what Atlantis Paradise Island says; we have not visited.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The resort credit can pay for the kids club or a dolphin or marine activity.
  • Stays can run to November 15, 2027, so summer 2027 trips qualify (book by Dec 8).
  • Kids 5 and under are listed as eating free.

Cons

  • 21% tax and levies, a daily resort fee and gratuities for guests 12 and older are added.
  • Christmas to New Year and mid-February to late March 2027 are blacked out.
  • "Up to 30%" is a maximum, and no regular rate is shown to compare.
