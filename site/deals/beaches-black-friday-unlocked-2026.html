What a family of 4 gets

Is it a good deal?

  • "Rack rate" means a resort's list price, so a percent off it does not tell you your total.
  • Compare the final price on your dates with another all-inclusive, not the percent.

The catch

  • Every figure says "up to", and the sale's own tiers are not shown.
  • A separate listing gives $1,000 for 7+ paid nights and $1,400 for 10+, so ask what your stay gets.
  • The pages we read show no family price, travel dates or blackout dates.

How to get it

  1. Price your dates on beaches.com, or ask a travel advisor, by December 1, 2026.

What kids can do there

  • Kids Camp, included in the stay: five age levels from infants to age 12, three sessions between 8 a.m. and 9 p.m. daily.
  • Sesame Street parades, stage shows and weekly activities.
  • Pirates Island water parks: waterslides, lazy rivers, a surf simulator and areas for toddlers.
  • Kids scuba (PADI Bubblemaker and SEAL Team) for ages 8 and up, an arcade, and a Choo Choo train at Turks & Caicos for ages 3 to 10.

From Beaches kids and family vacations page, checked October 29, 2026. This is what Beaches Resorts says; we have not visited.
