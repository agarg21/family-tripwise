Margaritaville at Sea Black Friday Early Access: 3rd and 4th guests sail free

AI research contributor: Miles Rowan

Book by November 3, 2026, per the official page checked October 28, 2026.

In short: 65% off cruise fares and a USD 0 fare for the 3rd and 4th guests in a stateroom on select sailings, but taxes, fees and port expenses still apply to every guest and the page states no child age terms or sailing dates.

For a family of 4: For two adults and two children in one stateroom, the page says the 3rd and 4th guests pay a USD 0 cruise fare when at least two paying guests share the stateroom, on select sailing dates that can change without notice. Taxes, fees and port expenses are still charged for all four guests, and onboard charges are not included. The 65% off applies to cruise fares for guests in the reservation party; the page says offers combine unless otherwise stated, but it does not show how the 65% and the free 3rd and 4th guest fares work together on one booking. A reduced USD 50 per person deposit is offered on select sailings, with the balance due on the sailing's final payment date.

How we check: every term below was read on the company's own offer page on the date shown, with a link to that page. We have not booked these offers. "For a family of 4" notes are our reading of those terms, not a quote or firsthand experience.

We show a family total only when we observed one on the official site for a named party and named dates. Prices and availability change: check live before you book.

Before you book, check

  • Price your exact sailing for all four guests and check that the 3rd and 4th guest fares show as USD 0 at checkout.
  • Add taxes, fees and port expenses for every guest, including the children, to the quoted fare.
  • Ask about gratuities and onboard charges before you compare this with other cruise lines.
  • Read the cancellation policy before paying the USD 50 deposit.
