Best for: Families whose kids are all under 4, going to a Club Med resort in the Caribbean, Mexico or Canada by July 2, 2027.

Saves: kids under 4 stay free, plus up to $300 per person per week off and up to $300 air credit if you book flights with Club Med. The page says up to 40% off; footnoted conditions apply.

Trip cost: we have no checked price for a family of 4 yet, and kids 4 and up pay. Price your dates on clubmed.us with every child's age entered, and add flights from your airport (for example New York City).

What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 2 and 3.

  • Both kids (2 and 3) are shown as staying free; Petit Club Med, the club for ages 2 to 3, costs extra and the price varies by resort.

  • Up to $300 off per person per week, and up to $300 air credit when flights are booked with Club Med (both have footnoted conditions).

Is it a good deal?

  • The offer page shows no regular price to measure "up to 40%" against, so compare the Club Med quote for your week, with every child's age entered, against other all-inclusive family resorts for the same dates.
  • Mini Club Med+ (ages 4 to 10) runs at Punta Cana, Michès, Cancún and Québec Charlevoix, but its page does not say whether it is in the price; ask before you compare.

The catch

  • Only kids under 4 are shown as free; kids 4 and up pay.
  • The conditions behind the * and ** marks were not readable on the offer page, so minimum stays, blackout dates and fees are unknown.
  • The page does not list which resorts qualify, and the toddler club (ages 2 to 3) costs extra.

How to get it

  1. Book by January 6, 2027 for travel by July 2, 2027 at a Club Med resort in the Caribbean, Canada or Mexico.
  2. Price your exact dates on clubmed.us with every child's age entered, and ask for the full offer terms behind the asterisks.
  3. Before you pay, ask about per-child fees, minimum stays and kids club prices at your resort.

What kids can do there

  • Mini Club Med+ for ages 4 to 10, run by Club Med staff, at Punta Cana, Michès Playa Esmeralda, Cancún and Québec Charlevoix.
  • Mini Club classics: circus lessons, flying trapeze, archery, mini tennis, team sports and the Mini Club Med Show.
  • Newer games such as Nature Detective (clue hunts about the resort's plants and animals) and Ninja Bootcamp (kids design their own obstacle course).
