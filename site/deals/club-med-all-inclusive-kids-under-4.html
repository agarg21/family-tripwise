In short: Book by January 6, 2027 and travel by July 2, 2027 in the Caribbean, Canada or Mexico; only children under 4 are shown as free, and the conditions behind the asterisks were not readable on the offer page.

For a family of 4: Only children under 4 are shown as staying free. Children 4 and older pay a price the offer page does not show, so a family with two school-age kids should not expect free child stays.
