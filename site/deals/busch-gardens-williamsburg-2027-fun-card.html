Our verdict: For three days, a $110 Three-Day ticket costs a little less than the $113.99 card; from the fourth day, the card costs less.

Best for: Families within a drive of Williamsburg, Virginia who will spend four or more days at Busch Gardens or Water Country USA between February and September 2027.

Not for: Three days or fewer: a $110 Three-Day ticket (3 days) or single-day tickets from $39.99 (1 or 2 days, cheapest dates) cost less. Christmas Town visits are not included.

What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 4 and 8 (cards are for ages 3 and up with no child price, so each of you needs one). Under 3: the card page states no rule; the tickets page mentions a Free Preschool Pass without details, so check.

  • Unlimited Busch Gardens Williamsburg visits from February 12 to September 6, 2027, for each person with a card.
  • Water Country USA visits from May 8 to September 19, 2027 on the 2-Park card, which costs the same $113.99 as the Busch Gardens-only card.
  • The Food & Wine Festival, Bier Fest and the free concert series are included with the 2027 card, per Busch Gardens; Christmas Town is not included.
  • Each card cannot be refunded or passed on.

Is it a good deal?

  • Buy it if you will go on four or more days between February and September 2027.
  • For exactly three days, four $110 Three-Day tickets cost $440 against $456 in cards; all three ticket days must fall within 7 days of the first.
  • For one or two days, single-day tickets from $39.99 on the cheapest dates cost far less; the regular price is $121.99.

The catch

  • No end date is posted, so the $113.99 price could change any time (banner file names say "soon", with no date).
  • Visits run only February to September 2027: no Christmas Town, and nothing for the rest of 2026.
  • Parking and tax are extra, and no child price is shown (ages 3 and up need a card).

How to get it

  1. Buy online on Busch Gardens Williamsburg's Fun Cards page or Limited-Time Offers page.
  2. Choose the 2-Park card (Busch Gardens + Water Country USA): same $113.99 as the Busch Gardens-only card.
  3. Add a card for each family member aged 3 and up, add parking if you will drive, and check in the cart whether the card renews.

Go to the Busch Gardens Williamsburg Fun Cards page (terms checked November 20, 2026).

Comparing parks? See Busch Gardens passes for a family of 4, park by park.

What kids can do there

  • Nine European-themed villages, with rides such as the Busch Gardens Railroad.
  • Big coasters such as Verbolten, Griffon and Alpengeist for older kids and teens (check height rules on Busch Gardens' site); Return to Corkscrew Hill is listed for 2027.
  • Water Country USA, with rides such as Cutback Water Coaster and Vanish Point, May 8 to September 19, 2027 on the 2-Park card.
  • Food & Wine Festival, Bier Fest and free concerts are included with the 2027 card, per Busch Gardens.

From Busch Gardens Williamsburg "10 reasons to get a Fun Card" page, checked November 14, 2026. This is what Busch Gardens Williamsburg says; we have not visited.

Cons
