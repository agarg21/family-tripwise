What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 4 and 8 (the offer page shows no child price, so each of you likely needs a card; Busch Gardens says kids 2 and under need no ticket).

  • Unlimited Busch Gardens Tampa Bay visits for each person with a card, from the day you buy through December 31, 2027.
  • Adventure Island water park visits through October 24, 2027, only on the Busch Gardens + Adventure Island card, $118.99 a person.
  • Blocked days in 2027: 8 at Busch Gardens (including March 12 and 27 and July 4) and 7 days at Adventure Island from May 30 to July 24; no 2026 blockout dates are listed.
  • Each card cannot be refunded or passed on, and it renews automatically.

Is it a good deal?

  • Buy it if you will go on two or more days before the end of 2027; for one day, a day ticket (from $71.99 online, date-specific) costs less.
  • Want one day each at SeaWorld Orlando and Busch Gardens? The $93.99 Two Park ticket costs about the same as one card, but the second visit must be within 14 days of the first.
  • Busch Gardens' Fun Card page still showed $129.99 on Nov 6, while its tickets and offers pages showed $89.99. Confirm the price in the cart.

The catch

  • The $89.99 price may end soon: no date is in the page text, but a promo file name on the offers page says Nov 8.
  • No child price is shown, so each family member aged 3 and up likely needs their own card.
  • Parking is not included (from $34, per the FAQ), tax and service fees are extra, and the card renews automatically: check that setting before you pay.

How to get it

  1. Buy online in advance on Busch Gardens' offers page; the $89.99 price is not sold at the park.
  2. Choose the Busch Gardens card, or the $118.99 card if you want Adventure Island too.
  3. Price a card for each family member in the cart, check your dates against the 2027 blockout dates (Busch Gardens: March 12 and 27, July 4 and others in Full terms), add parking, and check the automatic-renewal setting.

Go to the Busch Gardens Tampa Bay Limited-Time Offers page (terms checked November 6, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • For younger kids: Wild Oasis has interactive play areas.
  • See more than 200 animal species, including lions, tigers, giraffes and gorillas, and the lions and hyenas at Lion & Hyena Ridge.
  • Big coasters such as Iron Gwazi, Cheetah Hunt, SheiKra and Montu for older kids and teens (check height rules on Busch Gardens' site).
  • Christmas Town runs on select nights from November 13 to January 4, 2027; Busch Gardens lists it among the events a Fun Card gets you into.

From Busch Gardens Tampa Bay "10 reasons to get a Fun Card" page, checked October 30, 2026. This is what Busch Gardens Tampa Bay says; we have not visited.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • One per-person price covers unlimited visits until the end of 2027.
  • At $89.99 a card costs little more than one $71.99 day ticket.
  • Rest of 2026 is included, with Christmas Town on select nights.

Cons

  • No child price is shown, so a family of 4 buys a card for everyone aged 3 and up.
  • Parking, tax and service fees are extra, and the card is non-refundable and renews automatically.
  • Adventure Island costs extra at this price, and blockout dates include July 4 and spring dates in March 2027.
