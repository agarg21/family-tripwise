Before you buy a pass

  • Count the visits you will really make before the pass ends: in our checks, day tickets cost less until the 3rd visit in New York, the 3rd visit in California and the 3rd visit in Florida.
  • Price a pass for each family member aged 2 and up: day tickets are needed from age 2 at all three parks; New York's pass page says ages 2+ need a pass, and the California and Florida pass pages state no age rule.
  • Check parking (no Standard pass includes it; Preferred includes free standard parking at all three parks), the Standard blockout calendar (52 dates in New York, 86 in California, 83 in Florida) and any tax at checkout.
