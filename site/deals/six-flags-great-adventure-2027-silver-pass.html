What a family of 4 gets

Example family of 4: 2 adults and 2 kids aged 5 and 8, so four passes.

  • Visits through Labor Day 2027 at Great Adventure, Wild Safari and Hurricane Harbor New Jersey.
  • Free general parking, and 10% off food and merchandise, per the pass chart.
  • Bring-A-Friend: the page says buy by Nov 30 for a free ticket (use by Jan 3, 2027), but the chart leaves it blank for Silver, so check at checkout.

Is it a good deal?

  • Buy it if you can go three or more times by Labor Day 2027, or want water park days too.
  • A single Good Any Day Ticket is also $79, so at that price the pass costs no more; check blackout dates.
  • Gold at $105 adds all East Coast Six Flags parks and visits through all of 2026 and 2027.

The catch

  • No child price or age rule is shown, so check at checkout whether young kids need a pass.
  • The chart marks holiday admission "2026 Only" with a footnote we couldn't read, and no pass start date is stated. Check before counting on it.
  • A fee of up to $9.99 and tax are extra; blackout dates are not stated, and free parking has a footnote we couldn't read.

How to get it

  1. Buy online on the Six Flags Great Adventure Season Passes page by Nov 30.
  2. Pick Silver ($79), not the $179 Silver Bundle, unless you want the dining plan.
  3. Check the fee, tax and any under-age rule at checkout before you pay.

Go to the Six Flags Great Adventure Season Passes page (terms checked November 6, 2026).

What kids can do there

  • Holiday in the Park runs on select dates, Nov 21 to Jan 3 (check that your pass covers it): visit Santa at St. Nick's Pics; check whether photos cost extra.
  • Snowflake and Tinsel's snow party is part of the same holiday event.
  • The pass also covers Hurricane Harbor New Jersey and the Wild Safari; check their opening dates.

From Six Flags Great Adventure Holiday in the Park page, checked November 3, 2026. This is what Six Flags Great Adventure says; we have not visited.

Cons

  • Silver stops at Labor Day 2027; Gold and Prestige run through all of 2027.
  • No child price or age rule is shown.
  • Fee and tax are extra; blackout dates are not stated.
