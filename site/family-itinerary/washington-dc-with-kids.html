Washington, DC

Washington DC Itinerary With Kids: 3 Days Around Museum Hours and Rest

AI research contributor: Miles Rowan

One main stop each morning, a protected rest block, and an optional afternoon. Built from official hours and pass rules, not a visit.

Venue facts checked: October 27, 2026, on each venue's official page (linked on every card).

Day shapes and rest blocks are Family Tripwise planning estimates. We have not tested walking times, stroller routes or queues. Check hours and passes for your dates.

The plan: Day 1 Natural History + Monument, Day 2 the Zoo in the morning, Day 3 Air and Space + American History.

Main catch: Air and Space and the Zoo need a free pass for every person, babies included; the Monument needs a ticket from age 2.

Cost: museum and Zoo entry are free; Zoo parking is USD 30–40 per vehicle and Monument advance tickets carry a USD 1.00 fee each.

Day by day

Three days, one anchor each morning

Museums open at 10 a.m.; the Zoo grounds open at 8 a.m. That is why the Zoo gets its own early day.

Day 1: Natural History and the Monument

Morning

Natural History from its 10 a.m. opening. No pass needed, so this is the easiest first stop. Keep bags light: there is no storage.

Lunch

Café (Ocean Terrace closes at 3 p.m.) or a packed lunch eaten outside on the Mall.

Rest block

Plan about two hours of rest: back to the room or a quiet sit. Whether a room return works depends on your hotel; see the museum-rest notes in the DC hotels guide.

Afternoon

Washington Monument at an entry time booked ahead on recreation.gov (every person 2 and up needs a ticket), or same-day tickets picked up at the Lodge from 8:45 a.m., before the museum. The NPS fees page lists no hours, so check your ticket time and the closure list. If there are no tickets or the weather turns, view it from outside.

Day 2: National Zoo morning

Morning

Arrive near the 8 a.m. grounds opening with an entry pass for every person. Indoor exhibits open at 9 a.m.

Midday

Picnic is allowed. The Zoo suggests 2–3 hours and is on a hill; plan to leave before small children tire.

Rest block

Long afternoon rest. In winter hours the grounds close at 4 p.m. with last entry at 3 p.m., so the Zoo cannot be pushed into the afternoon.

Evening

Keep it free: dinner near the hotel.

Day 3: Air and Space, then American History

Morning

Air and Space with timed passes for everyone, babies included; book the earliest entry time available. Part of the museum is closed for renovation, so pick two or three galleries.

Lunch

Mars Café inside Air and Space, or eat before moving on.

Rest block

Short rest before the second museum, or end the day here with younger children.

Afternoon (optional)

American History. With children 6 and under, go Wednesday–Sunday so Wonderplace (10 a.m.–4 p.m.) is open.

Adjust the plan

Swaps for weather, weekday and age

Rain or high wind

Swap the Monument and the Zoo for any of the three museums. All three are indoors and open 10 a.m.–5:30 p.m.; the Monument can close in bad weather.

Monday or Tuesday with a child 6 or under

Wonderplace is closed. Put American History on another day, or use Natural History that day instead.

Only two days

Keep Day 1 and Day 3. Drop the Zoo, which needs the most planning: passes for everyone, parking or transit, and a hill.

Babies and toddlers

Every person needs a pass at Air and Space and the Zoo; the Monument needs a ticket from age 2. Children 4 and under ride Metro free (two per paying adult).

Teens

Keep the same skeleton. Swap the long rest for a later museum session and give them one gallery choice each day.

Official facts

Hours, passes and family rules by stop

National Museum of Natural History

Hours

10:00 a.m.–5:30 p.m. daily, including holidays; closed Christmas Day.

Entry

Free. No tickets or passes needed.

Family rules

  • Bagged lunches must stay stowed and be eaten outside; no lunch seating or refrigerated storage.
  • Cafés: Ocean Terrace 11 a.m.–3 p.m.; Atrium 11 a.m.–5 p.m. with limited choices after 3 p.m.
  • Security screening for everyone; no coat check, bag storage or lockers.
  • Strollers and small pushed wagons are permitted.

Official fact, checked October 27, 2026: Official visitor page

National Air and Space Museum (National Mall)

Hours

10:00 a.m.–5:30 p.m. daily; closed Christmas Day.

Entry

Free, but a free timed-entry pass is required for every visitor, regardless of age.

Family rules

  • One reservation covers up to nine passes; the pass does not limit how long you stay.
  • Enter on Jefferson Drive SW.
  • A large east-end section is closed for renovation and some galleries are still being built, so not every gallery is open.
  • The museum page does not say how far ahead passes are released; check it before the trip.

Official fact, checked October 27, 2026: Official visitor page

National Museum of American History

Hours

10:00 a.m.–5:30 p.m. daily except Christmas Day.

Entry

Free. No tickets required.

Family rules

  • Wegmans Wonderplace is designed for children 0 to 6.
  • Wonderplace is open Wednesday–Sunday, 10 a.m.–4 p.m., and closed Monday and Tuesday; the museum says hours are subject to change.

Official fact, checked October 27, 2026: Official visitor page · Wegmans Wonderplace page

Smithsonian's National Zoo

Hours

Open every day except Christmas Day. Sept. 15–March 14: grounds 8 a.m.–4 p.m. (last entry 3 p.m.), indoor exhibits 9 a.m.–4 p.m. March 15–Sept. 14: grounds 8 a.m.–6 p.m. (last entry 5 p.m.), indoor exhibits 9 a.m.–6 p.m.

Entry

Free, but every visitor of every age needs their own free entry pass; a limited number of walk-up passes are offered at pedestrian entrances.

Family rules

  • Parking: USD 30 per vehicle bought at least a day ahead, USD 40 on the day; it may sell out.
  • The Zoo says it sits on a hill with an incline and suggests 2–3 hours; its guest shuttle was listed as not available.
  • Stroller rental for a fee (no cash). Your own food and non-alcoholic drinks are allowed.

Official fact, checked October 27, 2026: Official visitor page · Zoo visitor FAQs

Washington Monument

Hours

The NPS fees page does not list hours. It lists one maintenance closure day most months plus holiday closures; check its list for your date.

Entry

Every guest age 2 and up needs a timed ticket. Free same-day tickets from 8:45 a.m. at the Washington Monument Lodge, first come, first served (up to six per person aged 16+). Advance tickets on recreation.gov open 30 days ahead at 10 a.m., with a non-refundable USD 1.00 service charge per ticket.

Family rules

  • The monument may close for high wind or thunderstorms, and tickets for those entry times are voided.
  • More advance tickets are released one day before the visit date.

Official fact, checked October 27, 2026: Official visitor page

Getting around

Metro fares for families

  • Up to two children age 4 and younger ride free with each fare-paying adult; children 5 and older pay and need their own card or SmarTrip.
  • Regular rail fares: weekdays 5 a.m.–9:30 p.m. USD 2.25–6.75; after 9:30 p.m. and weekends USD 2.25–2.50.
  • Pay by tapping a contactless credit or debit card, or SmarTrip on a card or phone.

Official fact, checked October 27, 2026: WMATA fares. Natural History lists Federal Triangle, Archives/Navy Memorial and Metro Center as nearby stations. Use WMATA's trip planner for your exact route.
