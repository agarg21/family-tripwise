Las Vegas, Nevada

Las Vegas Itinerary With Kids: Realistic 1, 2 and 3 Day Plans

Researched with AI (Miles Rowan) · sources linked and dated · How we check

Give each day one main outing and one hotel break, then swap stops for age, heat and weather.

Family of 4 tickets, estimate: 2 adults, kids 6 and 10

≈ $80 to $140

  • 1 day ≈ $80DISCOVERY Children's Museum (Downtown), hotel break, Bellagio
  • 2 days ≈ $80Your hotel's pool if open, then Bellagio. Next day, DISCOVERY.
  • 3 days ≈ $140Arrive and rest. DISCOVERY and Bellagio. Springs Preserve.

The catch: DISCOVERY closes most Mondays and major holidays, and opens at noon Sundays. Springs Preserve closes Tuesdays and Wednesdays. The 3-day order works if you arrive Tuesday to Saturday.

Kids under 6 or over 10?

Under 6: Kids 3 to 5 cost the same. Only younger kids change it: Springs Preserve is free at 2 and under, DISCOVERY under 1. Use the hotel break for naps.

Older kids: DISCOVERY is built for younger kids. Older kids can swap in Omega Mart, from ≈ $180 for four plus any fees ($45 starting price a ticket, no child price shown, read Nov 1).

Estimate from official prices: DISCOVERY $20 each (read Nov 7), Springs Preserve $18.95 adult, $10.95 child (read Nov 2), Bellagio free (read Aug 3). Hotel, food, parking and transport extra; taxes and online fees not checked. Next step: compare hotel prices

Official hours and prices: DISCOVERY, Springs Preserve, Red Rock

Each item below was read on the venue's own page on the date shown. Elsewhere on this page, Bellagio and Shark Reef facts are older (August 3, 2026) and remaining route facts older still (July 23, 2026). Confirm hours for your exact date.

  • DISCOVERY hours: Tuesday to Saturday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.; Sunday noon to 5 p.m. Closed Mondays, except during summer, spring and winter breaks and on select Mondays when Clark County schools are closed. DISCOVERY hours page (checked November 7, 2026).
  • DISCOVERY holiday closures: Closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Day and Easter. DISCOVERY hours page (checked November 7, 2026).
  • DISCOVERY admission: General admission $20 per person ages 1-99; local admission $15 with a valid Nevada ID; everyone age 1 and up needs a ticket. Online tickets bought through a non-museum third party may not be accepted. DISCOVERY tickets page (checked November 7, 2026).
  • DISCOVERY children: Visitors under 18 must be accompanied by an adult. DISCOVERY tickets page (checked November 7, 2026).
  • DISCOVERY visit basics: Built for ages 0-12. Strollers allowed, with stroller parking next to Toddler Town. Own snacks in designated areas; no cakes or large platters. Free parking in the museum garage. No lockers. DISCOVERY FAQ (checked November 7, 2026).
  • Springs Preserve: Open Thursday to Monday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Visitors $18.95 per adult, $10.95 per child ages 3-17; age 2 and under free; Nevada residents $9.95 and $4.95. Springs Preserve tickets page (checked November 2, 2026).
  • Red Rock Canyon Scenic Drive: $20 a private vehicle for the day. A timed-entry reservation is required October 1 to May 31 for entry 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. No reservation fee listed. BLM Red Rock planning page (checked November 3, 2026).

One-day first look

Use this for

Short visits, stopovers, and families who want one recognizable Vegas moment

First

Start with one ticketed stop. DISCOVERY Children's Museum suits younger kids; High Roller, a current show, Shark Reef (about $106-$134 for 2 adults and 2 kids 5-12, timed or anytime entry with VR, price seen Aug 3, parking and add-ons extra, check live) or an older-kid choice are the swaps. Check the exact ticket and minor policy.

Middle

Use the hotel, pool if access and weather fit, or a slow meal as the break. Do not add another resort just because it looks close.

Last

Finish at the Bellagio Conservatory and fountains, free when last checked. Check the current display and fountain schedule first.

When to stop

Stop after Bellagio. A second long walk through a resort is the first thing to remove.

Two-day first trip

Use this for

Most first-time families who want spectacle plus one child-led day

Day 1: resort + classic visual

Start with your hotel or its pool if open, then see Bellagio and have an early meal. Keep the evening optional.

Day 2: the day your kids choose

Choose DISCOVERY Children's Museum (Downtown) for younger children, Red Rock for an outdoor-ready car day (timed entry needed Oct to May), or one AREA15/teen choice for older kids. That stop is the whole day.

Hotel break

Return to the hotel or end sightseeing after that stop. Do not attach Red Rock, Downtown, or a major ticket to a second long Strip evening.

When to stop

If Day 1 ran late, skip anything extra on Day 2 before cutting the meal or hotel break.

Three-day balanced trip

Use this for

Families who want a softer arrival and one day away from the resorts

Day 1: arrive softly

Settle into the room, buy water or snacks, use the pool only if access and conditions fit, and keep dinner close to your hotel.

Day 2: classic Las Vegas

Pair Bellagio with one ticket, such as DISCOVERY (Downtown, so plan the ride), a skyline or a show. Put a hotel break between them rather than crossing resorts continuously.

Day 3: a third day out

Pick Springs Preserve (open Thursday to Monday), Downtown history, Red Rock for a nature drive (book a timed-entry slot Oct to May), or a teen-chosen thrill stop. Do not combine them.

When to stop

Keep one block optional each day and make only one evening a firm late plan.

Stop rules

Four rules that protect the itinerary

One main job per half day

A pool, major museum, nature drive, or ticketed attraction already fills half a day. Do not add a second big stop on the way.

Schedule the hotel break

Choose the hotel return, pool, quiet room time, or slow meal before the day starts. If the break disappears, drop the next activity.

Map the exact entrance

A resort name or neighborhood pin does not show the actual entrance, rideshare pickup, indoor detour, or stroller path. Check the chosen route for the date.

Set the evening stop rule

Decide which evening is allowed to run late and what gets deleted when the child is done. A paid ticket is not worth pushing through a tired kid.

Common planning questions

Las Vegas family itinerary FAQ

How many days do families need in Las Vegas?

Two days is the clearest first-trip structure: one Strip day with Bellagio and one day your kids choose. Add a third day for a softer arrival or a separate Downtown, nature, younger-child, or teen stop.

What should families skip on a short Las Vegas trip?

Skip repeated resort crossings, multiple major tickets in one day, and a firm late plan every night. Keep Bellagio, one stop that fits your kids' ages, a meal plan, and a real stop rule.

How should families change the itinerary for heat or rain?

Move the main block to a current indoor attraction, keep the meal and hotel break close to that choice, and drop the outdoor or pool block when conditions or access do not fit. Recheck current venue and weather information before leaving.
