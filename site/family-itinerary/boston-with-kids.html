Boston

Boston Itinerary With Kids: 3 Days Around Museum Hours and Rest

AI research contributor: Miles Rowan

One main stop each morning, a protected rest block, and an optional afternoon. Built from official hours and entry rules, not a visit.

Venue facts checked: October 27, 2026, on each venue's official page (linked on every card).

Day shapes and rest blocks are Family Tripwise planning estimates. We have not tested walking times, stroller routes or queues. Check hours and tickets for your dates.

The plan: Day 1 Museum of Science, Day 2 Children's Museum (younger children) or the Aquarium, Day 3 USS Constitution.

Main catch: the Children's Museum is closed Tuesdays and the ship opens Wednesday–Sunday only; leave strollers behind for the ship and, the Aquarium advises, its exhibits.

Cost: Museum of Science USD 33 adult / USD 28 age 3–11; Children's Museum USD 24 from age 1; the ship is free. Aquarium prices: check its ticket site.

Day by day

Three days, one anchor each morning

The Museum of Science, Children's Museum and Aquarium open at 9 a.m. (Children's Museum 10 a.m. on the first Saturday); the USS Constitution Museum opens at 9:30 a.m. and the ship at 10 a.m. Same-day re-entry is stated by the Museum of Science and the Aquarium only.

Day 1: Museum of Science

Morning

Museum of Science from its 9 a.m. opening. No timed ticket is needed; under-3s are free. Pick one or two halls rather than the whole building.

Lunch

Food from home goes outside on the plaza or at the picnic tables behind the Museum.

Rest block

Plan about two hours of rest. Same-day re-entry works with your confirmation or hand stamp, so a room or quiet break can come between halls. Whether a room return works depends on your hotel; see the rest notes in the Boston hotels guide.

Afternoon (optional)

Come back for a USD 6 Planetarium or Omni show, or stop for the day. Exhibit Halls close at 5 p.m.

Day 2: Children's Museum or the Aquarium

Morning

Our routing suggestion for younger children: Boston Children's Museum from 9 a.m. (closed Tuesdays; 10 a.m. on the first Saturday of the month). Our routing suggestion for older children: New England Aquarium at your timed entry.

Lunch

At the Aquarium, own food is eaten at the outdoor picnic tables. The Children's Museum visit page does not state its lunch rules.

Rest block

Long rest. The Children's Museum page does not state a re-entry rule, so finish there before the break; the Aquarium allows same-day re-entry.

Late afternoon (optional)

If you started at the Aquarium, the afternoon can return there before its 5 p.m. weekday closing (6 p.m. weekends).

Day 3: USS Constitution

Morning

USS Constitution Museum from 9:30 a.m., then the ship when it opens at 10 a.m., Wednesday–Sunday only. Adults bring photo ID; leave the stroller on the pier.

Lunch

Eat before or after the ship; the museum page does not list food rules.

Rest block

Afternoon rest. The ship closes at 4 p.m., so it fits best as a morning stop.

Afternoon (optional)

If you skipped it on Day 2, the Aquarium is the indoor option for this afternoon.

Adjust the plan

Swaps for weekday, weather, season and age

Monday or Tuesday

The USS Constitution ship is closed both days, and the Children's Museum is closed Tuesdays. Use the Museum of Science or the Aquarium instead.

Rain or cold

The Museum of Science, Children's Museum and Aquarium are indoor anchors; switch the ship day for one of them.

Babies and toddlers

Under 3 free at the Museum of Science; under 12 months free at the Children's Museum. The Aquarium advises against strollers and the ship does not allow them on board.

Only two days

Keep Day 1 and Day 2. Drop the ship, which needs photo ID for adults, no stroller and a Wednesday–Sunday date.

Spring and summer trips

The Swan Boats in the Public Garden (10–15 minute rides) are closed now and return in April 2027, the operator says. Check its page for dates and fares before adding one.

Teens

Keep the same skeleton. Swap the long rest for a later Museum of Science show and let them pick the Day 2 stop.

Official facts

Hours, tickets and family rules by stop

Museum of Science

Hours

Exhibit Halls open daily, 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Closed Thanksgiving and Christmas; closes at 2 p.m. the day before Thanksgiving and on Christmas Eve.

Entry

Exhibit Halls: adult USD 33, child (3–11) USD 28, senior (60+) USD 29; children under 3 free. Timed tickets are no longer required: pick your visit date. Planetarium, Omni and 4-D shows are USD 6 add-ons.

Family rules

  • Re-entry the same day: show your confirmation or a Museum hand stamp.
  • Food from home is eaten outside, on the front plaza or at picnic tables behind the Museum; no eating in the Exhibit Halls.
  • Strollers are available first come, first served; lockers for a small fee.

Official fact, checked October 27, 2026: Official visitor page · Visitor FAQs

Boston Children's Museum

Hours

Wednesday–Monday 9 a.m.–4 p.m.; closed Tuesdays. Opens at 10 a.m. on the first Saturday of each month.

Entry

USD 24 per adult and per child aged 1–15; infants under 12 months free. Advance online tickets are highly recommended.

Family rules

  • Its visit page does not state a re-entry rule, so do not plan to leave for a nap and come back on the same ticket.
  • Its visit page suggests the Red Line or Silver Line to South Station, then a short walk.
  • Garage parking validation at four listed garages needs a physical ticket pulled at the garage gate.

Official fact, checked October 27, 2026: Official visitor page

New England Aquarium

Hours

Fall hours: weekdays 9 a.m.–5 p.m.; Saturday and Sunday 9 a.m.–6 p.m. The page does not give the season dates, so check hours for your trip.

Entry

Timed tickets; buy ahead to guarantee entry, as weekends and holidays often sell out. Prices were not shown on the visit pages we could read; the ticket site did not load for us.

Family rules

  • You may leave and come back the same day; you may be asked to show your ticket.
  • Strollers are allowed but the Aquarium strongly recommends leaving them at home or in the car; it has none to borrow or rent. Lockers USD 5 small, USD 7 large.
  • Bring your own food to the blue picnic tables outside; only water inside the exhibits.
  • The Atlantic harbor seals are temporarily off exhibit.

Official fact, checked October 27, 2026: Official visitor page · Aquarium FAQs

USS Constitution and USS Constitution Museum

Hours

Museum open daily 9:30 a.m.–5 p.m. (closed Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day). The ship is open Wednesday–Sunday, 10 a.m.–4 p.m.

Entry

Ship: free, first come, first served. Museum: suggested admission USD 15 per person (USD 25 pay-it-forward, free to USD 10 reduced).

Family rules

  • Everyone boarding the ship goes through security; adults need a federal or state photo ID, visitors under 18 do not.
  • Strollers are not allowed on board and must be left on the pier.

Official fact, checked October 27, 2026: Official visitor page

Swan Boats, Public Garden

Hours

Closed for the season; the operator says it returns in April 2027. Its page showed no 2027 dates, hours or fares on October 27.

Entry

Pay by cash or card; individuals need no reservation. Check the operator page for 2027 fares.

Family rules

  • Rides last 10–15 minutes and do not run in rain, high wind or extreme heat.
  • Strollers fold onto the boat or stay on the unsecured patio by the dock.

Official fact, checked October 27, 2026: Official visitor page

Getting around

MBTA rules for children

  • Children age 11 and under ride the MBTA free. The page does not say how many children may ride with each adult.
  • Groups of 10 or more children must register travel in advance through the MBTA field-trip form.

Official fact, checked October 27, 2026: MBTA free-ride rules. Adult fares are not shown here: the MBTA fares site's robots.txt blocks AI agents, so we do not read it. Check MBTA fares and its trip planner yourself.
