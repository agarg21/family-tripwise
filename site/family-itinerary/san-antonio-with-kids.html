San Antonio, Texas

San Antonio Itinerary With Kids: Realistic 1, 2 and 3 Day Plans

Researched with AI (Miles Rowan) · sources linked and dated · How we check

Three realistic plans for kids 4 and 8: one main stop at a time, with a break built in. Swap stops for age, heat and your hotel.

Family of 4 tickets for the whole trip, estimate: 2 adults, kids 4 and 8

≈ $52 to $180

  • 1 day ≈ $52The Alamo (church free, exhibit paid), hotel break, short River Walk stroll
  • 2 days ≈ $124Add The DoSeum, a hands-on children's museum
  • 3 days ≈ $180Add the Witte Museum, or the zoo from ≈ $32 instead

The catch: Reserve the free Alamo church ticket first. The DoSeum is closed Tuesdays and opens at noon Sundays, so on a weekend go Saturday. Check its closure notice.

Spending less, or kids 13 and up?

Spending less: Skip the Alamo exhibit and tour only the free church: 1 day $0 in tickets, 2 days ≈ $72. Witte is free for 3 and under, The DoSeum for under 1.

Kids 13 and up: The Alamo exhibit is the $15 adult price and Witte is $16. Swap a day for a theme park if that's the trip: Six Flags from ≈ $196 or SeaWorld from ≈ $216 for four, with parking, taxes and fees extra.

Day-by-day routes

Your day-by-day plans

Our suggested order. Open a plan, then check tickets, hours and your route for your dates.

One-day first look

Use this for

Short visits and first-time families who want one easy San Antonio day

First

The Alamo: reserve the free church ticket, then add the paid exhibit if your kids will enjoy it. Decide before you arrive.

Then

Lunch, then a hotel break or nap. Keep this even if the morning runs long.

Last

A short River Walk stroll, Hemisfair or a simple dinner near your hotel. GO RIO's 35-minute narrated cruise is an option (no price on its official pages Nov 1, so check live). Drop this first if heat or waiting runs long.

When to stop

Stop after the Alamo and an easy finish. Pearl, the zoo, a cave tour and a theme park are the first things to leave out.

Two-day first trip

Use this for

Weekend families who want Downtown plus one day for the kids (on a weekend, swap the days: The DoSeum Saturday, the Alamo Sunday)

Day 1: The Alamo

Follow the one-day plan. Keep dinner and the evening close to your hotel.

Day 2: The DoSeum

Hands-on children's museum with free parking. Closed Tuesdays and opens at noon on Sundays. On a weekend, swap the days and go Saturday. If that doesn't work, use the zoo or Witte (check their hours). Older kids may prefer one theme park, which fills the day, or Natural Bridge Caverns (about 2 to 5 hours by our estimate, depending on the tour, plus the drive).

Keep one break

Protect one hotel return, slow meal or free afternoon. If Day 1 runs late, drop the Day 2 extras before you cut food or rest.

When to stop

Make Day 2 one stop. Don't combine the zoo, The DoSeum, caverns, SeaWorld, Six Flags and a late trip back Downtown.

Three-day balanced trip

Use this for

Families who want two set days plus one flexible or slower day

Day 1: The Alamo

Follow the one-day plan, with a clear way back to the hotel if it gets hot.

Day 2: The DoSeum

One main stop, with the last part of the day optional. Same Tuesday and Sunday rules as above.

Day 3: Witte or the zoo

Witte is indoors and suits a wider age range. The zoo is the outdoor choice, from ≈ $32 for four. A mission, Pearl or a slow hotel day also work; check openings and how you'll get there.

When to stop

Keep one stop optional each day, and commit to only one far-out big outing.

Stop rules

Four rules that protect the itinerary

One main stop at a time

A museum, the zoo, a cave tour or a mission route can take up a half day or more, and a theme park the whole day. Don't add another big stop because it's nearby.

Schedule the break

Choose the meal, hotel return, shade or quiet indoor time before the day starts. If the break disappears, drop the next activity.

One far-out big outing

Natural Bridge, SeaWorld and Six Flags each need their own dated tickets, transport and return plan. Don't stack them.

Decide what to drop

Choose which stop goes first and how you'll get back to the hotel when weather, waiting or tired kids change the day.

Common planning questions

San Antonio family itinerary FAQ

How many days should a family spend in San Antonio?

Two days works for a first trip: a Downtown day with the Alamo, then a kids' day at The DoSeum. Add a third day for Witte, the zoo, a mission, a theme park or a slower hotel day.

What should families skip on a short San Antonio trip?

Skip crossing the city for several stops, more than one far-out big outing, and a firm late plan after every big stop. Keep one main stop at a time, a meal or break, and a clear point to stop.

How should families change a San Antonio itinerary for heat or storms?

Move the main stop to one indoor or shaded choice, keep the meal and hotel break close to it, and drop the zoo, park, long River Walk, mission or theme-park block when conditions do not fit. Recheck venue and weather information before leaving.
