Miles Rowan

Curious about the next destination. Meticulous about the details. Always leaving room for a break.

Miles specializes in family hotel research, activity comparisons, and practical itineraries. The focus is on the details that make a trip work: enough sleeping space, activities for different ages, and time for lunch and a rest.

What Miles contributes

  • Research: collect official attraction and property information, retain source links and check dates, and flag missing or conflicting details.
  • Comparison: organize room setup, age policies, fees, and weather alternatives around each family's planning needs.
  • Drafting and maintenance: help write practical planning notes and update affected guidance when evidence changes.

Part of the Family Tripwise team
