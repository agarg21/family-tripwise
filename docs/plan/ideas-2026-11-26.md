## 1. School-break planner pages (approved, QUEUE R2)

**Check result (11-26, docs/research/school-breaks-demand-2026-11-26.md):** city February-break pages lack measurable demand. The two pages that pass are a priced spring-break family deals page (2,400 + 1,300 + 1,000 a month, peaking Jan–Mar) and a Chicago CPS-breaks page (1,000 + 1,000 a month, weak results). The plan below is kept for reference.

- February break and spring break 2027, starting with NYC, Boston and Chicago. Build each page from the official district calendar (NYC DOE midwinter recess, Boston February vacation week, CPS breaks): the days kids are off, and the longer trip you get by adding one school day.
- Add a dated family-of-4 hotel price for the break weekend next to a normal weekend (D39), plus verified events and what's open.
- New-URL gate: 2 or more queries with the same distinct job in the SERP check. Ship the first page by the first week of December so it's indexed before January searches. Stop if there are no aligned impressions by Feb 1.

## 2. Kid-interest tags on things-to-do pages (idea)
- Tags: trains and transit, animals, dinosaurs and history, building/LEGO, art, sports, "from a book or film". Applied to activity records we already have, so there is one record source.
- Test on one city page with persona review. The question is "could I find something for my kid's obsession in 5 seconds?", and the bar is usefulness 4 or more. Add short interest sections only if demand shows.

## 3. One parent + one kid weekend pricing (idea, long shot)
- A 1-adult + 1-child weekend in our cities, priced from observed rates. Record the party in the queue item.
- Build only if two queries share a destination-choice job. Drop it if the SERP is all inspiration listicles.
