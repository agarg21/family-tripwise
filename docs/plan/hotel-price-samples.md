# Hotel price samples: weekend + holiday (owner D39, 2026-11-05)

Owner review of /where-to-stay/new-york-city-family-hotels.html: a single Tuesday night (Jan 19, 2027) is not how most
families travel. Replace the Tuesday samples on every family-hotel page with two date sets, and add small holiday tables
while the season is near.

## Date sets (party: 2 adults + kids 5 and 8, as now)

1. Normal weekend: Friday to Sunday (2 nights), 8–12 weeks out, never a holiday or long weekend (skip MLK, Presidents'
   Day, Easter, Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas–New Year, and spring-break weeks). For the
   current refresh: Fri Jan 22 – Sun Jan 24, 2027. Show it as "≈ $X a night (Fri–Sun, Jan 22–24, 2027)" and the 2-night
   total when the provider row allows it (pair 1-night and 2-night searches as in ops/research-requests/README.md).
2. Next peak holiday, while it is within about 10 weeks: for Nov–Dec 2026, Christmas weekend Fri Dec 25 – Sun Dec 27, 2026
   and New Year's weekend Fri Jan 1 – Sun Jan 3, 2027. Later peaks: Presidents' Day weekend, spring break, July 4.

## Holiday tables (Christmas and holiday cities first: NYC, Chicago, Orlando, San Diego, San Antonio, DC)

- Two small tables (Christmas weekend, New Year's weekend): hotel, ≈ price a night, "seen <date>".
- Availability: Google showing no price is "No price shown on <date>", never "sold out" (it may be unlisted). Every
  holiday row carries its observation date; say "Prices and rooms change fast; check live."
- Refresh weekly while shown (one search set per city per week, within the D8 budget); a sample leaves the page 7 days
  before arrival (existing rule), so the Christmas table drops on Dec 18 and the New Year table on Dec 25.

## Same pass

- Hero line: say "family of 4" as the main basis; mention room for 5 only as the Family of 5 option ("…kitchen,
  breakfast or pool; family of 5? see the 5-person options").
- "Hotel facts checked" dates from July (NYC July 25, LV July 22, Chicago July 23, SA July 26): re-read the official
  hotel facts (layouts, occupancy, breakfast, pool, fees) for pages being re-priced (QUEUE G13 step 4), so the visible
  date is honest and recent.
- Why not weekdays: most US families travel on weekends and school breaks. NYC also has strong weekday business demand,
  so a weeknight is not reliably cheaper. Measure the two date sets rather than assume.
