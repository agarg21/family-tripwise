# Events, News and Trends Inventory: Family Tripwise

- Prepared 2026-10-28 by a research subagent, using public web pages only (WebSearch/WebFetch). No logins, forms or posting.
- Scope: family-relevant events (Nov 2026 – Jan 2027), news from roughly the last 30 days, and trend signals. Deals are covered separately in `deals-inventory.md`.
- Destinations: San Diego, NYC, Chicago, Las Vegas, San Antonio, Orlando, Washington DC, Boston, Cancun, Puerto Vallarta, London, plus a few national items.
- Every date below comes from the cited page as fetched on 2026-10-28. Before any public use, re-verify it on the official page (see the integrity notes in §6).

## 0. Bottom line

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Genuinely new, verifiable, family-relevant items per day (Nov–Dec peak) | **About 3.5–6 per day (25–40 per week)** across all 11 destinations plus national items. Only **1–2 per day are true news**. The rest are dated event moments: announcements, on-sale or lottery dates, opening nights, sell-outs and last-chance dates. |
| Same measure, mid-Jan to Feb | **About 1–2 per day**. Holiday events end Jan 3–17, and the stream shrinks to spring-break, price and policy news. |
| Can 5–6 social posts per day be sustained from fresh, verifiable items alone? | **Only in Nov–Dec, and only by counting event moments.** Strict news supports about 2 posts per day. The other 3–4 must be date-pegged reminders (e.g., "DC tree lottery closes today 3 pm ET") or evergreen planning cards tied to existing guides. Otherwise the cadence pushes toward thin or repeated posts. |
| Site-page-worthy items | **About 1–3 per week.** Most timely items fit social or a dated section inside an existing guide. Few justify a new URL, and new URLs need SERP evidence plus owner approval under AGENTS.md. |
| Strongest recurring timely engine | Orlando theme-park news. One fan news site (BlogMickey) ran about 40 Disney/Universal headlines in Oct 1–27, of which about 15 affect family planning. |
| Weakest coverage | Las Vegas, Puerto Vallarta, Boston and San Antonio. Official 2026 holiday dates were often **not yet published** on 2026-10-28. |

## 6. Integrity and operational notes

1. **Official pages lag.** As of 2026-10-28, several official or tourism pages still showed old seasons:
   - sandiego.org Top December Events (2025)
   - December Nights PDFs (2025)
   - Griffin MSI listing (2025)
   - Meet Boston lights trail (2025)
   - Visit San Antonio holiday page (2025)
   - Kew Christmas (2020)
   - National Zoo release (2025)

   Late Oct to mid Nov is the announcement wave, so expect many "2026 dates confirmed" items over the next 3 weeks. Do not fill gaps by carrying 2025 dates forward. That would breach the repo's rule that "a date passing never renews a fact".
2. **Search results are heavily polluted.** They include AI or SEO spam pages that pose as 2026 previews: scratchandwin.tcl.com, do-b.clients.kiwiirc.com, pixidia.com, tap-app-api.adeq.arkansas.gov, assorted "featurespace" mirrors, and templated "2026" aggregator pages. Use only official, primary-press or established fan-news sources, and confirm against the official page.
3. **Headlines exaggerate.** An Inside the Magic headline ("Magic Kingdom Tickets Unavailable Beginning November 8", [link](https://insidethemagic.net/?p=850792)) referred only to one party-night sell-out. Never repost a headline without reading the item.
4. **Conflicts to flag rather than resolve:**
   - Hogwarts in the Snow end date (Jan 17 vs Jan 19)
   - DC ZooLights "daily" (Destination DC) vs select nights (2025 official pattern)
   - Bronx Zoo release's "Dec 3, 2025" sensory-night typo
5. **Labels.** Social posts and site rows need an as-of date and a source label (official, press, or fan-site report). Fan-site Disney reporting is reliable in practice but is not official. Hotel-status changes such as the Polynesian refurb should be confirmed on Disney's own pages before the hotel evidence record changes.
6. **Approvals.** Under the current operator rules, new seasonal URLs need SERP evidence of a distinct user job plus an owner Approved row. Posting on social is an external action that needs owner approval. This report only sizes the inventory.
