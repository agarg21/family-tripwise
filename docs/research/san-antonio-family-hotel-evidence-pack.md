Status: implementation evidence for `FT-PUB-005`

Target: `https://familytripwise.com/where-to-stay/san-antonio-family-hotels.html`

## Decision

Publish one 12-property San Antonio family hotel comparison organized around trip shape and hotel function, not an ordinal ranking.

The page owns the overlapping hotel, resort, pool, lazy-river, suite, kitchen, breakfast, River Walk, SeaWorld, and Six Flags variants. It should not create a separate URL for every modifier. The existing stay-area page remains the answer to which base fits the itinerary; the hotel page answers which named property fits the family's room, water, meal, location, and approximate-price constraints.

The selected set covers:

- three full-service resort comparisons with substantial pools or slides;
- one vacation-club villa option with kitchens, laundry, and a lazy river;
- a Downtown two-room suite with breakfast;
- two Downtown kitchen-and-breakfast choices;
- an included-food value choice;
- an all-suite River Walk reset;
- two central classic-sights hotels; and
- an indoor-pool and Rivercenter option.

## Selected Property Evidence

- Role: full resort and water-play trip near SeaWorld.
- Official facts: Hyatt publishes a water park, multiple pools, the Big Spring lagoon, and two-queen rooms that accommodate two adults and two children.
- Family constraint: the resort is not a walkable Downtown base. The exact room, water-feature schedule, food hours, parking, and daily driving plan matter.
- Public price basis: Expedia exposed about `$437` total for a one-night standard-room example.
- Page range: `$360-$700+`; the lower bound allows lower-demand dates, while room category and resort demand can move above the observed example.
- Review sample: 8 recent visible Booking.com entries, June 2026-January 2026; at least 5 explicitly identified families with young children.
- Directional themes: pools, lazy river, activities, grounds, and service were common positives. Limited midday food, kid-heavy crowding, no microwave in some rooms, and a sand-rinse issue appeared as conflicts.
- Check: exact room occupancy, current water-feature schedule, food hours, parking, and final total.

## Price Method

The page uses rough total-per-night planning bands rather than live quotes.

- The evidence check looked for a public one-night price example with taxes and mandatory fees included when the page exposed that total.
- Most observed examples were for two adults and a standard room. They are anchors, not family-booking scenarios.
- The published range widens the observed anchor for date volatility and known room-category variation.
- Upper edges are editorial planning ceilings, not observed quotes.
- Parking, optional food, optional activities, incidental holds, a second room, and optional purchases are outside the range.
- Wild Oak's visible example excluded taxes, so its published lower bound was raised into a rough total-planning band rather than being repeated as a total.
- The page does not expose a booking flow or imply live availability.

Refresh the price examples on any material hotel-page update. Treat a range more than 90 days old as stale context rather than a current price claim.

## Review Method

For each selected property:

1. inspect a recent public review slice;
2. count explicit family context only when the public page identifies it;
3. paraphrase recurring positives and decision-relevant conflicts;
4. do not infer a property-wide consensus from a small slice;
5. do not reproduce reviewer names, IDs, full text, or a complete export;
6. prefer conflicts that alter a family decision, such as current pool operation, sleeping surfaces, food hours, room condition, parking, or sales-presentation context; and
7. convert unresolved conflicts into a specific pre-booking check.

The live page says "themes in recent online reviews," not "review signal." It explains whether family labeling was strong or thin and avoids presenting desk research as a stay.

## Claim Boundaries

The page may say:

- an official source publishes a room, pool, kitchen, breakfast, or water feature;
- a small recent public review sample commonly praised or surfaced a theme;
- a rough planning range starts from a public example;
- a property is worth comparing for a named trip style; and
- a family should confirm a specific unresolved detail.

The page must not say:

- Family Tripwise personally stayed at or inspected a property;
- a hotel, room, pool, route, or area is guaranteed safe, quiet, stroller-friendly, clean, open, available, or suitable;
- a crib, rollaway, connection, kitchen, pool, or sleeping surface is guaranteed without a room-level source;
- a small review sample represents all families;
- a rough range is a live quote;
- a hotel is the universal best option; or
- a water feature or pool will operate on a traveler's dates.
