## 3. Free and cheap programs (as the brief read them)
- SKI NY Free for Kids Passport (grades 3–5): $45; up to 2 free lift days per area with a paying adult; 2026-12-01 to
  2027-06-30. Near NYC per the brief: Belleayre (holidays incl.), Mount Peter (not MLK/Presidents), Thunder Ridge (not
  holidays), Windham (weekdays), Plattekill, Holiday Mountain; Hunter does not participate.
- Hunter: kids 6 and under ski free (lift) — third party.
- Mount Peter free beginner lessons: weekends/holidays on the half hour 10 a.m.–2:30 p.m.; weekdays 11 a.m. and 1 p.m.;
  paid lift ticket required.
- PA 4th–5th grade pass: 2026–27 terms not verified by the brief.
