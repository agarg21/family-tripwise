## Representative Ranking Pages

### Expedia Magazine

- **Current signal:** dated February 2026 and inspected September 16.
- **What it does well:** covers several areas and connects each to pace, kid-oriented attractions, green space, dining, and transit context.
- **Area overlap:** Lincoln Park, Streeterville, and the Loop overlap directly with Family Tripwise; Expedia also includes Hyde Park and Andersonville.
- **Advantage Family Tripwise cannot claim:** OTA inventory, transactional reach, publisher distribution, and a larger destination-content network.
- **Boundary:** breadth and vivid area prose do not by themselves establish exact hotel routes, stroller practicality, or a universal family fit. Some transit and walking language is more certain than Family Tripwise should publish without current route-level verification.
- **Family Tripwise position:** keep its five-area set tied to explicit trip shapes and exact-route checks rather than matching Expedia's different five-area geography.

### HotelsVetted

## Result Overlap And Job Boundary

The visitor-oriented pages disagree on the universal starting area. Expedia emphasizes multiple paces and neighborhoods; HotelsVetted leads with Streeterville for many families; Travel Family Blog leads with Loop/South Loop based on one trip; Little Chicago Guide says Loop or Streeterville. This disagreement supports conditional routing rather than a single `best` verdict.

The overlap is stronger at the decision-field level than at the recommendation level:

- first two days and anchor attractions;
- ability to return to the room or reduce transport;
- child age and pace;
- downtown versus neighborhood tradeoff;
- named-hotel handoff for room, pool, breakfast, and price.

Those fields map cleanly to two existing Family Tripwise jobs:

## Reusable Lesson

A modifier that sounds travel-relevant can still carry a different dominant job. Before broadening a stay-area page around `family friendly neighborhoods`, separate visitor lodging from residential/moving intent. Preserve a clean area-to-hotel handoff when the family decision fields differ, even if competitors blend both jobs into one article.
