How to read it:
- "Suites" and "2 bedroom suites" are mixed intent: groups and couples search them too. In Las Vegas especially, the 4,400 is mostly not families.
- NYC is unusual for bunk-bed and 2-queen demand. Elsewhere those are near zero, so other cities' pages should lead with suites, kitchens and 2-bedroom units.
- SERPs (domains only, unverified): Orlando bunk beds = a hotel plus Tripadvisor threads; Las Vegas connecting = a blog plus forums; Chicago suites = hotels' own pages plus OTAs. All look beatable by a sourced comparison.
- Miami would be a new destination, which needs owner approval.
