# Growth strategy research (owner request, 2026-11-03)

Owner question: does fresh deal content actually get found when people search for deals, and how should Family Tripwise grow (SEO, social, anything else)? Full strategy for the owner: Claude Doc "Family Tripwise growth strategy (Nov 2026)". Nothing below is approved work until `ops/OWNER.md` has the rows listed at the end.

## Findings
- Our numbers: 12 clicks / 3,454 impressions in the 28 days to 2026-11-01, average position 44; impressions up from 213 (July) but clicks never above 12. No deal page has impressions; 7 deal pages, /deals/ and /this-week/ not indexed (GSC snapshot 2026-11-03). About 10 social posts since 10-29 (7 Facebook, 3 Instagram); Facebook Page 0 followers; the site has no analytics.
- Demand (`docs/research/dataforseo-growth-deal-search-2026-11-03.json`, provider estimates, US): holiday-event queries are the largest low-difficulty demand (e.g. "busch gardens christmas town" 18,100/mo, 90,500 in Nov; "zoolights" 90,500 in Dec; city "christmas events" 4,400–12,100 in Dec; KD 0–5 mostly). Black Friday travel/cruise/Disney deals 40,500–60,500 in Nov. Brand deal queries steady (e.g. "great wolf lodge deals" 12,100). "Worth it / cost / cheapest time" queries small (480–2,900) but low difficulty and matched to our evidence.
- Results pages: 10 of 12 live SERPs had an AI Overview. Facebook Group posts ranked on page one for family-cost queries; an Instagram post ranked #1 for "chicago zoolights". Most result URLs came back masked (google.com/goto), so domain-level conclusions use web-search checks.
- Google applies freshness only where the query expects it; deal SERPs are held by official pages, resellers, news and established publishers. Pew (2025-07-22): 8% click a result with an AI summary vs 15% without; 1% click inside the summary. Feb 2026 Discover update favours local, original, timely content from sites with topic expertise (SEJ). Aug 2026: Google partly relaxed site-reputation enforcement for publisher coupon sections.
- Social (vendor data): Facebook link posts reach ~0.2% of followers (Socialinsider); Meta testing a 2-link-posts-a-month cap for Pages (TechCrunch 2025-12-17). Instagram: 5 hashtags max; public pro-account posts can appear in Google. Pinterest: 619M MAU (Q4 2025). Deal brands that grew from zero grew email lists via press and Reddit (Scott's Cheap Flights: ~300 → 3,000 after one Business Insider story; under $500 marketing, CNBC 2016-11-29).

## Recommended direction
Keep the agents' fresh-evidence engine; feed durable pages (one per recurring offer, no year in the URL; city holiday hubs refreshed yearly; "what it really costs a family of 4" pages), an email list and data-led press. Fresh deals go to email and social first. Add analytics before scaling.
