# Skiing near Chicago with kids: groundwork (owner session, read 2026-11-10)

Facts below were read on the official pages on 2026-11-10. Re-read each page before the build (weekly

## Cascade Mountain, Portage, WI (official, 2026-27 prices posted)
- Lift tickets: https://www.cascademountain.com/?p=1478 ("2026/2027"). Adult (18+) open-close: Base $79,
  Select $108, Peak $115; night (last 5 hours) $43 / $60 / $65. Sales tax not included. Pre-purchase
  required Fri–Sun and on all Select and Peak days. Cashless. "All dates for the 2026/2027 season have
  limited availability." Day-type calendar is a separate page (not read).
- Kids: https://www.cascademountain.com/cascade-kids. "Kids 12 and under receive a free lift ticket when
  accompanied by a paid adult"; one adult ticket covers up to 5 child tickets. Junior (13–17): Base $69,
  Select $98, Peak $105; night $43 / $60 / $65. Tubing: 6 and under $15 base / $25 select-peak; 7 and up
  $30 / $45. Snow Angel lift and tube combo +$5 on non-peak Mon–Thu Base days from Jan 11.
- Rentals (helmet included): Experience package Base $45, Select $59, Peak $65 open-close.
- Family-of-4 estimate (2 adults, kids 5 and 8), lift tickets only, Base day: 2 × $79 = $158 before tax
  (kids free with paid adult); Peak day $230. With 4 Experience rentals on a Base day: $158 + $180 = $338
  before tax. Ski-school prices and minimum age: separate page, not read.
- Drive time from Chicago: not stated on the pages read.

## Chestnut Mountain Resort, Galena, IL (official; 2025-26 prices only on 11-10)
- https://www.chestnutmtn.com/activity/rates/ still shows "2025 – 2026 WINDOW PRICING". 6 and under free
  lift ticket; one price for 7 and older. Bundles (lift + rental + group lesson) exist. Re-read for 2026-27.

## Villa Olivia, Bartlett, IL (Bartlett Park District; 2025-26 only)
- Snow Kids (https://bartlettparks.org/villa-olivia/snow-kids/): ages 4–6 intro ski, 6–11 group ski,
  6–7 intro snowboard, 8–12 group snowboard; each 3 × 1.5-hour classes with slope ticket, lesson,
  rental and helmet. Registration closed for 2025-26; no 2026-27 dates or fees on 11-10.
- Lesson packages page: same, 2025-26 only, fee tables blank.
