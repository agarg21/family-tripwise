## Las Vegas

### Lee Canyon, Mount Charleston (official)
- Season opens Fri Nov 20, 2026 (https://www.leecanyonlv.com/winter-lift-ticket/: "26/27 Ski season
  starts november 20!"; News 3 LV 11-10 also: Wild West Bowl from Sat Dec 12).
- Tickets online only, variable pricing that "go[es] up with demand"; "starting as low as $9 + a $10 Lee
  Cash" (ticket type not stated); News 3 says prices rise after Tue Nov 10. Changes free until 11:59 p.m.
  the night before; free parking; reusable RFID card.
- Kids 12 and under ski free with a Power Kids pass (enrollment required; read its page for the fee).
- Learn2Turn ($99, ages 13+, 2 days lessons + lift + rental, third day at Brian Head) page still names
  the 2025-26 season and Jan 6, 2026: stale, do not use until updated.
- Family-of-4 figure: not computable on 11-10 (variable prices; read the dated online price for a named
  Saturday at build time, with the observation date).
- Only one hill within a day trip; the page shape is one hill plus Brian Head (UT) as the overnight option.
