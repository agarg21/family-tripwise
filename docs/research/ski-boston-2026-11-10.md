## Wachusett Mountain, Princeton, MA
- Lift tickets (https://www.wachusett.com/?p=162): "2026/2027 Rates Coming Soon!" Online discount
  applies until midnight the day before; Lower Mountain and 5-and-under tickets sold onsite only; no
  third-party tickets. Night session 7–9:30 p.m. runs 12/25/26 to 3/13/27.
- Polar Kids, ages 4–8, "26/27" (https://www.wachusett.com/lessons-rentals/lessons/polar-kids/): 90-minute
  lesson with a 4-hour lift ticket; $129 weekday, $139 weekend (8 a.m. session $139, weekends and holidays
  only); rentals extra (helmet with rental). Online reservations required, opening end of November; no
  walk-ins; non-refundable. 3 sessions 20% off (code on its page). Child must use the bathroom alone.
  Holiday periods listed: Dec 24, 2026–Jan 3, 2027; Jan 18, 2027; Feb 13–21, 2027.

- Ski NH 4th & 5th Grade Passport (https://www.skinh.com/passport): $59; one lift ticket or trail-pass
  voucher at each of 30+ member areas; some areas may need a $5 RFID card; 2026–2027 blackouts Dec 27,
  2026–Jan 3, 2027, Jan 16–18, Feb 13–15 and Feb 20, 2027; applications opened Sept 23, 2026 via
  skinh.winterkids.org and the WinterKids app; limited, no refunds. Page does not say whether a paying
  adult is needed (read FAQ/rules next). Massachusetts association program: not read yet.
