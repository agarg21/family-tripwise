# Family Tripwise SEO Doctrine

Last updated: 2026-08-01

This doctrine converts the current strategy into decisions. It is intentionally opinionated. Use it unless fresh project evidence, a current SERP, or an explicit user decision justifies an exception.

## 1. Search Is Distribution For A Product

Family Tripwise is not building an article inventory. It is building a family-travel decision product that search can distribute.

Every indexable page must do at least one difficult job well: normalize fragmented facts, compare options consistently, expose important conflicts, calculate a useful band, route a family by constraints, or make the next decision materially faster.

Clean prose is not information gain. A page that merely summarizes official sites, reviews, Reddit, and competitors should not be published.

## 2. Page Count Is Not Topical Authority

We reject page-count goals, one-post-per-day goals, modifier factories, and competitor-sitemap mimicry.

A new URL is justified only when:

1. at least two representative queries expose the same distinct user job and page type in current SERPs;
2. the job cannot be satisfied cleanly by a current page without confusing its primary decision;
3. the evidence can support a useful default, not only a keyword-shaped introduction;
4. the page can be maintained without weakening existing cities;
5. the required new-destination or batch approval is satisfied.

Otherwise improve, consolidate, or leave the current architecture alone.

## 3. One URL Owns A User Job, Not One Keyword

Synonyms and close variants usually belong to the same page. A page can serve `family friendly hotels in San Diego`, `best family hotels in San Diego`, and `San Diego hotels for kids` when the observed SERPs and user job overlap.

Create a separate page for a materially different decision, not an exact-match phrase. Protect role clarity through internal links and distinct page promises rather than repeating whole sections across variants.

## 4. SERP Analysis Diagnoses; It Does Not Dictate

Use ranking pages to learn:

- dominant intent and page type;
- recurring entities and decision criteria;
- SERP features and authority constraints;
- what users still need to reconstruct across tabs;
- which competitor advantages are firsthand, local, transactional, community, or link-based.

Do not merge competitor headings into an outline and call it research. Our implementation must name the information gain before drafting begins.

## 5. Technical SEO Is A Gate, Not A Growth Story

A page must be 200, crawlable, self-canonical, indexable, internally linked, in the sitemap when appropriate, structured correctly, and usable on mobile. Fix verified defects immediately.

Once that gate is green, another Lighthouse point, schema type, resubmission, or indexing inspection is not presumed to improve competitive rankings. Move to intent, usefulness, evidence, and authority unless a new defect appears.

## 6. Diagnose The Ranking Lane Before Editing

Use query-level evidence whenever available. Page and property averages are orientation, not query rank.

| Observed state | Default diagnosis | Eligible response |
|---|---|---|
| Not discovered or not crawled | Discovery or crawl state | Verify technical gate and internal discovery; do not rewrite merely to provoke a crawl |
| Crawled/indexable with no aligned impressions | Intent still unknown or demand mismatch | Observe a finalized window; inspect target SERPs only if the page role itself is doubtful |
| Aligned query cohort averages worse than position 40 | Low visibility/authority | Check gross intent mismatch and missing information gain; prioritize authority and patience over CTR work |
| Aligned cohort averages position 21-40 | Relevance exists, competitiveness is weak | Inspect SERP gaps, evidence depth, internal links, and authority; edit only for a concrete deficiency |
| Aligned cohort averages position 8-20 | Near-page-one opportunity | Compare the live SERP, title/snippet, decision support, freshness, and links; one bounded improvement may be warranted |
| Aligned cohort averages position 1-7 | Protect and refine | Avoid broad rewrites; consider a small title/snippet or usefulness test only with enough stable impressions |

These bands are project heuristics, not claims about individual impressions or universal Google thresholds.

## 7. CTR Work Must Earn Eligibility

Do not call a page a CTR problem because aggregate CTR is low.

A title/snippet action normally requires:

- one coherent, relevant query family;
- period-average position 20 or better;
- at least 100 impressions in a comparable finalized 28-day window;
- a current crawl after the last material page change;
- live-SERP inspection showing a plausible promise, title, snippet, or result-format disadvantage;
- no stronger indexation, intent, content, or authority diagnosis.

An obvious broken or misleading title remains a defect and can be fixed without meeting the experiment threshold. Otherwise low-rank impressions are a visibility problem before they are a CTR problem.

## 8. Existing-Page Edits Need A Named Deficiency

Aligned impressions make a page worth investigating, not automatically editing. A substantive edit must identify at least one of:

- wrong or mixed intent;
- a missing decision criterion visible across current SERPs or parent questions;
- stale or weak evidence;
- unsupported certainty or trust debt;
- excessive repetition or scan friction;
- an internal-link or page-role conflict;
- a competitor usefulness gap we can honestly exceed.

If the review cannot name the deficiency and its expected user benefit, the correct action is `OBSERVE`.

## 9. AI Wins Through Research Operations

AI may accelerate source discovery, extraction, normalization, entity matching, contradiction detection, comparable-field construction, freshness checks, calculations, drafting, and constraint routing.

AI does not earn credit for page count, word count, publishing frequency, or stylistic polish. Automation begins after a human-readable action has a user job, evidence model, acceptance criteria, and stop rule.

Core page answers remain visible without agent interaction. The agent personalizes the same validated evidence rather than inventing a second factual layer.

## 10. Research-Only Travel Can Be Authoritative

We cannot honestly compete on lived local experience. We can compete on research discipline and decision speed.

An online-research page becomes defensible when it:

- separates official facts, price observations, review signals, community questions, estimates, and interpretation;
- records freshness and conflicts;
- compares the same criteria across options;
- explains why a criterion matters to a particular family constraint;
- makes calculations or routing rules inspectable;
- links directly to volatile verification sources;
- stays calm about uncertainty instead of hiding it or covering the page in disclaimers.

The standard is not "well researched." The standard is "meaningfully less work and a better decision for the parent."

## 11. Authority Must Have A Citation Reason

We reject bulk directories, paid placements disguised as editorial links, automated outreach, raw referring-domain quotas, and third-party authority scores as goals.

An authority action needs:

1. a named useful asset, dataset, comparison, or finding;
2. a specific audience that can reuse or cite it;
3. legitimate relevant prospects with a page-level reason to reference it;
4. separately approved external contact;
5. measurement of relevant citations, referral use, discovery, and aligned query movement without promising causality.

Three relevant editorial links can teach more than 300 anonymous directory links.

## 12. Time Is Evidence Collection, Not A Sandbox Countdown

One month can diagnose crawl, indexing, query interpretation, obvious defects, and whether the research workflow is functioning. It cannot fairly judge durable authority.

Use the current 30-day, 30-90-day, 3-6-month, and 6-12-month decision windows. Do not wait passively for domain age, and do not manufacture edits because a checkpoint arrived. Change something when evidence identifies a better action.

## 13. Strong SEO Includes Stop Rules

Stop or reject an action when:

- the idea exists only because a tool surfaced a keyword;
- SERP evidence does not support a distinct page;
- the output would summarize rather than add information gain;
- the evidence cannot support the promised decision;
- a current page already owns the job;
- the only rationale is freshness cadence, page volume, or competitor count;
- the measurement sample cannot distinguish the proposed effect;
- maintenance cost exceeds the likely learning value;
- external authority work lacks a credible citation reason.

Doing nothing is a valid SEO decision when it preserves a coherent, useful, measurable site.

## Practitioner Evidence Rule

Reddit, newsletters, case studies, and SEO vendors are hypothesis sources. Before adopting advice, ask:

- Is the site or page disclosed?
- Are baseline, duration, age, query mix, and absolute numbers provided?
- Which other variables changed?
- Is the author selling the recommended method?
- Does the claim survive official guidance and project evidence?
- What observation would falsify it here?
