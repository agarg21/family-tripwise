# Family Tripwise Current Strategy (2026-10-27 → 2026-11-13)

The previous long-form strategy is frozen at `archive/2026-10/strategy/current-strategy.md`; `strategy/seo-doctrine.md` and `strategy/content-principles.md` still apply.

## Goal for this cycle
Turn a technically clean but stalled site into one that ships trustworthy improvements every week and starts earning clicks. Success on 2026-11-13 is the scoreboard in `ops/STATE.md` moving in the right direction, every live page free of stale or unobserved facts, and a written renew-or-pause recommendation (Q14).

## What the evidence says (2026-10-27 review)
- Traffic is early-stage: 8 clicks / 3,202 impressions per 28 days, average position 44. Impressions roughly doubled in October; clicks have not.
- Itinerary pages (avg position 8–14) and stay-area pages (~20) earn the clicks. Hotel lists hold most impressions at position 45–60 with almost no clicks and carry July "editorial" price bands.
- Five sitemap URLs are unknown to Google; seven pages show past-due review dates; the October lodging pages have 1–2 inbound links.
- No public release since 2026-10-13: the old process produced research and offline tooling instead of site changes.

## Priorities, in order
1. Trust and freshness: no past-due dates, no past-stay price samples, no unobserved price bands, truthful About page.
2. Discovery: sitemap lastmod, shared navigation, ≥3 inbound links per URL, owner resubmits the sitemap in GSC.
3. Refresh the highest-impression hotel lists with observed, dated evidence (NYC, San Diego, Chicago, Las Vegas).
4. Grow where clicks come from: improve the Las Vegas and San Antonio itineraries and add itinerary pages for existing destinations that lack them (Orlando, DC, Boston, Cancun) when the SERP check qualifies.
5. Ship finished work (Orlando admission form).

## Not now
Offline CLIs and output-isolation hardening, new micro-calculators, the source-governance pilot, outreach, community posting, analytics and monetization (revisit at the Nov 12 go/no-go).

## Differentiation (unchanged)
Current, dated, source-backed family decision support (room capacity, age rules, real costs, nap-friendly day shapes) presented as fast answers, not audit logs. Rigor stays in the data; pages lead with the answer and put provenance in a collapsed "How we checked" block.
